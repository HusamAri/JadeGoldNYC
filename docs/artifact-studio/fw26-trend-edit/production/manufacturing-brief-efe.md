## Tools and masters to make

- Wedge punch set (hardened steel, three heads: 1.6, 2.4 and 3.2 mm) and a sign card with TI, SHA, E and AN and their stroke order (L06).
- Two paw templates, dog and cat, so every intaglio stays consistent (L09).
- Masters: seal cylinder with 12 flutes (L01), eight-petal rosette (L02), pomegranate with crown (L05), infinity loop with the set-of-2 and set-of-3 wave cuts cut in wax (L08), almond and iris connectors (L10).
- Ur Band master per size, US 4 to 10 in half sizes (13 masters, or one CAD file scaled per size) (L04).
- Torch-textured master for the Thaw Hoops if reticulation yield on 10K is poor (L07).

## Alloy questions

1. Is your 10K yellow alloy safe for enamel firing at 780 to 820 C? If not, the Ur Band (L04) drops 10K.
2. Does enamel hold on your white and rose alloys? L05 Nar and L10 Twin Eye offer all three colors; if not, they go yellow only.
3. For the Thaw Hoops (L07) white gold tip: palladium white rather than nickel white, and no rhodium. Is that available?
4. Fibula (L03) in 18K: the note asks for 1.4 mm wire for spring tension. Please weigh the 18K sample; the price is built on 1.3 mm.
5. Processing time: L01, L06, L08, L09 and L10 are listed at 5 to 7 business days (engraving or sets), the rest at 3 to 5. Confirm both.

## Pieces

### L01 Seal Pin

Variations: Karat: 10K / 14K / 18K (price varies); Metal Color: Yellow Gold / White Gold / Rose Gold

Cylinder 14.0 x 3.5 mm, solid, cast in 14K from a hand-carved wax master with 12 longitudinal flutes 0.3 mm deep; flutes hand-finished with a fine needle file, satin inside the flutes and polished on the ridges. End cap flat, 3.5 mm, polished, engraved after casting (laser or graver, 1.2 mm serif capitals). Stick pin 0.9 mm hard 14K wire, 45 mm, soldered into a 1.0 mm blind hole at the cylinder's top end, tip rounded. Clutch: solid 14K pin protector (findings supplier), no plated parts. Hallmark on the end cap edge. Est. 14K weight 2.4 g (cylinder 1.75, pin 0.37, clutch 0.3). One master serves 10K, 14K and 18K.

### L02 Ishtar Rosette

Variations: Karat: 10K / 14K / 18K (price varies); Metal Color: Yellow Gold / White Gold / Rose Gold; Chain: None / 16 in / 18 in / 20 in (price varies)

Rosette 22 mm diameter, 1.2 mm sheet thickness equivalent, eight pointed petals with pierced (open) spaces between the inner ring and the petal tips, cast from a hand-carved wax master; openwork cleaned with a saw and needle files, edges crisp, back flat and polished. Center: 5 mm round garnet cabochon in a closed-back bezel cup (0.4 mm wall) soldered to the rosette, bezel burnished over the stone. Brooch fittings in solid 14K: pin stem 0.8 mm hard wire, joint (hinge) and revolving safety catch, soldered on the back at 2 and 10 o'clock so the piece hangs level. Hidden bail: 2.0 mm OD gold tube, 6 mm long, soldered horizontally behind the top petal, sized to pass a 1.2 mm cable chain. Est. 14K weight 3.2 g (rosette 2.6, fittings and bezel 0.6). Chain add-on: 1.2 mm cable 16 in, est. 1.4 g, spring ring clasp.

### L03 Fibula

Variations: Karat: 10K / 14K / 18K (price varies); Metal Color: Yellow Gold / White Gold / Rose Gold

One piece of 1.3 mm round 14K wire, approx. 120 mm, annealed. Form: pin shank 38 mm; two full coils (spring) of 4 mm ID at the head; bow forged flat with a planishing hammer to 2.5 x 0.8 mm over 30 mm, leaving visible facets; foot bent into a rolled catch (hook) 3 mm deep that receives the pin tip. Work-harden the pin and spring after forming (planish, then twist-harden; do not anneal again). Pin tip ground to a blunt point and polished so it does not snag fibers. Hallmark stamped on the inside of the bow. Est. 14K weight 2.9 g. Note for Efe: 18K is softer; use 1.4 mm wire for the 18K version to keep spring tension.

### L04 Ur Band

Variations: Karat: 10K / 14K / 18K (price varies); Ring Size: US 4 / US 4.5 / US 5 / US 5.5 / US 6 / US 6.5 / US 7 / US 7.5 / US 8 / US 8.5 / US 9 / US 9.5 / US 10; Palette: Ur / Festival

Band 3.5 mm wide x 1.4 mm thick, comfort-fit inner profile, cast per size from a master ring (CAD or hand-carved) with twelve rectangular recessed cells 2.3 x 1.4 mm, 0.4 mm deep, separated by 0.5 mm gold walls; cells have a slightly undercut floor to key the enamel. Champleve process: degrease, counter-enamel not required at this depth, wet-pack lead-free vitreous enamel (opaque lapis blue, opaque cranberry red, opaque white; Festival adds opaque emerald, saffron and pink), fire at 780 to 820 C, two to three fills per cell, stone flush with alundum/diamond stick under water, flash fire for gloss, final polish on the gold only. Ur sequence: blue, red, white, repeated four times. Hallmark inside. Est. 14K weight 2.9 g at US 7. Yellow gold only (alloy must tolerate enamel firing; confirm Efe's 10K yellow alloy is enamel-safe, otherwise offer the Ur Band in 14K and 18K only).

### L05 Nar (Pomegranate)

Variations: Karat: 10K / 14K / 18K (price varies); Metal Color: Yellow Gold / White Gold / Rose Gold; Chain Length: 16 inches / 18 inches / 20 inches (price varies)

Pomegranate body 12 x 10 mm, slightly flattened sphere, cast as an open-backed shell with 0.8 mm walls; six-point crown 2.5 mm tall cast integral; fixed bail loop 1.5 mm ID at the crown. Front: one recessed enamel cell following the body silhouette, 0.4 mm deep, with a 0.5 mm polished gold rim; a vertical split 5 x 1.8 mm left bare in the cell and drilled with three 1.3 mm seats for bead-set round garnets (or 1.3 mm cranberry-red garnets matched to the enamel). Enamel: opaque cranberry red, wet-packed, three fills, fired 780 to 820 C, stoned flush, flash fired; set the garnets after enameling. Chain 1.2 mm cable, spring ring. Est. 14K weight 3.0 g with a 16 in chain (pendant 1.5, chain 1.4, findings 0.1). For white and rose gold, confirm enamel adhesion on Efe's alloys; if not, offer yellow only.

### L06 Wedge

Variations: Karat: 10K / 14K / 18K (price varies); Chain Length: 16 inches / 18 inches / 20 inches (price varies); Sign: TI (life) / SHA (heart) / E (home) / AN (star, sky)

Bar 16 x 5 x 1.0 mm cut from rolled 14K sheet, corners radiused 0.6 mm, edges rounded, annealed before stamping. Punch set to be made (hardened steel, three wedge sizes: 1.6, 2.4 and 3.2 mm heads) matching the cuneiform stroke shapes; strike on a steel block with a 200 g hammer, one stroke per wedge, then flatten the back lightly. Signs (Sumerian, Unicode reference for the sign drawings): TI U+122FE, SHA (SA3) U+122AE, E (E2) U+1208D, AN U+1202D; prepare a sign card with the four signs and their stroke order for the bench and for photo 3. Back: engraved up to 8 characters in a plain sans serif, 1.3 mm cap height. Bail: 1.5 mm ID loop soldered at the top short edge so the bar hangs vertically (or two end loops for a horizontal bar if Husam prefers; decide before the master). Finish: fine satin brush, edges bright. Hallmark on the back. Est. 14K weight 2.55 g with 16 in chain (bar 1.05, bail 0.1, chain 1.4).

### L07 Thaw Hoops

Variations: Karat: 10K / 14K / 18K (price varies); Metal Color: Yellow/White Gold / Rose/White Gold

Pair of hoops, 15 mm outer diameter, 1.6 mm round wire (yellow or rose 14K), formed round, ends butted. Texture: reticulation by torch on a charcoal block, heating until the surface just flows, then quenched; repeat until the ripple reads at arm's length (reference the Thaw Cuff surface); alternatively cast from a torch-textured master if reticulation yield is poor on 10K. White gold tip: 3 mm cap of 14K white (palladium white preferred over nickel for skin), soldered to one end, polished bright; no rhodium. Post 0.8 mm hard wire soldered to the opposite end, 10 mm long, notched; solid 14K butterfly backs. Match left and right by eye for ripple density. Hallmark on the inside of the hoop near the post. Est. 14K weight 2.5 g per pair (hoops 2.2, posts and backs 0.3).

### L08 Split Infinity

Variations: Set: 2 pendants / 3 pendants (price varies); Karat: 10K / 14K / 18K (price varies); Metal Color: Yellow Gold / White Gold / Rose Gold

Master: infinity loop 16 x 8 mm in 1.3 mm round section, flattened slightly on the back (D profile) for comfort and engraving. Two cutting patterns on the same master: Set of 2 (one wave cut through the crossing, giving a left and right piece) and Set of 3 (two wave cuts giving left lobe, center crossing, right lobe). Cast each piece separately from piece-specific masters (do not cut finished castings; cut the wax, then cast, so the break faces are clean). Each piece: 1.5 mm ID bail soldered at its top point, back engraved with one initial 1.5 mm cap height. Chains 1.2 mm cable, 18 in standard, spring ring. Packaging: one box per pendant. Est. 14K weights: set of 2 approx. 4.0 g (pieces 1.2, chains 2.8); set of 3 approx. 5.9 g (pieces 1.5, chains 4.2 plus bails). Note: center piece of the set of 3 is the smallest; make sure it keeps enough face for the initial.
