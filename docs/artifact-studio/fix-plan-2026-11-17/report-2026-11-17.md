## 2. Verdict

The listings are well built. The shop fails for five reasons, in this order of weight:

1. Prices do not follow the product, and discounts of up to 25% still convert nobody.
2. Buyers cannot verify what they are buying: no reviews, no weights, no real photo on request.
3. The only order ever placed never moved in the mail and was fully refunded.
4. All ad money goes to the cheapest six items and has produced nothing.
5. A set of concrete errors in listings, messages and settings.
