## Brief

30 Christmas listings for by Artifact Studio Jewelry: 10 rings, 10 bracelets,
10 necklaces, each with 10 Etsy sales images. Same language as FW 26/27 and
SS27: solid 10K/14K/18K gold in yellow, white and rose, kiln-fired enamel set
flush in gold cells, made to order.

## What the season asks for (research 2026-10-06)

- Etsy's Christmas charm searches are led by trees, candy canes, bells,
  ornaments, snowflakes, stockings and gingerbread; reindeer, Santa and
  snowmen follow but read as costume jewelry at 6 to 10 mm, so they stay out.
- Colourful enamel and resin are the bright note of holiday dressing; the shop
  already sells fine gold, so enamel in gold cells is the difference.
- Buyers shop Christmas from early November. Made-to-order listings need to be
  live by the end of October.

## Production rules

- Enamel cells at least 1.5 mm wide, gold walls at least 0.4 mm, at most two
  enamel colours per piece, every cell framed by a polished gold rim.
- Solid gold pieces are sculpted with a closed back, no hollow.
- Ring: US 3 to 16 whole and half (27 sizes). Necklace: 1.2 mm cable chain,
  16, 18 or 20 in. Bracelet: 1.1 mm cable chain, 6.5, 7 or 7.5 in. Full grid:
  Karat x Metal Color x size, nothing skipped (243 ring variants, 27 others).

## Pricing (provisional)

FW 26/27 rule: price = ceil(2 x (grams_k x spot/31.1034768 x purity x 1.07 +
category labor) / 10) x 10, labor from the owner's 2027 enamel quotes (ring,
necklace, bracelet). Spot kept at the FW basis 4,178.20 USD/ozt; live spot on
2026-10-06 was 4,169.50 (-0.2%, inside the deadband). Grams are geometry
estimates; the workshop has not quoted these designs.
