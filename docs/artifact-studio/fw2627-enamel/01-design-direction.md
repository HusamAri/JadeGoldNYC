# Artifact Studio · FW 26/27 Enamel · 01 Design direction

Status: proposal for owner approval (2026-09-30). Nothing generated, nothing in the panel yet.

## Brief (owner, 2026-09-30)

Producible, colored-enamel, minimalist rings, necklaces, earrings and bracelets.
Pieces coordinate with each other but are not copies of one another. Fall/Winter
2026-27 direction. 10 models per category (40 total), each listing-ready with one
Higgsfield hero image in the Artifact Studio visual style, saved in the panel as
listing proposals (drafts only, no Etsy).

Reference screenshots (a Turkish retailer's enamel animal pieces) are used only
for technique and scale: a thin round shank or fine cable chain, one flat enamel
field framed by a polished gold rim, small wearable size. No motif, animal or
silhouette from the references is reused.

## Season signal (what the direction rests on)

- WGSN x Coloro A/W 26/27 key colours: Transformative Teal (2026 Colour of the
  Year), Wax Paper, Fresh Purple, Cocoa Powder, Green Glow.
- Fall 2026 jewelry coverage: black enamel for contrast, enamel blocks set beside
  each other like mosaic, deep jewel tones (mahogany, chocolate, ocean blue, berry,
  muted green, royal purple) replacing summer pastels; heavy yellow gold and
  sculptural forms at the statement end.
- What we take: the colour list, black enamel as a graphic accent, two-colour
  blocking. What we leave: oversized statement scale, which works against a
  low-labor, low-gram enamel line.

## How this set differs from what already exists

- The 2027 Color & Enamel set (20 proposals: seed, orbit, fold, arc, comma, offset,
  tide, interval, marker, horizon, link, turn, dawn, passage, petal) is spring
  pastel and abstract. This set is winter, darker, and object-based.
- None of the 10 motifs appears in a current by Artifact Studio title (checked
  in the panel; "crescent/moon" already exists, so it was dropped).

## Structure: 10 families x 4 categories

the four pieces change form and scale (for example a stud, a drop, a station
chain, a signet top), so they coordinate without repeating.

Category spread inside the 40 (fixed in step 2):
- Rings: signet tops, a thin stacker, an open ring, a small solitaire-style top.
- Necklaces: single pendant, drop on a bail, station chain, a lariat or Y drop.
- Earrings: studs, a mismatched pair, huggie hoops with an enamel charm, a short drop.
- Bracelets: fine cable chain with one station, three stations, a charm, one slim cuff.
- Scale: petite (5 to 7 mm), everyday (8 to 11 mm), one bolder piece per family
  (12 to 16 mm).

## Production rules (why it can be made)

- Technique: champlevé. Kiln-fired vitreous enamel sits in cells recessed about
  0.4 mm into solid gold; every cell has a polished gold rim.
- At most two enamel colours per piece; each enamel cell at least 1.5 mm wide;
  gold walls between cells at least 0.4 mm.
- No enamel on surfaces that rub (ring shank, clasp, earring post), no hollow
  backs, no gradients, no painted detail. Gold pips, stems and caps are metal, not
  enamel.
- Chains: 14K cable, 1.1 to 1.2 mm, spring ring or lobster clasp. Earrings: posts
  with butterfly backs; huggies with hinged closure.
- Metal: the shop rule is a full 3-axis grid (Karat 10K/14K/18K x Metal Color
  Yellow/White/Rose x size). Enamel colour is fixed per model, not a variation.
  Pricing and the grid are step 3.

## Visual style for the hero images (step 5)

Artifact Studio style from the current shop: warm off-white or linen ground, soft
directional daylight, shallow depth of field, the piece at true scale, gold with
real reflections, enamel flat and glossy (not domed, not cabochon), one image per
model, 2048 x 2048.
