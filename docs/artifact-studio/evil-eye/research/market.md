# Evil eye (nazar) collection: market research brief (2026-10-09)

Scope: solid 10K/14K/18K gold evil eye rings, necklaces and bracelets, enamel and two-tone, for by Artifact Studio Jewelry. I did web research only (WebSearch standard and extended, WebFetch). Etsy blocks direct fetches (HTTP 403), so the Etsy prices below come from search-engine snippets. Their index dates are unknown, so treat them as snapshots. Keyword volumes come from RankHero, an Etsy keyword tool. Its round figures look like Google Ads buckets, so they show direction only and are not Etsy's own counts.

## 2. Colour conventions

- **Classic nazar** (Turkish *nazar boncuğu*, Greek *máti*): handmade glass in "concentric circles or teardrop shapes in dark blue, white, light blue and black, occasionally with a yellow/gold edge" [W1][C1]. In Turkish belief, the blue shields against evil [W1][C1]. The Greek mati is described as visually the same as the Turkish nazar [G1]. A gold bezel therefore counts as authentic, because it is the traditional "gold edge".
- **Authentic vs generic:** the eye reads as nazar when the rings run in order (cobalt outer, white, light blue, dark pupil) and cobalt dominates. Round and teardrop shapes are traditional [W1]. Almond/marquise shapes are the modern fine form [M1]. A realistic iris, eyelashes, or an almond eye with a lid line reads as a generic "eye" or as the Eye of Horus, a different symbol.
- **Trending variants:** sellers attach popular meanings to other colours. These come from seller blogs, not from tradition: red = courage, pink = love and friendship, green = luck and health, purple = intuition, black = protection, turquoise = calm [V1][V2]. Etsy demand: "red evil eye" 2.9k searches and "pink evil eye" 1.9k searches a month (pink peaked in August 2026) [K12][K13]. Turquoise and emerald-green eyes also appear in solid gold [E7][M2].
- **Recommendation (inference):** make classic cobalt the default palette. Offer one or two colour variants as their own designs, each keeping the concentric structure. A colour swap alone does not justify a listing.

## 3. Two-tone gold in 2026

- **Trend:** retailer and jeweller trend guides for 2026 call mixed metals standard. The most common pairing is yellow, white and rose gold, with two-tone stacking rings and necklaces highlighted. Their styling advice: at most two tones, one lead metal, and one finish (polished with polished) [T1][T2][T3]. These are commercial sources, not sales data.
- **Search terms:** buyers search "mixed metal ring" (6.6k searches, 10,205 listings, the easiest keyword at difficulty 32), "two tone ring" (4.4k searches, 39,986 listings, peak 6.6k in December) and "two tone necklace" (2.4k searches, peaks in November and December) [K10][K11][K14]. Tags should carry both "two tone" and "mixed metal".
- **Whitespace:** a search for a two-tone 14K evil eye piece found no current product. The only hit was a vintage Buccellati two-tone gold evil eye necklace on 1stdibs [D4]. Two-tone evil eye in solid gold is therefore an open niche, not a crowded one.
- **Fit (inference):** two-tone suits the nazar structurally. White gold can play the nazar's white ring or the sclera, and yellow or rose gold the "gold edge" frame [W1]. Pairings: yellow + white (classic), rose + white (soft).
- **Implications:** (a) to keep the 3-axis full grid, a two-tone listing's Metal Color axis takes 3 two-tone values, for example Yellow & White, Rose & White and Yellow & Rose (3 x 3 x 27 = 243 ring variants, under 400). (b) Ask the maker whether white gold next to enamel is rhodium-plated after firing, or left unplated (slightly warm).

## 6. What to avoid

- **Brand and product names:** never use designer names (Sydney Evan, Mateo, Alison Lou, Jennifer Meyer, Suzanne Kalan, Buccellati) in titles or tags. Do not borrow product names such as "Eye of Protection" (Mateo) [M1].
- **Registered marks:** "POP Power of Protection" is a registered stylized evil eye mark, so avoid the phrase "power of protection" [TM1].
- **Generic terms:** "Evil eye" and "nazar" are not ownable. The USPTO required a disclaimer of "EVIL EYE", and the NAZAR registration was cancelled in 2005 [TM2][TM3].
- **Signature designs:** do not replicate distinctive designs such as Sydney Evan's linked-eye eternity band or Kalan's baguette eye [SE4][N1]. Jewellery design copyright is actively enforced: David Yurman v. TJX, February 2026 [L1].
- **Etsy policy:** Etsy bans "items that advertise a metaphysical outcome" and medical claims, including implied claims in tags and images [EP1][EP2]. Describe the nazar as a traditional symbol, not a promise ("wards off", "protects you", "healing"). "Protection necklace" is a weak tag anyway (1.9k searches against 97.5k listings) [K15].
- **FTC (US):** hollow items must not be called "solid" or "solid gold" (16 CFR 23.3) [F1]. Keep backs closed. Stones, if ever used, need an accurate natural or lab-grown disclosure.
- **Cultural:**
  - The hamsa is sacred: the Hand of Miriam in Judaism, the Hand of Fatima in Islam. Avoid Hebrew or Arabic inscriptions, the Star of David and Quranic text, and "yoga/boho" framing [H1][H2]. If a hamsa is used at all, keep it small and neutral, or leave it out.
  - Do not mix in crosses (the Greek religious variant) [G1].
  - Do not conflate the nazar with the Eye of Horus.
  - Write "Turkish/Greek-style nazar", not "Made in Turkey", unless that is true.
  - Do not call a lashed or realistic eye a "nazar".

## Implications for the design direction (inference)

1. Lead with the classic cobalt concentric eye in a polished gold bezel (5-9 mm, flush enamel, closed back). This reads as authentic and fine.
2. Make two-tone the differentiator: white gold as the white ring, yellow or rose as the frame. It is whitespace on Etsy, and buyers search it as "two tone" and "mixed metal".
3. Forms by category:
   - Rings: single-eye solitaire band, eye band, signet.
   - Necklaces: round, teardrop or marquise pendants; eye station chain.
   - Bracelets: stations. This is the highest-demand category at 135k searches.
4. At about $560-1,000 we sit above the Etsy mass market. Finish quality, two-tone and clean enamel must justify a near-designer price. Lighter gram designs widen the audience.
5. Use the colour variants (red, pink, green, turquoise) sparingly, as separate designs that keep the nazar structure.
