## Brief

30 evil eye listings for by Artifact Studio Jewelry: 10 rings, 10 necklaces, 10
bracelets that, in the owner's words, "can be enamelled, can be two-colour gold". Same
language as FW 26/27, SS27 and Christmas 26: solid 10K/14K/18K gold, kiln-fired
champlevé enamel set flush in polished gold cells, made to order, and the full
Karat x Metal Color x size grid.

The ten families come from three independent proposals (A Heritage nazar, B Modern
minimal, C Gift and Stack), judged as a portfolio and then stress-tested by three
reviews: manufacturing and price, market and compliance, image generation. Every
accepted correction is in the body. The review points not applied are listed at the end
with the reason.

## What the market asks for (research 2026-10-09)

- **Titles say "evil eye", not "nazar".** Monthly searches: evil eye bracelet 135,000,
  evil eye necklace 40,500, evil eye pendant 18,100, evil eye ring 14,800, against 880
  for nazar necklace ([bracelet](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/evil-eye-bracelet),
  [necklace](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/evil-eye-necklace),
  [nazar necklace](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/nazar-necklace)). Solid gold is
  the thin end: "14k gold evil eye necklace" has 640 competing listings against 67,723
  for "evil eye necklace" ([RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/14k-gold-evil-eye-necklace)).
- **What reads fine:** flat flush enamel, a polished gold rim and a closed back, about
  5 to 9 mm per eye. Sydney Evan's medium enamel charm is about 6.4 x 9.5 mm at $320
  ([Sydney Evan](https://www.sydneyevan.com/products/gold-enamel-medium-evil-eye-charm)).
  Costume signals: 12 mm and up, domed glass beads, resin, lashes, red string, open
  adjustable rings, CZ halos.
- **What makes it a nazar:** concentric rings, dark blue outside, then white and light
  blue, a dark pupil, sometimes a gold edge. Round and teardrop are traditional; the
  almond is the modern fine form. Lashes, a lid line or a realistic iris read as a
  generic eye or the Eye of Horus ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazar_(amulet)),
  [KU CREES](https://crees.ku.edu/nazar)).
- **Colour variants are a minority taste:** red evil eye 2,900 and pink evil eye 1,900
  searches a month. Keep the concentric structure and use them sparingly
  ([red](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/red-evil-eye),
  [pink](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/pink-evil-eye)).
- **Two-tone is open ground:** "mixed metal ring" 6,600 searches at difficulty 32, the
  easiest term of the set; "two tone ring" 4,400 with a December peak. A web search
  found no current solid two-tone evil eye piece, only a vintage Buccellati. That is
  consistent with whitespace, not proof
  ([mixed metal ring](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/mixed-metal-ring),
  [1stdibs](https://www.1stdibs.com/buy/vintage-evil-eye-necklace/)).
- **Price band:** Etsy solid 14K evil eye rings about $120 to $600, necklaces $85 to
  $215, bracelets $135 to $400; designers $320 to $1,500 and up
  ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/market/14k_solid_evil_eye_bracelet),
  [Sydney Evan](https://www.sydneyevan.com/products/pure-gold-evil-eye-link-eternity-ring)).
  At 2 x maker cost this set lands 2 to 4 times the Etsy median and at or under the
  designer tier, so it has to look designer-made.
- **Gift calendar:** "evil eye gift" peaks in December, May and August; "evil eye
  jewelry" peaks in February; the bracelet term peaks in August. New mother, new home
  and milestones are the traditional moments
  ([gift](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/evil-eye-gift),
  [jewelry](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/evil-eye-jewelry)).
- **What to avoid:** metaphysical promises (Etsy bars items that "advertise a
  metaphysical outcome"), the registered phrase "power of protection", designer names

## Collection idea: one nazar grammar, ten forms

Every eye follows one grammar: concentric cells, dark outside and light inside, a
polished gold rim (the nazar's traditional "gold edge") and a gold or cobalt pupil. No
lashes, lid lines, realistic irises, hamsas, crosses, stones or domed glass. The
families change the form and the construction; seven of them stay cobalt-led.

Six families are single-metal enamel and four are two-tone (three with enamel, one
metal only): 9 of 10 families carry enamel, 12 of 30 pieces are two-tone. Red (9) and
pink (10) are the only colour variants, and each keeps the nazar structure.

## The ten families

Rules for every piece unless a family says otherwise: champlevé enamel recessed 0.4 mm,
flat, flush and glossy, every cell in a polished gold rim; closed backs, nothing hollow;
rings US 3 to 16, whole and half; necklaces on 1.2 mm solid cable, 16/18/20 in, spring
ring; bracelets on 1.1 mm solid cable, 6.5/7/7.5 in, spring ring; on two-tone pieces the
chain is always in the body gold. All dimensions in mm.

Enamel palette, fixed per model and never a variation (hex for prompts, plain names in
copy):

- **Nazar cobalt #17368C** (1 to 7): "cobalt blue enamel" in copy, never "cobalt" alone,
  which is also a ring metal. Deeper than the shop's Luminous Blue #1F4FD1, brighter
  than FW navy #1D2F5C.
- **Porcelain white #F3F1EA** (2, 3, 10): "white enamel" in copy.
- **Sky blue #8CC3E8** (1, 6): pale, clearly not Luminous Blue.
- **Tile turquoise #23A5B2** (5): brighter and greener than FW Transformative Teal
  #0F5C63; "turquoise enamel" in copy, never "turquoise" alone.
- **Poppy red #D7352B** (9): warmer than Christmas poinsettia #C21F2E and cranberry
  #9E1B32.
- **Petal pink #EC9AB4** (10): ΔE76 41.7 from porcelain white, 26.1 from rose gold,
  26.4 from SS27 Pop Pink #F06AA8 and 39.9 from FW berry #A3265B. The earlier blush
  #F2B6C3 sat at 28.7 from white and 14.3 from rose gold and read as a pale disc.

### 1. Halka · two-tone, enamel (cobalt + sky)

The nazar's white ring is white gold. Gift moment: the classic, and the December "evil
eye gift" piece once the maker quotes two-tone.

- **Large eye (N01), 11.5:** visible from the edge: body collar 0.8 · cobalt 1.6 ·
  accent ring 0.9 · sky 1.6 · accent pupil 1.7. The accent plate is 10.7 across; its
  own 0.4 rim sits wholly under the bezel (0.4 wall, lip burnished 0.4 over the rim), so
  no accent hairline shows outside the cobalt. Five visible zones, two enamels.
- **Small eye (R01, B01), 8.4:** body collar 0.8 · cobalt 1.5 · accent ring 0.9 ·
  cobalt centre 2.0. Plate 7.6 with the same hidden 0.4 rim. One enamel, four zones.
- **Read in Yellow/White:** yellow collar, cobalt, a pale white-gold ring, sky, a
  white-gold pupil. In White/Yellow the ring and pupil turn yellow.
- **Construction:** the plate is one alloy, fired alone, then set like a stone in a
  cup cast in the body gold with a closed 0.5 back. Nothing is soldered or fired after
  enamelling.
- **R01:** cup 1.8 high on a 2.0 x 1.4 D-shank. **N01:** 1.5 thick; tube bail behind
  the upper edge, bore 1.4, outside 2.0, chain threaded before the clasp. **B01:**
  inline, integral loops on the cup at 3 and 9 o'clock.
- **Split:** body = cup, bezel, shank, bail, loops, chain. Accent = eye plate.

### 2. Damla · enamel (cobalt + white)

The teardrop nazar hung over doors and cradles, point up. Gift moment: everyday, new
home.

- **Two-colour drop (N02, B02), 8.8 x 10.6, 1.2 thick:** a circle of radius 4.4 with two
  tangents meeting at 90 degrees. From the edge: rim 0.4 · cobalt 1.5 · wall 0.4 ·
  white 1.5 · gold pupil 1.2 at the circle's centre. The bands keep full width along the
  straight sides; the last 1.5 of the point is solid polished gold.
- **One-colour drop (R02), 6.0 x 7.2, 1.1 thick:** rim 0.4 · cobalt 1.5 · gold pupil
  2.2. The last 1.5 of the point is solid here too, so the cobalt stops 1.88 wide
  instead of running into a knife edge.
- **R02:** pear-set, point toward the nail, low on a 1.6 round band, cast in one piece.
  **N02:** integral loop at the point, jump ring closed by laser weld. **B02:** hangs
  free from a jump ring at the centre of the cable, the set's only dangle.

### 3. Mati · enamel (white + cobalt)

The almond eye with a white sclera and a ringed cobalt iris. Gift moment: bride and
bridesmaids, Greek-heritage gifting.

- **Almond 11.0 x 6.6, 1.2 thick:** two arcs of radius 6.23, rim 0.4.
- **Iris:** a 5.0 round island at the centre: gold pupil 1.2 · cobalt annulus 1.5 · gold
  wall 0.4, with 0.8 of gold above and below it to the rim.
- **Sclera:** white cells either side from 2.9 to 4.5 off centre: 1.6 long, 4.26 tall at
  the inner end and 1.56 at the outer end, with the rim measured normal to the edge.
  Solid gold tips from 4.5 to 5.5. The almond does not go smaller: at 10 x 6 the sclera
  breaks the 1.5 rule.
- **R03:** a rigid, sized 1.5 round band whose ends pass on top: one ends in the almond,
  long axis along the band; the other in a plain polished tapered end that stops 4 mm
  short of the almond.
- **N03:** Y lariat: a 3.0 solid bead at the Y, then a 40 drop ending in the almond,
  which hangs level from a hidden integral loop behind the middle of its top edge;
  16/18/20 in measured to the Y.
- **B03:** almond east-west; the cable runs through two hidden integral loops behind the
  upper edge, about 2.5 in from each tip. The chain lies along the top, and both tips
  hang free below it.
- No lid line, no lashes, never an aperture.

### 4. Bead String · enamel (cobalt)

The blue beads and gold spacers of a Turkish bead string, rebuilt in solid gold without
glass or cord. Gift moment: first nazar, self-gift, summer and travel.

- **Eye 6.0:** rim 0.5 · cobalt 1.7 · gold pupil 1.6.
- **Station unit:** one cast bar 10 long: a 2.0 solid bead · the 6.0 eye · a 2.0 solid
  bead, with integral end loops. The chain is cut and joined at both ends of each unit
  by laser weld (6 joins per piece). A bead threaded on the cable would need a 1.4 bore
  and become a tube, not a solid bead.
- **R04:** a rigid closed band of fused 1.8 solid beads (about 28 at US 3, 30 at US 7,
  46 at US 16) with the eye flat on top on a 0.6 gallery, cast in one piece. One
  parametric model: the bead count is rounded and the diameter trimmed by about 0.05 so
  the band closes evenly. Copy never states a count.
- **N04:** three units fixed 22 apart at the front. **B04:** three units fixed 18 apart.
- Bead · eye · bead units on cable only, never a full bead strand.

### 5. Çini · enamel (cobalt + turquoise)

The first two glazes of Ottoman tilework, cobalt and turquoise; turquoise is also a
traditional nazar colour. Gift moment: travel, Turkish heritage.

- **Tile:** a hexagon with a 0.4 rim. **Iris:** a 5.2 island: wall 0.4 · turquoise
  annulus 1.5 · gold pupil 1.4. **Field:** cobalt out to the rim, 1.6 wide at the flats
  on the 9.2 tile and 2.0 on the 10.0 tile, so the order stays dark outside, light
  inside.
- **R05:** 9.2 across the flats, 1.3 thick, lying flat on a 2.0 x 1.4 D-shank.
  **N05:** 10.0 across the flats, 1.2 thick, hung from a corner by an integral loop.
  **B05:** the 9.2 eye tile between two 5.0 one-cell turquoise tiles (cell 4.2, plain,
  no dot), integral loops, fixed 5 apart.

### 6. Mother and Child · two-tone, enamel (cobalt + sky)

Two eyes, two golds. Gift moment: new mom, push present, Mother's Day; every piece is
presented as a gift for the parent.

- **Mother eye 9.0, body gold:** rim 0.4 · cobalt 1.5 · wall 0.4 · sky 1.5 · gold
  pupil 1.4.
- **Child eye 6.0, accent gold:** rim 0.5 · cobalt 1.5 · accent pupil 2.0, one enamel.
  It sits in a 6.8 cup in the body gold (bezel 0.4), cast with the mother rim to rim,
  with at least 0.8 of gold at the joint. The accent shows as the pupil and a thin rim
  inside the body collar.
- **Construction:** each eye is fired alone; the child plate is set after firing.
- **R06:** toi-et-moi, the child at the mother's 4 to 5 o'clock, about 13.5 x 13.5
  overall, on a 1.8 round band. **N06:** the child directly below the mother at 6
  o'clock, 9.0 x 15.8 overall; tube bail behind the mother at 12 o'clock (bore 1.4,
  outside 2.0), on the vertical through the centre of mass, so the pendant hangs as
  drawn. **B06:** mother and child as two stations with integral loops in their own
  gold, joined at the centre of the cable by one 0.7 body-gold jump ring, laser-welded.
- **Split:** body = mother eye, cup, band, bail, jump ring, chain. Accent = child plate.

### 7. Medal · two-tone, enamel (cobalt)

The gold coin given at milestones, made as a two-gold heirloom. Gift moment: graduation,
new job, anniversaries; unisex.

- **Coin, body gold:** satin field, polished 0.6 border, reeded edge on the necklace and
  bracelet.
- **Almond, accent gold:** polished, 0.9 thick, a cobalt iris cell in a 0.4 rim, two
  0.6 pins cast integral with it, so no solder seam enters the kiln.
- **Join:** the almond is fired alone; the pins pass through the coin and are riveted
  flush at the back. It is a cold join; no heat reaches the enamel.
- **R07, coin ring:** a 9.0 x 1.3 disc with a polished bevel (no reeding on a ring edge)
  on a 2.0 x 1.4 D band; almond 7.0 x 3.6, iris cell 1.6 (2.4 with the rim, 0.6
  margin), long axis across the finger. The band lies under the pins, so pins about 3.2
  long pass through the disc and the band head and are riveted flush inside the band,
  or are laser-welded blind pins, as the maker prefers.
- **N07:** coin 11.0 x 1.0; almond 8.0 x 4.0, iris cell 2.0 (2.8 with the rim);
  integral top loop and jump ring. **B07:** coin 8.5 x 0.9; almond 6.4 x 3.2, iris cell
  1.5 (2.3 with the rim, 0.45 margin), inside the 0.6 border; one centre station with
  integral loops at 3 and 9 o'clock.
- Single-sided, no rays, no pavé, no lettering.
- **Split:** body = coin or disc, band, loops, chain. Accent = almond.

### 8. Twin Wire · two-tone, no enamel

The two-wire mixed-metal band is the identity; the metal nazar is the detail. Gift
moment: stacking, buyers who want no colour, "mixed metal ring".

- **Eye 6.0:** body rim 0.5 · accent ring 1.5, inlaid flush 0.5 deep · body pupil 2.0,
  domed 0.3. The body disc is cast with a channel; an accent washer is hard-soldered in
  and filed flush. There is no kiln, so this family sits outside the enamel firing
  qualification.
- **R08:** two 1.3 round wires soldered side by side (body toward the fingertip, accent
  toward the hand), 2.6 wide; the eye (6.0 x 1.2) flat across both on a 0.6 gallery.
- **N08:** one 6.0 x 1.0 eye inline at the centre front, integral body-gold loops at 3
  and 9 o'clock, cable on both sides: the set's only inline necklace.
- **B08:** an open cuff of two 1.0 round wires (body and accent) soldered side by side,
  2.0 wide, with the eye across both at the front as on R08. Sized 6.5/7/7.5 in inner
  circumference, like the live Crush cuff.
- **Split:** body = one wire, eye rim, pupil, chain. Accent = the other wire and the
  inlaid ring.

### 9. Sweetheart · enamel (poppy red)

A polished gold heart with a red evil eye set into it. Gift moment: Valentine's,
anniversary.

- **Heart:** solid polished gold, full round lobes and straight sides to an 81-degree
  point; 9.6 x 9.2 x 1.1 (ring, bracelet) and 11.0 x 10.5 x 1.2 (necklace).
- **Eye:** one colour, inset flush at the centre of the heart's largest inscribed circle,
  about 4.35 and 4.95 below the top line. Small heart: a 5.4 eye, rim 0.4 · red 1.5 ·
  gold pupil 1.6, with at least 0.45 of gold to the heart's edge. Large heart: a 6.0
  eye, rim 0.5 · red 1.7 · gold pupil 1.6, at least 0.6.
- **Why this form:** a red heart field around a ringed white iris needs a heart of about
  12.7 x 12.2 before the red reaches 1.5 everywhere; at 9.6 and 11.0 it fell to 0.1 to
  0.8 on the lower flanks. A gold heart with an inset eye keeps the Valentine story,
  is buildable, and stays clear of the shop's enamel-filled Crush hearts.
- **R09:** the 9.6 heart upright, point toward the wrist, low on a 1.5 round band, cast
  in one piece. **N09:** the 11.0 heart on a tube bail behind the cleft, bore 1.4,
  outside 2.0. **B09:** the 9.6 heart as a centre station with integral loops at the two
  lobes.

### 10. Paperclip · enamel (petal pink + white)

The eye as a link in the chain rather than a hanging charm. Gift moment: best friend,
birthday.

- **Connector 9.0:** rim 0.4 · pink 1.5 · wall 0.4 · white 1.5 · gold pupil 1.4;
  integral loops at 3 and 9 o'clock.
- **Links:** solid paperclip links 7.0 x 2.4 in 0.8 wire, two on each side of the eye,
  closed around the fired connector by laser weld. The rest is the quoted cable, so the
  piece stays on the shop's chain rule.
- **R10:** a rigid band of soldered paperclip links, sized like a band (one parametric
  model; the link count follows the size), with a 6.0 one-colour pink eye (rim 0.5 ·
  pink 1.7 · pupil 1.6) fired alone and laser-welded on top; closed eye back.
- **N10:** the connector and its links at the front. **B10:** the same at the centre of
  the cable.

### Copy rules

- The nazar is "a traditional Turkish and Greek symbol" and the style is
  "Turkish/Greek-style". Never "Made in Turkey".
- A generator assert bans these in titles, tags and descriptions: `protect`, `ward`,
  `luck`, `power of`, `healing` (Etsy bars metaphysical outcomes; "power of protection"
  is a registered mark), and `baby`, `newborn`, `baby shower`, `christening`, `kids`. A
  piece presented as for a child of 12 or under falls under CPSIA third-party testing,
  and vitreous enamel has no exemption. Gifts are for the wearer or the parent.
- Never designer names, hamsa, crosses, the Eye of Horus or "Something Blue" (a US
  Class 14 mark is listed for it). "Iznik" appears only in the Çini description
  ("inspired by Ottoman tilework from Iznik", a registered Turkish geographical
  indication for tiles), never in a title or tag.
- Every name carries "evil eye"; "nazar" goes to the descriptor and the tags. Hanging
  pendants (N01, N02, N05, N06, N07, N09) carry "Evil Eye Pendant" in the descriptor.
- Two-tone titles carry "two tone"; their tags carry "two tone" and "mixed metal". The
  panel's colour facet reads "two tone" from the title.
- Colour words: "cobalt blue enamel", "white enamel", "sky blue enamel", "turquoise
  enamel", "red enamel", "pink enamel". White gold is "white gold, not rhodium plated"
  unless maker question 2 says otherwise.

| No mixed alloy in the kiln (new for this set) | Each enamelled part is one alloy, fired alone, then joined by setting (1, 6), cast-pin rivets (7) or laser weld. Twin Wire is never fired. No mixed-alloy firing has to be qualified; single-alloy firing on white and rose gold is maker question 2 |
| Motif size 6 to 12 | Motifs 6.0 to 11.5. The Mother and Child pair is 13.5 (ring) and 15.8 (necklace) overall, each eye in range; R09's inset eye is 5.4 inside a 9.6 heart |
| Counts | Any count the copy promises or the eye reads at a glance is three or fewer per group |

- **A. Findings on enamelled parts:** every loop, pin and bail on an enamelled part is
  cast integral. Every closure made after firing (jump rings, chain joins, paperclip
  links, the R10 eye) is laser-welded. No torch solder goes near fired enamel, and any
  seam that enters the kiln is enamelling-grade solder.
- **B. White gold:** palladium white, unplated, across the whole collection. Rhodium
  burns off in the kiln and plating after firing risks the enamel, so enamelled white
  parts cannot be plated; leaving every white part unplated makes the three variants,
  their photos and the copy match. Natural white gold is a soft warm grey; at 10K it is
  close to pale yellow beside yellow gold, where the two-tone read is weakest.
- **C. Hard minimums:** the maker is told that cells drawn at exactly 1.5 have no
  negative tolerance.

## Pricing (provisional)

- **Rule:** price = ceil(2 x maker cost / 10) x 10 (`docs/artifact-studio/maker_cost.py`,
  owner 2026-10-07). 14K = 100 USD/g with labour; spot basis 4,178.20 USD/ozt; 10K and
  18K through `gold_cost` (gold and chain x 0.691 and x 1.389; enamel, assembly and
  two-tone items stay fixed).
- **Rings:** a 3 mm band from the maker's list, the maker's rule for motif rings: 14K
  US 7 = 3.59 g, price 720. Single-metal range 420 (10K US 3) to 1,390 (18K US 16).
  Every ring here weighs 1.2 to 3.2 g, under the band, so the rule overcharges the light
  ones (R04, R10) and is honest for R07 now that it is a coin-top band.
- **Necklaces:** grams x 100 + 50 enamel + 130 chain (18 in, the maker's middle-length
  convention). Grams count at least 1 g per motif unit (a pendant or a chain station),
  the maker's own count on Christmas B09. This raises N02, N03 and N08 and prices the
  same part alike on a necklace and a bracelet. Pieces whose parts sit in the chain
  (N04, N08, N10) add 50 assembly.
- **Bracelets:** grams + 50 enamel + 100 chain + 50 assembly, on one of two gram bases
  (decision 2): **(a)** the same 1 g per unit floor, or **(b)** the approved Christmas
  rule, geometry x 3.75 (the B09 ratio). The B08 cuff is grams + 50 assembly with no
  chain, the same under both. Under (a), if the maker's real cost follows the Christmas
  reading, a 600 single-charm bracelet loses 120 per sale on a 30% offer.
- **Two-tone:** not quoted by this maker. Provisional 50 USD maker cost per two-tone
  operation (`UNCALIBRATED_ASSUMPTION`), counted per operation, not per piece: an accent
  part set, riveted or inlaid (1, 6, 7, N08) is 1; R08 and B08 (a wire seam plus an
  inlay) are 2. Each 50 USD of maker surcharge is +100 retail. The repo's only figures
  are other makers' (Tamsan +20 per piece, EON +195). At EON's +195 every one-operation
  piece loses 25 to 57 per sale on a 30% offer after about 9.5% Etsy fees, so
  "TWO-TONE UNQUOTED" stays a blocker and no two-tone piece goes live before the quote.
  Never run the EON pricing engine on this org: it maps "two tone" titles to its own
  $250 profile.
- **Margin check:** at 2 x maker cost a 30% offer leaves about 13% of the price after
  fees (0.7 x 0.905 = 0.634 of the price against a cost of at most 0.5).
- **Repo fix before the catalog:** `maker_cost.price_cents` ceils float noise
  (`gold_cost(1.6 + 1.3, "14K") + 50` is 340.00000000000006, which prints N07's base
  price as 690 instead of 680). Use `math.ceil(round(MARKUP * cost_usd, 6) / 10)`; it
  moves no Christmas or For Him price. The generator takes one gram value per piece and
  prints the price that value produces.
- **Model fee:** 25 USD x 30 = 750 USD one-off, not in unit prices; up to 300 more if
  each two-tone accent part counts as its own model.
- **Flags written at generation:** `pricing.status = ESTIMATE_MAKER_V2`;
  `weightSource = geometry_estimate`; blockers "ESTIMATED PRICE", "grams are geometry
  estimates", "TWO-TONE UNQUOTED" (families 1, 6, 7, 8), "alloy/enamel firing

## Image notes

Each listing gets one reference hero, then 10 sales frames held to it (nano_banana_2 at
2k, count 1, every prompt shown to the owner before it runs). What every hero prompt
must state physically:

- **Size in mm, against a known object, and "never larger".** The old anchor "a 9 mm eye
  is about the width of a fingertip" asks for 18 to 20 mm and is dropped.

| Size | Anchor in the prompt |
|---|---|
| 6 mm | slightly thinner than a pencil |
| 8.4 to 9.6 mm | a little narrower than the index fingernail |
| 10 to 11.5 mm | about as wide as the index fingernail |
| 13.5 mm (Mother and Child ring) | about as wide as a thumbnail, never wider than the finger |
| 15.8 mm tall (Mother and Child necklace) | about the length of a thumbnail |

- **The metal of every part, by name.** Never "two tone" or "mixed metal" in a prompt.
  One part is one metal: cup and plate, mother and child, coin and almond, wire and
  wire. Never a split within one part (the Meridian arc split), and never a request to
  swap metals between copies.
- **White gold is metal, not enamel:** "polished unplated white gold, a soft warm grey
  metal with mirror reflections". White enamel is "porcelain white enamel, flat, opaque
  and glossy". In Halka the white-gold ring sits exactly where a nazar's white enamel
  would; QA asks of every frame whether each white zone is the right material.
- **Enamel flat, flush and glossy, never domed.** No glass beads, red string, lashes,
  lid lines, realistic irises, shading, iris striations or hamsa props.
- **Closed backs:** every back or side view says "closed, solid, flat gold back".
- **Counts:** any count the copy promises or the eye reads at a glance is three or fewer
  per group. Uniform repeats (the R04 ball band, the R10 link band, a ridged coin edge)
  are allowed only if the copy never states a number; QA checks equal units and a rigid
  band.
- **Words kept out of prompts:** "Iznik" and "tile" (write "a flat hexagon");
  "satellite"; "paperclip chain" (write "two elongated oval links on each side, then a
  fine round cable chain"); "coin" (write "a round disc with a finely ridged edge and a
  plain satin face"); "signet" (write "a flat round top, no engraving"); "bezel", "cup",

  [metal] collar, flush, not domed, with a closed solid back"); "nazar bead" and "bead
  string" (the eye is "a flat enamel disc", the beads "solid polished gold balls").
