# Artifact Studio · SS27 Anklets · 01 Design direction

Status: proposal for owner approval (2026-10-01). Nothing generated, nothing in the panel yet.

## Brief (owner, 2026-09-30 / 10-01)

A new collection of 40 anklets, creative and minimalist, inspired by the six
reference screenshots (a Turkish retailer's anklets). Research decides the icons.

What the references give us, and only this:
- a fine cable chain, about 1 mm, worn low on the ankle with flats or sneakers;
- one small charm (about 6 to 10 mm) hanging from a jump ring at the centre;
- the charm is either one flat enamel field in a polished gold rim, a field of
  tiny set stones, or (seahorse) solid sculpted gold with no colour at all;
- rose and yellow gold; spring ring clasp.

Not taken: their motifs. Butterfly, dragonfly, sailboat, oval turquoise and
seahorse stay theirs; none of them appears below.

## Season signal

- WGSN x Coloro S/S 27 key colours: Luminous Blue (Colour of the Year 2027,
  125-28-38), Energy Orange (018-57-34), Pop Pink (151-73-22), Meadowland Green
  (050-61-19), Clay (014-60-13).
- Anklets in 2026: charm anklets are the lead style (McQueen SS26 sent charm
  anklets with mules and sandals); the dainty single gold chain is the most
  versatile; ocean motifs (shells, starfish), coins, colourful enamel; evil eye
  and hearts top the Etsy charm searches; the category is moving from summer-only
  to year-round.
- What we take: the five key colours, one small charm on a fine chain, shore and
  garden motifs, a share of all-gold charms. What we leave: beads, layered
  multi-chains, plated steel (we stay solid gold), name plates (personalization
  adds a workflow; can be a later line).

## Collection idea: postcards from summer

Each family is one place or moment of a summer day, told in one colour story.
Inside a family the four charms are four different objects, so the family
coordinates without repeating. 32 charms are enamel, 8 are solid sculpted gold.

## Changes after approval (step 3, 2026-10-01)

Checked every drawing against the 1.5 mm minimum cell rule: A21 Evil Eye (7 mm,
three rings, about 1 mm cells) became 8 mm with a blue ring, a white centre and a
solid gold pupil; A34 Lifebuoy and A08 Marigold went to 8 mm (A08 with one ring of
eight petals); A10 Lollipop uses a single-turn spiral; A05 has six segments, A02
four ribs. A35 Sardine is drawn in an open gold tin so it does not repeat A04
Little Fish. Lengths 9, 10, 11 in and provisional FW-rule pricing approved.

## Production rules (why it can be made)

- Enamel: kiln-fired, in cells recessed into solid gold, every cell with a
  polished gold rim; at most two enamel colours per charm; each cell at least
  1.5 mm wide; gold walls at least 0.4 mm. On the smallest charms (6 mm) only one
  colour.
- Solid-gold charms (8): sculpted, closed back, no hollow; texture only where
  stated (starfish dots, coral matte).
- Every charm hangs from a gold jump ring at the centre of a 1.0 mm solid gold
  cable chain, spring ring clasp.
- Metal: the shop rule is the full 3-axis grid (Karat 10K/14K/18K x Metal Color
  Yellow/White/Rose x length). Length options and pricing are step 2.

## Visual style for the hero images (step 4)

Same Artifact Studio language as FW 26/27: warm off-white linen, soft daylight,
true scale, enamel flat and glossy. For anklets, hero is the anklet laid in a
loose curve on linen with the charm in focus; worn shots come later and stay
within the references' scale (charm smaller than the ankle bone).
