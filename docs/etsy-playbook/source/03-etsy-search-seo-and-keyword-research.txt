 1. How Etsy search works in 2025-2026
 1.1 The two-phase model (official)
 Etsy describes search as two phases [S1: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]:
  1. Query matching. Etsy finds listings that match the buyer's query. The handbook says the
    system "takes a holistic view of your listing" and names title, tags, attributes, descriptions, first
    photo and reviews as inputs.
  2. Ranking. Matched listings are ordered so that items shoppers are most likely to buy appear
    earlier.
 Practical meaning: if a listing is not matched (phase 1), no ranking signal can save it. If it is matched
 but ranks poorly (phase 2), the problem is quality, conversion or customer service, not keywords.

 1.3 The Search Visibility page (minimum requirements)
 Etsy Help lists requirements an active listing must meet to get "priority placement" [S2:
 help.etsy.com, undated page, footer 2026]:
  • At least one listing image.
  • A high-resolution primary photo, ideally 2000 px or more in width and height.
  • A primary photo that shows one finished product, not a collage.
  • A clear, well-lit primary photo.
  • A category that is as specific as possible.
  • A return policy, even if it is "No returns".
  • For US listings, domestic shipping under $6 gets priority.
 Shop-level: an incomplete shop (for example, no logo) may lower listing visibility. Customer service
 minimums: respond promptly, ship on time with tracking, keep a positive review score, avoid a high
 number of cases [same source].
 Timing: insights typically clear within 24 hours after fixes, and search changes can take up to a day
 to appear [same source].
 How to use it: Shop Manager > Listings > "Search factors" bar filters listings by visibility issue [same
 source].
 Relevance for this US-based shop: the $6 shipping rule applies to US domestic listings, which is
 what this shop sells. The original Etsy announcement (October 2024, so pre-2025) excluded "listings
 shipped from outside the US" [S3: etsy.com, 2024-10-03]. The 2025 handbook still lists shipping
 price as a US-seller factor [S1: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]. For a shop shipping domestically inside the
 US, the $6 rule applies directly: keep visible shipping under $6 or offer free shipping built into price,
 since buyers also see total cost and conversion (a ranking factor) is affected by it. Verify in your own
 Search Visibility page whether any shipping flag appears.

 1.4 How Etsy uses LLMs and semantic models (Etsy Engineering)


Etsy Fine Jewelry Master Course | Module 3: Etsy Search, SEO and Keyword Research                                  Page 46
 These are engineering posts, not seller guidance. They explain the mechanism; they do not give
 rules.
 LLM-based relevance scoring (published 2026-01-16) [S4: etsy.com, 2026-01-16]:
  • Etsy added "semantic relevance" alongside engagement signals because engagement alone
    favors popular listings over the best match.
  • Relevance labels: Relevant (matches all parts of the query), Partially relevant (matches part,
    or is thematically related), Irrelevant.
  • An LLM annotator (o3 model, few-shot chain-of-thought) reads title, images, description,
    attributes, variations and extracted entities. It trains a Qwen 3 VL 4B "teacher", which trains a
    lightweight BERT two-tower "student" running live in search with under 10 ms latency.
  • In production the student filters irrelevant listings, supplies ranking features and boosts
    highly relevant results.
  • Result: share of fully relevant listings rose from 58% to 62% between August and October 2025.
  • Etsy says better semantic relevance "can also help surface items from small or new sellers."
 Seller implication (inference): a listing is judged on whether it matches all parts of the query. For
 "14k solid gold engraved band", a listing whose text, attributes and images clearly show 14K, solid
 gold, engraving and a band is "Relevant". A gold-plated item stuffed with "solid gold" risks being
 filtered as partially relevant or irrelevant, and violates Etsy's accuracy requirement.
 LLM attribute extraction (published 2025-10-13) [S5: etsy.com, 2025-10-13]:
  • Etsy runs LLMs over listing titles, descriptions and images to infer structured attributes, because
    most attribute fields are optional and details are often "buried in the listing description or only
    available in listing photos."
  • LLM-inferred attributes were added to search filters, increasing filter engagement and post-click
    conversion. Inferred color now drives color swatches on search results.
  • In target categories, complete attribute coverage rose from 31% to 91%.
  • "Seller provided details become especially critical" for niche items.
 Seller implication: your description and photos are read by machines to fill attributes. State metal,
 karat, stone shape, stone type, band width and ring size range in plain words in the description, and
 show them in photos.
 LLM listing summaries (published 2026-05-26) [S6: etsy.com, 2026-05-26]:
  • Etsy fine-tunes Qwen3-VL-8B to write free-form listing summaries capturing distinguishing details
    (for example, how an item was made).
  • Adding these summaries to the semantic relevance model improved macro F1 by 8.7% relative to
    using description n-grams (offline). Online experiments were planned next.
 Seller implication (inference): distinguishing, factual details (handmade in the USA by our partner
 workshop, solid 14K, hand-engraved, made to order in X days) are exactly what these models look
 for. Vague adjectives add nothing.

 2. Exact current guidance per listing element
 2.1 Titles (the "short title" guidance)
 Timeline:
  • Etsy posted updated title guidance and an AI title tool in August 2025 [S16:
    valueaddedresource.net, 2025-08-21].
  • Etsy's Fall 2025 update confirmed the optional title tool in the Search Visibility dashboard [S17:
    etsy.com, 2025-09-15].
  • The current handbook article "New Guidance for Listing Titles, and a Tool to Help" is dated
    2026-04-27 [S13: etsy.com, 2026-04-27].
 Etsy's rules [S13: etsy.com, 2026-04-27; S12: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]:



Etsy Fine Jewelry Master Course | Module 3: Etsy Search, SEO and Keyword Research                                Page 48
  1. State the item clearly (a noun such as "ring"), once.
  2. "Include the most important traits upfront", such as color, material, size.
  3. Include the top three critical descriptors for your category plus any key differentiator.
  4. "Consider using less than 15 words."
  5. Holidays, occasions, recipients only when essential to the item.
  6. Move subjective words ("perfect", "beautiful") to the description.
  7. Do not repeat words or phrases, do not repeat gifting phrases ("gift for him").
  8. Do not add price, shipping or sale info (Etsy badges these).
  9. No keyword stuffing.
  10. Shoppers see only the first few words in search results.
 On placement: Keywords 101 says "keyword placement within the title doesn't affect ranking"; lead
 with the most descriptive words for buyers on mobile [S11: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]. "How to Get
 Found in Search" says keep the most important search terms at the beginning [S18: etsy.com, page
 metadata about 2026-10]. Reconciled reading: front-loading is for the human click, not for the
 algorithm.
 Etsy's own example [S13: etsy.com, 2026-04-27]:
  • Old: "ON SALE ! Birthday Bandana - Happy Birthday, Birthday Dog Bandana, Birthday Cat
    Bandana, Birthday Bones, Birthday Pet Present"
  • New: "Birthday Bones Cotton Dog Bandana - Easy Slip Over the Collar"
 AI title tool facts [same source, 2026-04-27; S2: help.etsy.com, undated, footer 2026]:
  • Optional, English-language shops only; uses current title, first photo and description.
  • Appears in Search Visibility only when suggestions exist; if titles already meet guidance, it does
    not appear.
  • Download a CSV of listings before changing anything, to keep original keywords.
  • Changes can take time to take effect and can be reverted.
  • Seeing a suggestion does not mean your title is underperforming.
 Third-party interpretation (eRank, 2025-08-27, modified 2026-01-06) [S19: help.erank.com]:
  • Etsy said older-style titles are not penalized; updating is optional.
  • Do not change bestsellers. Test underperformers in small batches and monitor 1 to 3 months.
  • The AI tool ignores sales and search performance and may drop important keywords or suggest
    trademarks.
  • Removed keywords should be moved to attributes (if a matching attribute exists), tags (if not; for
    example "gift for her"), or the description. Remove sales/shipping phrases entirely.
  • Formula: Noun + top 3 objective descriptors + other key item-specific info.
 Third-party caution (Value Added Resource quoting a seller, 2025-08-21): change a few listings at a
 time and check whether new titles actually perform better [S16: valueaddedresource.net,
 2025-08-21]. Etsy published no data showing short titles rank better [same source].
 Hard limits: Alura's (undated) doc lists a 140-character title maximum, limits on %, :, &, prohibited $,
 ^, `, and no more than three all-caps words [S20: alura.io, undated]. These limits were not
 re-verified on an Etsy 2025-2026 page (search budget exhausted). Treat as UNVERIFIED but stable
 long-standing limits; Etsy's listing editor enforces them at entry.

 2.2 Tags


Etsy Fine Jewelry Master Course | Module 3: Etsy Search, SEO and Keyword Research                  Page 49
 From Keywords 101 [S11: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]:
  • Use all 13 tags. Each can be up to 20 characters.
  • Use multi-word phrases.
  • "Don't repeat tags." Make them as distinct as possible.
  • Do not repeat categories and attributes as exact tags; you can use those words inside a longer
    tag.
  • "Don't worry about plurals" (root-word matching).
  • "Don't include misspellings" (Etsy redirects common typos).
  • "Don't add tags in multiple languages."
  • Use synonyms and regional terms Etsy may not map.
  • "Do target 'long tail' keywords", which are less competitive and "tend to convert better".
  • For phrases longer than 20 characters, split into tag-sized chunks.
  • Refresh tags on low-traffic listings after checking Shop Stats.
 From the anatomy article [S12: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]:
  • "If you can't imagine someone typing the phrase into Google, leave it out."
  • Vary tags across what the product is, how it's made, and who it's for.
 From "How to Get Found in Search" [S18: etsy.com, about 2026-10]: do not repeat attribute info like
 color in tags, but specialized terms (for example "mint green") still belong in tags and titles. Applied
 to jewelry: the color attribute "gold" is covered, but "rose gold" or "yellow gold" as a phrase may still
 deserve a tag if no attribute captures it. Verify in your listing editor which values exist.

 2.3 Attributes and categories
  • Choose the most specific subcategory; it unlocks category-specific attributes and the item also
    appears in parent categories [S11: etsy.com, 2025-08-26].
  • Fill in all relevant attributes, "even less precise ones" [same].
  • "Choose the option that best fits, even if it isn't how you would describe the item" [S12: etsy.com,
    2025-08-26].
  • Search Visibility flags a category that is not as specific as possible [S2: help.etsy.com, undated,
    footer 2026].
  • Etsy's Fall 2025 attribute suggestions tool was limited to Wall Hangings and Prints, "with more
    categories to come" [S17: etsy.com, 2025-09-15].
  • Third-party (Listadum, 2026-07-09): when a buyer applies a filter, "Etsy only shows listings that
    have set that attribute"; an unset attribute is "a door you closed" [S21: listadum.com,
    2026-07-09].
 Ring subcategories visible on Etsy's Rings category page (fetched 2026-10-22): Wedding &
 Engagement, Statement Rings, Signet Rings, Multi-Stone Rings, Bands; listed ring types include
 bands, midi rings, multi-stone, triplet and double, ring guards and spacers, solitaire, stackable,
 statement, signet, fidget [S22: etsy.com, accessed 2026-10-22]. The filter panel did not render in
 fetch; the full attribute list for rings is a GAP (see section 13). Action: open the listing editor in each
 ring subcategory and screenshot every attribute field and its allowed values. That screenshot
 becomes your attribute map.

  • Shop title: up to 55 characters; shows under shop name; you can preview how it looks in Google

 2.7 Official Etsy checklist ("Checklist: Optimize Your Shop for Etsy Search",
 updated August 2025)
 [S15: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]
  1. Use the search visibility page.
  2. Meet customer service standards (review rating, message response rate, case rate, past three
    months).
  3. Use all 13 tags, multi-word natural phrases.
  4. Keep shipping prices competitive (US domestic under $6 gets priority).
  5. Add relevant attributes (no need to repeat them as tags).
  6. Write short, clear titles, most important keywords first.
  7. Lead with your best photo.
  8. Add clear shop policies; Etsy's policy template can provide an additional boost.

 3.2 eRank
 Metric definitions [S28: hc.erank.com, 2026-06-05]:
  • Average Searches: average monthly searches for the keyword in the selected marketplace and
    country, averaged over the past 12 months.
  • Average Clicks: average monthly clicks on listings for that keyword, past 12 months.
  • Average CTR: clicks divided by searches, as a percentage. Can exceed 100% because one buyer
    can click several listings [S29: help.erank.com, 2024-12-05, modified 2025-05-28].
  • Etsy Competition: number of listings matched to the keyword, excluding ads and personalized
    results.
  • Keyword Difficulty (KD): ratio of monthly search volume to Etsy competition; lower suggests
    less competition relative to demand. (eRank's own example numbers do not reproduce the ratio
    exactly; the page does not explain a scaling factor.)
  • Trend graphs: 15 months of monthly volume, for seasonality.
  • Color coding: dark green very good to red very poor (for competition and KD, green means low).
 CTR guidance (eRank, third-party): "A low CTR often indicates a keyword is too broad"; compare
 related phrasings, for example a lower-volume phrase with higher CTR may have more engaged
 buyers [S29: help.erank.com, modified 2025-05-28].
 Data source: eRank estimates search volume using third-party analytics data and offers 15 months
 of history and per-country filtering, versus Marketplace Insights' 30 days, global [S30:
 help.erank.com, 2025-10-22]. Quotas quoted there: Keyword Tool 35 keywords/week free, 1,400
 Pro, 24,500 Expert [same].

 Keyword workflow in Alura (Search tool, a.k.a. Keyword Finder) [S33: help.alura.io, 2025-10-14]:
  1. Seed 5 to 8 terms or use AI Suggestions from a product description or existing listing.
  2. Check Keyword Score (0-100), which balances demand against competition: 80-100 excellent,

  3. In Similar Keywords, toggle Valid Etsy Tag (hides phrases over 20 characters) and choose

    "for her", "personalized".
  4. Listings tab: open top listings, read their tags; tags shared by several top listings suggest
    proven phrases.
  5. Save with the heart (Keyword List) or plus (add to a listing).
  6. Validate in Bulk Search or Tag Manager, sort by Keyword Score.
  7. Alura's suggested 13-tag mix "4-6-3": 4 core, 6 long-tail, 3 exploratory. Skip terms covered by
    category or attributes; avoid trademarks; protect tags already bringing traffic (Search Terms

  Question                                                   Best source

 4. STEP BY STEP KEYWORD WORKFLOW
 Use this for every new listing and every quarterly refresh. Time per listing after the first few: about
 30 to 45 minutes.
 Step 1. Define the product facts sheet (5 min). Write, in plain words: item noun (ring, band,
 signet ring), metal (solid 14K or 18K gold), color of gold (yellow, white, rose), stone (shape: princess
 cut; type), technique (engraved, hand engraved, enamel), customization (name, initials, date,
 coordinates, font choice), sizes offered, band width, made-to-order lead time, origin (made in the
 USA by our partner workshop, ships from [US state]). This sheet feeds attributes, description and alt
 text. It also prevents inaccurate keywords.
 Step 2. Build the seed list by intent bucket (10 min). Create five columns:
  • Product: "gold ring", "gold band", "signet ring", "stacking ring", "princess cut ring".
  • Material/spec: "14k solid gold", "18k gold", "yellow gold", "white gold", "rose gold".
  • Personalization: "engraved", "personalized", "custom name", "initial", "date".
  • Occasion: anniversary, wedding, engagement, birthday, push present, graduation, Mother's Day,
    Valentine's Day, Christmas.
  • Recipient: for her, for him, for wife, for mom, for husband.
 Step 3. Harvest Etsy autocomplete (5 min). Type each product seed into the Etsy search bar
 and record suggestions. Etsy's own handbook recommends this and testing synonyms [S18:



Etsy Fine Jewelry Master Course | Module 3: Etsy Search, SEO and Keyword Research                                Page 54
 etsy.com, about 2026-10]. Use Alura's Chrome extension to see data on suggestions (Growth: 500
 autocomplete lookups/day) [S32: alura.io, accessed 2026-10-22].
 Step 4. Validate demand with Marketplace Insights (spend 3 to 5 of your 15 weekly
 searches). Run only the 3 to 5 most important head phrases. Record 30-day search volume and
 listing count, and copy every related term. Related terms with high interest and fewer listings are
 your priority long-tails [S26: help.etsy.com, undated, footer 2026].
 Step 5. Expand and score in Alura (10 min). Put head phrases into Alura Search. Toggle Valid
 Etsy Tag. Use Phrase match. Export or save all candidates with Keyword Score of 60 or more. Then
 Bulk Search up to 100 candidates and sort by score [S33: help.alura.io, 2025-10-14].
 Step 6. Check engagement and seasonality in eRank (10 min). Put the top 20 candidates into
 eRank Bulk Keyword Tool (20 at a time). For each, note Average Searches, Competition, CTR, and
 the 15-month trend shape [S28: hc.erank.com, 2026-06-05]. Drop phrases whose trend shows they
 are only alive in a season you are not listing for.
 Step 7. Apply the selection heuristic. (Heuristic, not an Etsy rule.)
  • Keep: Alura score 60+, eRank CTR not in the lowest color band, competition not in the red band,
    and the phrase is 100% accurate for the item.
  • Prefer 3 to 5 word long-tails. Etsy itself says long-tails are less competitive and tend to convert
    better [S11: etsy.com, 2025-08-26].
  • When two phrasings exist, keep the higher-CTR one in a tag; eRank's example shows a
    lower-volume phrasing can have more engaged buyers [S29: help.erank.com, modified
    2025-05-28].
  • Never keep a phrase that is only true of a different metal (for example "gold filled", "gold plated",
    "sterling silver") on a solid gold listing.
 Step 8. Assign keywords to slots.
  • Attributes first: every fact that has an attribute field goes there (metal, color, occasion, recipient,
    personalization, style, stone). Then do not spend tags on exact attribute values [S15: etsy.com,
    2025-08-26].
  • Title: noun + top 3 objective descriptors + one differentiator, under 15 words [S13: etsy.com,
    2026-04-27].
  • Tags: 13, each under 20 characters, each distinct. Suggested mix (Alura 4-6-3): 4 core product
    phrases, 6 long-tails with occasion/recipient/personalization, 3 exploratory.
  • Description first 2 sentences: the main phrase and 2 or 3 secondary phrases written naturally.
  • Alt text: describe what the photo shows (metal color, stone shape, engraving visible), up to 250
    characters.
 Step 9. Map across the catalog (prevents cannibalization). See section 5.
 Step 10. Publish, record baseline, and wait. Record date, title, tags, attributes, and the
 baseline from Search Analytics. Search Visibility changes can take up to a day to appear [S2:
 help.etsy.com, undated, footer 2026]. Do not judge results before the windows in section 6.
 Step 11. Monthly review. Etsy Search Analytics and Stats > How shoppers found you > Etsy
 search. Keep tags that bring visits and orders. Replace tags with zero impressions after 30 to 60
 days. eRank Traffic Stats and Spotted on Etsy confirm which keywords each listing appears for [S31:
 hc.erank.com, 2026-08-04].
 Step 12. Seasonal refresh. Etsy: "refresh tags and titles, especially before seasons or holidays"
 [S15: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]; "list seasonal items early, before demand peaks" [S27: etsy.com,


Etsy Fine Jewelry Master Course | Module 3: Etsy Search, SEO and Keyword Research                     Page 55
 2025-11-04]. For made-to-order gold, lead time is long, so seasonal tags should go live well before
 the season's last order date. Use eRank's 15-month trend to see when each occasion phrase starts
 rising, and update before that month. (No Etsy-published lead-time number exists; derive it from
 your own trend graphs.)


 5. Keyword mapping and cannibalization
 Etsy's guidance: "Don't give similar items identical or nearly identical names. Varied keywords reach
 different buyers" [S18: etsy.com, about 2026-10]. Diverse inventory and varied relevant tags create
 more search matches [S1: etsy.com, 2025-08-26].
 Method (keyword map spreadsheet):
  1. Columns: Listing ID | Primary keyword (title) | Secondary keywords (tags) | Attributes set |
    Occasion focus | Recipient focus | Status.
  2. Each listing owns one primary phrase. No two listings share the same primary phrase.
  3. Listings of the same product type split by a differentiator: metal color, karat, stone shape, band
    width, technique, occasion, recipient.
  4. Shared broad tags (for example "gold ring") are acceptable on several listings; the long-tails
    must differ.
  5. Re-check the map whenever a new listing is added.
 Example split for five engraved band listings (illustrative, validate before use):

  Listing            Primary phrase owned

  A                  14k solid gold engraved band (yellow)

  B                  18k gold name ring (personalized name)

  C                  white gold wedding band engraved

  D                  rose gold stacking ring with initial

  E                  anniversary band with date engraving

 6.1 Etsy Stats

  • Visits = people who looked at your shop or listings; views = total listing looks. One shopper who

  • Traffic sources show where the buyer first came from (Etsy search, Etsy app and other pages,

  • Visits refresh a few times a day; bot filtering can lower counts at day end.

 6.4 Testing title changes safely
  1. Download listing CSV first (Etsy's own instruction) [S13: etsy.com, 2026-04-27].
  2. Do not touch listings that already sell well (eRank) [S19: help.erank.com, 2025-08-27].
  3. Change one variable at a time (title OR tags OR thumbnail), on a small batch. Etsy: "Make
    changes gradually so you can see what works" [S18: etsy.com, about 2026-10].
  4. Compare same-length windows before and after, avoiding holiday spikes. eRank suggests
    monitoring 1 to 3 months [S19: help.erank.com, 2025-08-27].
  5. Alura Growth allows 4 active A/B tests on thumbnails, titles or prices [S32: alura.io, accessed
    2026-10-22]. Use them on mid-traffic listings, where there is enough data.
  6. Keep a change log (date, listing, old value, new value, reason).

 6.5 How long ranking takes to update
  • Etsy: Search Visibility insights clear within 24 hours; "search changes can take up to a day to
    appear" [S2: help.etsy.com, undated, footer 2026].
  • Etsy: title changes "may take time to take effect" [S13: etsy.com, 2026-04-27].
  • Third-party: rankings can appear in hours or take 1 to 2 days [S23: growingyourcraft.com,
    2025-12-13].
  • Distinguish indexing (a day) from performance stabilizing (weeks, because engagement rate is
    computed from accumulated views and purchases).

 6.6 New listing boost: verified vs myth
  • VERIFIED: new listings get "a small, temporary boost" lasting a few hours to a few days; renewed
    listings get a smaller one [S1: etsy.com, 2025-08-26].



Etsy Fine Jewelry Master Course | Module 3: Etsy Search, SEO and Keyword Research                                   Page 57
  • VERIFIED: renewing only for the boost "isn't an effective strategy" [same].
  • MYTH: a long "honeymoon" period of weeks during which new listings rank high. No Etsy source
    supports this.
  • MYTH: deleting and relisting a listing to restart its boost. The boost is hours to days, and you lose
    accumulated reviews, favorites and engagement history on that listing (inference from
    engagement rate being a ranking factor).
  • Third-party claim of a new-shop boost "for a period of time" is unsourced [S23:
    growingyourcraft.com, 2025-12-13].

 7.4 Terms to avoid
  • Brand and designer names and "style/inspired" phrasings (for example a luxury jewelry house
    name followed by "style"). Etsy's IP policy page does not list keyword rules [S38: etsy.com, last
    updated 2023-06-15, pre-2025], but third-party guidance notes listings using protected names
    without license can be removed without warning and repeat infringement can lead to shop
    suspension [S39: outfy.com, 2025-10-21, updated 2026-07-22]. Alura's Keyword guide says avoid
    trademarked terms you do not own [S33: help.alura.io, 2025-10-14], and Alura's Listing Helper
    flags potential trademark infringement [S34: help.alura.io, 2024-03-29].
  • Marketplace Insights will show trademarked terms buyers search for; seeing a term there is not
    permission to use it [S26: help.etsy.com, undated, footer 2026].
  • Inaccurate materials: "gold filled", "vermeil", "plated", "sterling" on solid gold listings, or
    "diamond" for a simulant. Also, US jewelry marketing rules on metal and stone descriptions exist

  • Subjective words in titles: "perfect", "beautiful", "stunning" (move to description) [S13: etsy.com,
    2026-04-27].
  • Sale and shipping words: "free shipping", "sale" [same].
  • Misspellings and foreign-language tags [S11: etsy.com, 2025-08-26].

 9. TACTICS



Etsy Fine Jewelry Master Course | Module 3: Etsy Search, SEO and Keyword Research                 Page 60
  1. Attribute-first listing build. Fill every attribute before writing the title. Then only spend title
    words and tags on what attributes cannot express. Basis: attributes act like tags and drive filters
    [S11: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]; unset attributes exclude you from filtered results (third-party) [S21:
    listadum.com, 2026-07-09].
  2. Make "solid gold" machine-readable three times. Title, first sentence of description, and a
    close-up photo of the karat stamp with alt text "14K stamp inside solid gold band". Basis: Etsy's
    LLMs read title, description and photos to infer attributes and judge relevance [S5: etsy.com,
    2025-10-13].
  3. Match "all parts of the query". For your top 10 target queries, write the query down and
    verify each word is literally supported by the listing (text, attribute or visible in photo). Basis:
    "Relevant" = matches all parts of the query [S4: etsy.com, 2026-01-16].
  4. Thumbnail discipline. One finished ring, no collage, no text, 2000 px+, well lit [S2:
    help.etsy.com, undated, footer 2026]. Test thumbnails with Alura A/B (4 slots on Growth).
  5. Spend Marketplace Insights quota on head terms only. 15 per week. Use Alura (500/day)
    and eRank for expansion.
  6. Variant listings for different intent, not duplicates. Yellow vs white vs rose gold, 14K vs
    18K, name vs date engraving can justify separate listings if each targets a distinct phrase and
    has distinct photos. Basis: "Don't give similar items identical or nearly identical names" [S18:
    etsy.com, about 2026-10]. Note: variations inside one listing concentrate reviews and
    engagement; separate listings widen matching. Decide per product using Search Analytics data.
  7. Seasonal tag rotation. Keep 2 to 3 tag slots as "seasonal": anniversary/wedding in spring,
    Christmas in Q4, Valentine's in Q1, Mother's Day in Q2. Swap based on eRank trend graphs, early
    enough to cover made-to-order lead time.
  8. Customer service as SEO. Message response rate, review rating and case rate (past 3
    months) are ranking inputs [S1: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]. For made-to-order gold with long lead
    times, accurate processing times avoid late-shipment cases.
  9. Use Etsy's policy template. Etsy says it "can provide an additional boost" [S15: etsy.com,
    2025-08-26].
  10. Do not turn on vacation mode casually. Engagement data stops updating while others'
    keeps moving [S1: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]. For short breaks, extend processing time instead
    (practical inference).
  11. English only, US spelling. Shop language must match titles/tags; Etsy handles
    "jewelry"/"jewellery" but not all regional terms [S11: etsy.com, 2025-08-26].
  12. Use the AI title tool as a draft, not an answer. Check it kept "solid", karat, and your
    differentiator; check it did not add trademarks (eRank warning) [S19: help.erank.com,
    2025-08-27].


 10. COMMON MISTAKES
  1. Keyword-stuffed 140-character titles. Contradicts Etsy's under-15-words guidance [S13:
    etsy.com, 2026-04-27].
  2. Rewriting every title at once after the guidance. Etsy says older titles are not penalized
    (per eRank) and seeing a suggestion does not mean underperformance [S19: help.erank.com,
    2025-08-27; S13: etsy.com, 2026-04-27].
  3. Deleting removed title keywords. They should move to attributes, tags or description [S19:
    help.erank.com, 2025-08-27].



Etsy Fine Jewelry Master Course | Module 3: Etsy Search, SEO and Keyword Research                      Page 61
  4. Fewer than 13 tags, or single-word tags. Etsy: use all 13, use phrases; eRank Health Check
    flags one-word tags [S31: hc.erank.com, 2026-08-04].
  5. Tags that duplicate attributes or category exactly ("gold", "ring", "rings") [S11: etsy.com,
    2025-08-26].
  6. Plural and misspelling tags ("gold rings" plus "gold ring", "jewelery") [same].
  7. Generic category such as Jewelry > Rings when a specific subcategory (Bands, Signet Rings)
    exists [S2: help.etsy.com, undated, footer 2026].
  8. Collage or text-overlay thumbnail [same].
  9. Renewing listings for a boost. Ineffective per Etsy [S1: etsy.com, 2025-08-26].
  10. Trusting tool scores as Etsy's algorithm. Alura Listing Score and eRank grades are tool
    checklists; Etsy's factors are in section 1.2.
  11. Reading competitor sales estimates as facts. Alura est_ fields and eRank Competitor
    Sales are estimates [S32: alura.io, accessed 2026-10-22].
  12. Judging a change after 2 days. Indexing is about a day; performance needs weeks of data.
  13. Using brand names or "style/inspired" phrasing. IP takedown risk (see 7.4).
  14. Identical titles across color or karat variants, causing listings to compete for the same
    phrase.
  15. No alt text, leaving Etsy to auto-generate it for US sellers, or stuffing alt text with tags (no
    benefit) [S14: help.etsy.com, undated, footer 2026].
