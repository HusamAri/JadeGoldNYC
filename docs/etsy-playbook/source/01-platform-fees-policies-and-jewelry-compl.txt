 1.2 Core per-listing and per-sale fees (detail)
  • Listing fee: $0.20 per listing, charged whether or not the item sells. Listings expire after four
    months and auto-renew at $0.20 unless auto-renew is switched off. Private listings are
    charged only when sold. Editing a listing is free. [S9: etsy.com, last updated Oct 5, 2026]
  • Multi-quantity / renewal on sale: each sale of a listing with remaining quantity triggers
    another $0.20 renewal. [S9: etsy.com, Oct 5, 2026]
  • Transaction fee: 6.5% of the displayed price plus shipping and gift wrap. The fees page states:
    "If you sell from the US, the transaction fee will not apply to sales tax." [S9: etsy.com, Oct 5,
    2026]. This exclusion applies to this shop.
  • The October 5, 2026 revision of the fees policy did not change any rates. It moved the
    paragraph on fee avoidance (e.g. completing a sale off Etsy, buying via QR code) to a separate
    Off-Platform Transactions policy. [S10: growtsy.com, (third party), checked Oct 2026]. The fees
    page itself now says "any action to move a transaction off the Etsy platform is strictly prohibited
    by Etsy." [S9: etsy.com, Oct 5, 2026]
  • A February 13, 2026 fees-policy revision also left headline rates unchanged. [S10:
    growtsy.com, (third party), 2026]
  • No change to the 6.5% transaction fee, $0.20 listing fee or 3% + $0.25 US processing was found
    in 2025 to 2026 primary sources. [S1: etsy.com, 2026-10-05]; [S2: etsy.com, 2026-07-31]

 1.3 Payment processing (Etsy Payments, US)
  • Charged on the total sale price including tax and shipping; the rate is set by the location of
    the seller's bank account. [S9: etsy.com, Oct 5, 2026; S11: etsy.com, last updated Jul 31, 2026]
  • United States bank account: 3% + USD 0.25 per order. [S11: etsy.com, Jul 31, 2026]
  • Because processing is charged on the gross including sales tax, the shop pays 3% on the tax the
    buyer pays, even though the transaction fee excludes tax. [S2: etsy.com, 2026-07-31; S1:
    etsy.com, 2026-10-05]

 1.5 Currency conversion
  • 2.5% of the sale amount applies only when the listing currency differs from the payment-account
    currency. [S9: etsy.com, Oct 5, 2026; S11: etsy.com, Jul 31, 2026]
  • With listings in USD and a USD payment account at a US bank, no conversion fee applies. Do
    not switch listing currency or connect a non-USD account. [same sources]

 1.6 Regulatory Operating Fee (ROF)
  • The ROF is a fixed percentage set by country and charged on display price plus shipping in
    "certain countries"; the US is not listed. [S1: etsy.com, 2026-10-05]
  • "US-based sellers don't pay this particular fee." [S3: iscompliant.app, (third party), 2026-06-22]
  • The changes effective 22 Jun 2026 covered France, Hungary, India, Italy, Spain, Türkiye and the
    UK, not the US. [S15: valueaddedresource.net, (third party), 2026-04-23, updated 2026-08-09;
    S16: community.etsy.com, announced ~Apr 2026, effective Jun 22, 2026]
  • For this shop: ROF = 0%. Remove it from every fee formula.

 1.7 Offsite Ads
  • 15% of the attributed order for shops with under $10,000 in sales over the prior 365 days;
    these shops may opt out. [S9: etsy.com, Oct 5, 2026]

    mandatory and the 12% rate applies for the lifetime of the shop, even if sales later fall below

    [S4: help.etsy.com, read 2026-10-22]. With free shipping and no gift wrap, the item price is used
    in this module's examples.

    listings. [S17: help.etsy.com, Jun 2026 section]

 1.10 Shop set-up fee (new sellers)
  • The fees policy lists a one-time set-up fee charged after onboarding, non-refundable once
    paid, may be waived, may be subject to tax. No amount is published on the policy page; the
    amount is shown during shop opening. [S9: etsy.com, Oct 5, 2026]
  • A third-party 2026 guide quotes $15 to $29, applying to all new shops including additional shops
    opened by existing sellers. [S19: craftybase.com, (third party), "Updated for 2026"]
  • Etsy's 2025 10-K attributes part of the year-over-year decline in active sellers to the set-up fee,
    which it says was meant to encourage more committed sellers; active sellers were 5.6 million at
    Dec 31, 2025. [S20: investors.etsy.com, 10-K filed Feb 19, 2026]
  • Relevance: opening a second shop (e.g. Artifact Studio Jewelry alongside EON) triggers a new
    set-up fee and new identity verification.

    count; sales attributed to Offsite Ads do not qualify (and incur the Offsite Ads fee); refunded

 2.2 Federal tax forms and 1099-K
  • IRS: under the One Big Beautiful Bill, payment platforms need not file Form 1099-K unless
    payments to a payee exceed $20,000 and more than 200 transactions; the law
    "retroactively reinstated" the pre-2021 threshold. [S22: irs.gov, 2025-10-23 (IR-2025-107)]
  • Etsy: for the 2025 tax year (forms issued January 2026), federal threshold $20,000 and 200
    sales, both required. Some states use lower thresholds: Arkansas $2,500; DC $600; Illinois $1,000
    and 4 transactions; Maryland $600; Massachusetts $600; New Jersey $1,000; Vermont $600;
    Virginia $600. A 1099-K is issued if either the federal or the state threshold is met. [S23:
    help.etsy.com, no date shown, read 2026-10-22]
  • 2026 threshold: Etsy's page gives no 2026 figure; the IRS release describes a reinstated
    threshold without naming years. Treat $20,000 and 200 as the expected 2026 federal rule, but
    confirm in January 2027. (See gaps.)
  • Etsy needs a valid SSN, ITIN or EIN on the Legal and tax information page; reminders at $250
    and $500 in sales; shops without valid taxpayer details are suspended after exceeding $500.
    [S23: help.etsy.com, read 2026-10-22]
  • Business sellers provide an EIN (US only), entity name and owners holding 25% or more. [S14:
    help.etsy.com, no date shown, read 2026-10-22]
  • W-8BEN: Etsy says non-US sellers (or those whose address or currency marks them as US
    sellers) can remove the 1099-K obligation by submitting a W-8BEN through support. [S23:
    help.etsy.com, read 2026-10-22]. For a shop held by a US entity this route generally does not
    fit; get CPA advice before filing anything. (See gaps.)

 2.3 Foreign-owned US LLC filing note (high level only)
  • A foreign-owned US disregarded entity (typical: single-member LLC owned by a non-US person)
    has no income tax return of its own, but must file a pro forma Form 1120 with Form 5472
    attached each year. [S24: irs.gov, instructions revised 12/2024, page reviewed 2026-04-30]
  • Penalty for failing to file Form 5472 when due: $25,000, plus $25,000 per 30-day period if the
    failure continues more than 90 days after IRS notice. [same source]
  • Whether the owner personally owes US income tax depends on whether the business has US
    effectively connected income (likely, given a US production partner and US shipping). This is a
    CPA question, not a course claim. (See gaps.)

 2.5 If you ever ship outside the US or import components
  • Etsy's DDP rule (mandatory since July 9, 2026) applies only to sellers outside the US shipping
    to US buyers; orders shipped to the US must use a DDP method. It does not apply to this shop's
    domestic US shipments. [S30: etsy.com, last updated Oct 5, 2026; S17: help.etsy.com, Jun 2026].
    Purchase Protection's DDP condition is likewise only for US-bound international orders. [S31:
    etsy.com, effective 2026-07-09]
  • Importing Turkish-made jewelry or components (B2B, e.g. stock rings for the US workshop to
    finish): HTS 7113.19.50 gold jewelry carries 5.5% general duty [S32: hts.usitc.gov, read
    2026-10-22], plus the 12.5% Section 301 tariff tier for Türkiye effective 24 July 2026 [S33:
    taxnews.ey.com, 2026-07-24], for 18% of the import (customs) value, not the Etsy retail
    price, unless an annex exemption covers heading 7113 (not confirmed; see gaps).
  • Duty-free de minimis treatment ($800 or less) has been suspended for all countries since 29 Aug
    2025, so every import pays duty. [S34: kpmg.com, 2025-08-28]
  • Any international sale from the US shop needs customs forms and accurate declarations for
    the destination country; UPS Etsy labels are for domestic addresses only. [S26: help.etsy.com,
    read 2026-10-22]
  • If everything is made in the US from US-sourced metal, no import duty applies. [course correction
    file 09, 2026-10-22]

 3.1 Creativity Standards (current text last updated June 10, 2025)
 Every listing must fit one of four categories. [S35: etsy.com, last updated Jun 10, 2025]
  1. Made by a seller: physical items the seller makes by hand or with personal or computerized
    tools (laser cutter, 3D printer) in the seller's personal shop or home, including handcrafted,
    hand-altered and hand-assembled items. Does not qualify: kits assembled from manufacturer

     instructions, base items with superficial add-ons. Photos must be the seller's own photos or
     video of the final product; for personalized items the first image must show the
     finished item, not a blank or placeholder text.
  2. Designed by a seller: original designs sold as digital downloads, designs produced by a
    production partner, or AI creations based on the seller's original prompts. Does not qualify:
    bundles/scans of others' work, AI prompt bundles.
  3. Handpicked by a seller: vintage (at least 20 years old), one-of-a-kind natural items, curated
    gift baskets.
  4. Sourced by a seller: craft and party supplies, buyer-personalized items made by a production
    partner.
 Items made with computerized tools "must be produced based on a seller's original design." [S10:
 growtsy.com, (third party), quoting Creativity Standards, 2026]
 Where this shop sits: the owner creates original designs and the US workshop casts, sets,
 finishes and ships them, so listings are "Designed by a seller" with the US workshop disclosed
 as a production partner. Production-partner items fall under "Designed," not "Made." [S36:
 etsy.com, last updated 2025-06-10]. Etsy's Creativity Standards have no jewelry-specific section;
 the only jewelry example is attaching charms to a chain as "hand assembled," and casting is not
 mentioned. [S35: etsy.com, Jun 10, 2025]
 Listing image rule (22 Oct 2026): "For most products, you must include at least one unedited
 photo of the actual item against a real background," and all photos must accurately represent what
 the buyer receives. The policy requires "at least one" and does not say which slot; no effective date
 or exceptions list was published. [S37: etsy.com, last updated 2026-10-22; Etsy seller email,
 2026-10-22, reproduced on sellersasksellers.com]. For a remote owner this means the US workshop
 must supply a straight-from-camera photo of each real piece on a real surface (see module 05).

 3.2 Production partners (the US workshop)
  • Definition: a company or individual outside your Etsy shop that physically produces items from
    your original designs (print-on-demand, cut-and-sew, contract manufacturers, specialized
    services like laser cutting). Any size, from an individual crafter to an automated facility. [S38:
    help.etsy.com, © 2026]
  • Not production partners (and therefore not allowed as a sourcing route): you or your
    shop team; suppliers of raw materials or tools; partners who helped with only one part of an item
    you mostly made; white-label manufacturers, OEMs and ODMs; commercial retailers and
    wholesalers. Using these to source products "violates Etsy's reselling policies and may lead to
    penalties, up to suspension." [same article]
  • How to disclose:
     1. Listing editor: Listing details > Production partners (or Shop Manager > Listings > Editing

 6. US jewelry compliance
 6.1 FTC Jewelry Guides, 16 CFR Part 23 (in force; eCFR current to Oct 19,
 2026)
 Source for all bullets: [S57: ecfr.gov, eCFR up to date as of Oct 19, 2026]. The Guides were last
 revised in 2018 and no 2025 or 2026 revision exists (see section 11).
  • §23.1 General deception: misrepresenting type, grade, metallic content, treatment, origin or
    other material aspects is unfair or deceptive. Qualifications must be clear and prominent.
  • §23.3 Gold: unqualified "Gold" only for fine (24K) gold. Alloys need a karat designation of equal
    conspicuousness before "Gold": write "14 Karat Gold," "14K Gold," "18K Gold," never just "gold
    ring." Markings are subject to National Stamping Act tolerances.
  • Gold filled / rolled gold plate (§23.3(c)(2)): at least 10K; "Gold Filled" requires gold at least
    1/20th of total metal weight; rolled gold plate at least 1/40th, with the fraction disclosed.
  • Gold electroplate (§23.3(c)(3)): at least 10K and at least 0.175 microns fine gold equivalent;
    "Heavy Gold Electroplate" about 2.5 microns.
  • Vermeil (§23.4): sterling silver base, gold at least 10K, at least 2.5 microns fine gold
    equivalent; a nickel undercoat must be disclosed.
  • Platinum (§23.6): unqualified "Platinum" requires 950 parts per thousand; number-prefixed
    marks at least 850; 500 to 850 requires full composition disclosure.
  • Rhodium (§23.7): failing to disclose rhodium plating on a precious-metal product is deceptive.
    This matters for 14K and 18K white gold, which is normally rhodium plated: say
    "rhodium plated" in the listing.
  • Diamond (§23.12): unqualified "diamond" means natural; "cultured" may describe lab-created
    diamonds only with a clear disclosure such as "laboratory-created."

  • Lab-grown and simulated stones (§23.25): the gem name must be immediately preceded by
    "laboratory-grown," "laboratory-created," "[manufacturer]-created," "imitation" or "simulated."

 6.2 National Gold and Silver Stamping Act (15 U.S.C. §§291 to 300)
  • Tolerance (15 U.S.C. §295): gold tested without solder must not be below the stamped
    fineness by more than 3 parts per thousand; tested with solder as one piece: 7 parts per
    thousand for jewelry (3 for watchcases and flatware). The old half-karat standard was replaced
    by these thousandths in 1976. [S58: law.cornell.edu, read Oct 2026]. Practically: a "14K" (583.3)
    piece without solder must assay at least 580.3; with solder, at least 576.3.
  • Trademark requirement (15 U.S.C. §297(b)): anyone who applies a gold or silver quality
    mark must also apply a trademark that is "duly registered or applied for registration under the
    laws of the United States" within thirty days after an article bearing it is placed in commerce or
    imported. [S59: law.cornell.edu, read Oct 2026]. MJSA's compliance guide describes this as the
    "federally registered trademark" stamped near the quality mark. [S60: cms.mjsa.org, undated,
    read Oct 2026]
  • Enforcement: forfeiture, fines and possible jail; competitors and trade associations can sue.
    [MJSA guide, read Oct 2026]
  • Implication for EON / Artifact Studio: if the US workshop stamps "14K" or "585" on rings sold
    under your brand, the piece also needs a maker's mark that is a USPTO-registered or
    applied-for trademark (yours, or the workshop's if it is the one applying the quality mark;
    agree in writing whose mark is used). Any imported components with foreign assay marks alone
    do not satisfy §297.

 6.3 California rules (Metal-Containing Jewelry Law, H&S Code §25214.2)
 [S61: california.public.law, operative Jun 1, 2020, read Oct 2026]
  • Adult jewelry must be made entirely from listed materials. Karat gold, sterling silver,
    platinum-group metals, stainless/surgical steel and natural or cultured pearls are listed
    without a lead percentage (compliant by material class). Electroplated metal and unplated other
    metals: lead under 0.05% (500 ppm); plastic/rubber under 0.02%; coatings under 0.05%.
  • For body-piercing jewelry, solid 14K or higher nickel-free white or yellow gold is an
    approved material.
  • Children's jewelry: every component no more than 0.01% (100 ppm) lead (inaccessible parts
    excluded); coatings no more than 0.009% (90 ppm); cadmium over 0.03% (300 ppm) in any
    component prohibited; coatings over 0.0075% (75 ppm) soluble cadmium prohibited.

    Never connect a non-USD account to the US shop. [fees page Oct 5, 2026; Etsy Payments policy

  3. Keep Offsite Ads on for high-ticket listings (the $100 cap makes it 5% or less on a $2,000+

    branded box inside a plain outer mailer so the insurance is not voided. File any insurance claim
    before cancelling an order. Remove any leftover "duties prepaid" wording or tariff pricing from
    listings; it does not apply to domestic orders.
  5. Insure every shipment over $250 and use signature confirmation as a house rule (judgment,
    not a platform rule: signature on orders over $250, Adult Signature on $1,000+). Purchase
    Protection stops at $250, and carrier or insurer coverage must be claimed first anyway.
  6. Set realistic processing times for made-to-order (for example 5 to 7 business days making

    hurts Customer Service Standards, Star Seller and Purchase Protection eligibility all at once.
    Never agree to a new ship date only in Messages; update the order's ship-by date in Etsy.
  7. 48-hour Help with Order discipline from Türkiye: enable app notifications and set a
    vacation/auto-reply. A third-party source says acknowledging auto-replies can count. Aim for 24
    hours for Star Seller. Use the overlap of a Türkiye workday with US mornings, and plan cover for
    Turkish and US public holidays.
  8. Disclose production correctly: add the US workshop as a production partner (descriptive title
    allowed, e.g. "Fine jewelry casting studio," with its US city and state). Describe your role (design,
    CAD, QC direction, stone sourcing, final approval) and that you run the shop from Türkiye in the
    About section. Never use a white-label/OEM catalogue product.
  9. Ship to the Etsy order address only. Address changes requested in Messages may signal
    fraud and break Purchase Protection condition D. Brief the workshop on this rule in writing.
  10. Photos from the real piece: the workshop photographs each finished piece (or sends it to

    show a finished engraved piece, not a blank. Since 22 Oct 2026, every listing also needs at least
    one unedited photo of the real item on a real background. If you use renders, label them and
    keep real photos first.

    concept developed with AI-assisted tools; final design and CAD by our team, made by our US
    production partner."

 9. COMMON MISTAKES
  1. Quoting fees from old blog posts or from a non-US shop setup. This US shop pays 3% + $0.25
    processing, no Regulatory Operating Fee and no currency conversion; formulas carried over from
    a non-US setup (higher processing rate, ROF or 2.5% conversion) overstate fees.
  2. Leaving DDP, tariff or "duties prepaid" wording in listings and FAQs. It does not apply to
    domestic US orders and confuses US buyers.
  3. Believing Purchase Protection covers a $1,200 ring. It pays at most $250, and only one damage
    claim per calendar year.
  4. Shipping to an address the buyer sends in Messages instead of the Etsy order address. That
    breaks Purchase Protection eligibility.
  5. Shipping in branded outer packaging, which can void Shipsurance coverage.


Etsy Fine Jewelry Master Course | Module 1: Platform, Fees, Policies and Jewelry Compliance        Page 21
  6. Writing "gold," "white gold" (without rhodium disclosure), "lab diamond," "faux diamond" or
    "#diamond" on lab stones. These violate FTC Guides §§23.3, 23.7, 23.25.
  7. Stamping "14K" without a registered or applied-for US trademark (15 U.S.C. §297).
  8. Listing workshop-made pieces as "Made by a seller" without production-partner disclosure,
    hiding that the owner works from Türkiye in the About section, or buying finished catalogue rings
    from a wholesaler (OEM/ODM is reselling and can lead to suspension).
  9. Using CAD renders or AI images as the first or only photo for a made-to-order item, or having no
    unedited real photo in a listing after 22 Oct 2026.
  10. Letting Help with Order messages sit over 48 hours over a weekend, a Turkish public holiday or
    a US holiday.
  11. Listing processing times that are too short for made-to-order casting, which hurts on-time
    shipping (80% minimum, 95% for Star Seller).
  12. Agreeing to new delivery dates only in Messages. Etsy ignores informal agreements for
    Purchase Protection.
  13. Using brand names ("Cartier love style," "Tiffany setting," "Pandora compatible") in titles or
    tags. Trademark takedowns have no counter-notice route, and repeat notices can end the shop.
  14. Running permanent "50% off" strikethroughs. Etsy's January 2026 guidance calls this
    deceptive.
  15. Crossing $10k in trailing sales without noticing that Offsite Ads becomes mandatory at 12% for
    life.
  16. Marketing gold jewelry to children (baby rings, "for girls") without CPSIA certification.
  17. Taking orders or payments off Etsy (WhatsApp, bank transfer, QR code). This is now covered by
    its own Off-Platform Transactions policy and is "strictly prohibited."
  18. Sharing the owner login with the workshop, or logging in through changing VPN locations,
    instead of using team access and one consistent setup.
  19. Missing the Form 5472 plus pro forma 1120 filing for a foreign-owned US LLC ($25,000 penalty),
    or running past $500 in sales without a valid taxpayer ID on Etsy.
  20. Importing Turkish-made components "just this once" without budgeting 5.5% duty plus 12.5%
    Section 301 on the import value.
