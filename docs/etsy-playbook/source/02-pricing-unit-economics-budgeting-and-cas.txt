 1. The 2026 operating environment in one page
 Four facts define pricing in late 2026:
  1. Gold is near historic highs and volatile. Spot was about $4,052/oz on 22 Oct 2026, or
    $130.29/g for 24K, $97.72/g for 18K and $76.22/g for 14K [S1: 150currency.com, 2026-10-22].
    Gold set an all-time high of $5,589.38 on 28 Jan 2026, fell to about $4,400 on 2 Feb 2026, and
    was back above $5,200 on 23 Feb 2026 [S2: investingnews.com, 2026-02-25]. On 20 Oct 2026
    Fortune put spot at $4,120, down 4.54% from $4,316 a year earlier [S3: fortune.com,
    2026-10-20]. In practice that means a swing of more than $1,500/oz (about 37%) inside one year.
  2. The Etsy fee stack for a US shop is lean: about 9.5% plus $0.45 per order before ads.
    Transaction 6.5% plus US payment processing 3% + $0.25, plus the $0.20 listing renewal on
    each sale. No Regulatory Operating Fee and no currency conversion fee apply to a US seller
    listing in USD with a USD payment account [S4: etsy.com, last updated 2026-10-05; S5:
    etsy.com, last updated 2026-07-31; S6: iscompliant.app, 2026-06-22].
  3. Domestic shipping means no buyer-facing duty. Etsy's DDP rule, its tariff estimator and
    US-specific duty pricing apply to shops shipping into the US from abroad, not to domestic US
    shipments (module 09). Import duty matters only if the business imports components or finished
    pieces (section 7).
  4. Lab-grown diamond costs keep falling, but more slowly. Wholesale fell 26% in 2025, 14%
    YoY in Q1 2026 and 13% YoY in Q2 2026 [S7: jckonline.com, 2026-08-10]. Stone cost now
    matters far less than metal.
 Taken together: metal plus US bench labor now make up most of the price, and Etsy's
 percentage fees scale with gold, so a fixed 2x keystone on an old cost sheet is still the fastest
 way to lose money in 2026.

 2.2 Casting loss and finishing loss
 There is no published 2025 or 2026 US workshop loss rate for cast rings in the sources found. The
 best official 2025 benchmark comes from India's DGFT export norms, effective 1 Jan 2025: plain
 jewelry allows 2.25% wastage when handcrafted and 0.45% when mechanized, and studded jewelry
 allows 4.00% handcrafted and 2.80% mechanized [S9: taxguru.in, notice 2024-11-01, effective
 2025-01-01]. These are regulatory allowances, not workshop quotes.
 Action: ask the US workshop to state its metal loss allowance in writing as a percentage of the
 finished weight. The worksheet below uses a 5% metal loss allowance as a labelled
 assumption. That is deliberately above the DGFT handcrafted studded norm, so it is a conservative
 buffer.

 What this means for gold:
  • The base fee stack is about 9.5% plus $0.45 per order before ads: 6.5% + 3% of the item
    price, plus $0.25 + $0.20 fixed, plus 3% on any sales tax the buyer pays. Worked example from
    module 09: on a $400 ring with no tax, base fees are $38.45 (9.61%); with an illustrative 8% tax
    they are $39.41 (9.85%) [S4: etsy.com, module 09, citing , 2026-10-05 and [S5: etsy.com],
    2026-07-31]. Because the fees are percentages, they also apply to the gold portion of the price.
    When gold rises 10%, Etsy's take rises with it.
  • The Offsite Ads $100 cap works in your favour on high tickets. On a $1,500 ring, 15%
    would be $225 but the fee stops at $100, an effective 6.7%. On a $500 piece the full 15% ($75)
    applies. Fine jewelry over about $667 is the band where the cap starts to help at 15% (15% x
    $667 = $100), and over about $834 at 12%.
  • Shipping is not fee-free. Because transaction and processing fees include shipping, building
    shipping into the item price costs the same in fees as charging it separately. The difference is in
    search and conversion.


 4. Pricing methods for fine gold jewelry in 2026
 4.1 Cost-plus with metal pass-through (recommended core)
 Split the cost into a metal component that floats with gold and a fixed component (labor, stones,
 finishing, packaging, shipping). Mark up the fixed component at your design multiple, and pass the
 metal through with a smaller uplift that still covers percentage fees on it. This is how gold-heavy
 brands protect themselves when spot swings 30% or more.

 4.2 Keystone and multipliers: 2026 benchmarks

 A 2026 DTC jewelry analysis gives these figures [S21: eightx.co, 2026, citing mid-June 2026 spot of
 about $4,200/oz]:
  • Keystone (2.0x) gives a 50% gross margin and "under-recovers for gold fine jewelry in 2026."
  • Gold fine jewelry without stones: 3.5x to 4.0x (71% to 75% gross margin).
  • Stone-set or gemstone fashion: 2.2x to 2.5x.
  • DTC only: 3x to 5x, needed to fund customer acquisition.
  • Entry price points have moved up: the old roughly $99 gold entry piece is largely gone, with
    floors moving toward $200 to $500, and the mix is shifting toward 14K and 10K.
 A 2026 handmade jewelry guide gives 2.5x to 4x as the typical direct-sale multiple [S22:
 priceprofittools.com, 2026-09].
 Warning for this business: applying 3.5x to 4x to the whole cost of a solid gold ring, metal
 included, will price you out of Etsy search. The worked examples in section 6 show that about
 1.55x (lean, 15% net) to 2.06x (full marketing, 20% net) on fixed landed cost F is what the
 margin targets actually require once fees are counted correctly. Anything above that is design
 premium, and it has to come from the fixed component, not from marking up gold 4x. These
 multiples depend on the placeholder US labor figure (section 2.3); recompute once you have the
 real quote.

 4.3 Margin-aware formula (the one to use)
 Same 2026 source: retail = (cost + ops) / (1 minus target gross margin), then multiplied by a
 positioning factor of 1.0 to 1.2 (budget), 1.2 to 1.5 (mid), 1.5 to 2.0 (premium) or 2.0+
 (luxury-adjacent). It recommends pricing to operating margin, not gross margin [S21: eightx.co,
 2026]. The Etsy version in section 6 extends this so that every percentage-of-price cost sits in the
 denominator.

 4.4 Value-based pricing and anchoring
  • Price on what buyers compare against: US mall jewelers and DTC brands for the same karat and
    weight, not other Etsy sellers using plated metal.
  • Anchor with the 18K variant. Show 14K as the default and 18K as the visible step up. The 18K
    price makes 14K look reasonable.
  • Truthfulness rule: a strikethrough "original" price has to be a price you actually charged for a
    substantial period and sold at in reasonable quantities. Briefly inflating a price before a sale is
    prohibited [S23: etsy.com, 2026-01-12]. Anchoring has to come from real tiers, not from fake
    was-prices.

 4.5 Good, better, best
 Use Etsy variations, which allow up to three since Sep 2026 via custom options [S24:
 growingyourcraft.com, 2026-10-08]:
  • Variation 1: Karat (14K yellow, 14K white, 14K rose, 18K yellow and so on), each with its own
    price.
  • Variation 2: Size (price-neutral up to a threshold, then a surcharge for large sizes that use more
    metal).
  • Variation 3: Width or stone option (plain, pavé, LGD solitaire).
 Per-variation pricing lets you reprice one karat line without touching the listing's search history.
 Listing URLs stay stable after first publication [growingyourcraft, 2026-10-08].

 4.6 Dynamic pricing tied to gold
 The 2026 guidance is to treat metal as a variable input and set a repricing trigger. One example
 re-costs gold SKUs when spot moves more than 7% from the last costing, and says 5% to 10% is the
 common range. It also advises repricing in small, regular steps rather than rare large jumps
 [S21: eightx.co, 2026]. Another 2026 guide says to reprice when material costs rise more than
 about 8% to 10% [S22: priceprofittools.com, 2026-09].
 Recommended rule for this shop:
  • Band rule: record the "costing spot" (for example $4,050/oz). Reprice every gold variation when
    the 5-day average spot moves more than plus or minus 7% from it.
  • Asymmetry: reprice upward promptly. Reprice downward more slowly (for example after 10
    trading days below the band), and use the gap to fund promotions.
  • Never cut a price just before a sale. Etsy treats price-history manipulation as a violation
    (section 8).
  • Order acceptance: made-to-order means you pay for gold days after the buyer pays you. Have
    the US workshop buy or lock the metal within 24 to 48 hours of the order (see section 10).

 4.7 Psychological price points
 Charm pricing ($49 instead of $50) is described as a small, low-effort lever, and pricing well below
 peers can read as cheap rather than as a bargain [S22: priceprofittools.com, 2026-09]. For fine
 jewelry above $500, round-ish prices ending in 5 ($595, $895, $1,195, $1,495) are the common
 convention. That is an observation, not a sourced rule. Test it (section 12).


 5. Free shipping logic
  • Etsy's US free shipping guarantee applies to US orders of $35 or more. Listings that qualify get
    higher search visibility [S25: outfy.com, updated 2026-07-31]. Every solid gold item is above $35,
    so every listing should show free shipping to the US.
  • US domestic listings charging more than $6 for shipping may see reduced visibility. Etsy also
    offered a bulk tool to move shipping cost into item price (October 2024, reconfirmed in 2026)
    [S24: growingyourcraft.com, 2026-10-08]. As a US shop this rule now applies to you directly, and
    free shipping satisfies it (the original rule dates from 2024 and is UNVERIFIED (pre-2025) for its
    current search weight, per module 09).
  • Build in: carrier label cost, signature service, Shipsurance insurance, and the Etsy fees on all of
    these, since fees are charged on the total.
  • Free shipping to US buyers combined with "Support makers near you" and ship-from filters (Sep
    2026) means some buyers will filter by location [growingyourcraft, 2026-10-08]. A US ships-from
    address now qualifies the shop for these filters, while listings must still disclose design or
    production location truthfully (module 09).


 6. PRICING FORMULA WORKSHEET
 6.1 The master formula (net-margin target)
 Let:
  • F = fixed landed cost in USD: metal (including loss) + US bench labor + finishing + stones +
    packaging + certificate + shipping label + signature + insurance rounding + per-order Etsy fixed
    fees ($0.25 processing + $0.20 listing renewal) + an allocated remake or resize reserve + misc



Etsy Fine Jewelry Master Course | Module 2: Pricing, Unit Economics, Budgeting and Cash Flow       Page 31
  • v = the sum of all costs charged as a percentage of selling price: transaction (6.5%) + processing
    (3%) + processing on sales tax (3% x assumed tax rate) + Shipsurance (0.8%) + blended Offsite
    Ads share + Etsy Ads share + discount allowance + returns allowance
  • m = target net contribution margin after all of the above
 Then:
 Price P = F / (1 minus v minus m)
 Break-even price (m = 0): P_BE = F / (1 minus v)
 Check: Net contribution = P x (1 minus v) minus F. Margin % = net contribution / P.
 Notes:
  • Sales tax assumption: Etsy collects and remits sales tax as marketplace facilitator, but the 3%
    processing fee is charged on the gross including tax [S5: etsy.com, 2026-07-31]. The worksheet
    uses an illustrative 8% buyer sales tax (assumption, not a sourced rate), so processing
    on tax = 3% x 8% = 0.24% of price.
  • Insurance: Shipsurance is $0.80 per $100 or fraction [Etsy insurance help page, read
    2026-10-22]. Modelled as 0.8% of price in v plus $0.80 in F to cover the rounding up to the next
    $100.
  • Shipping label: the worksheet uses the worst-case USPS Priority Mail 1 lb zone 8 commercial
    rate, $16.07, plus online Signature Confirmation $4.15. Orders of $1,000+ under the house rule
    use Adult Signature ($9.70) instead; add $5.55 to F for those.
  • The Offsite Ads $100 cap means the real percentage falls as price rises. The worksheet uses a
    blended allowance, not 15% on every order.
  • There is no import duty in v for US-made pieces from US-sourced metal. If components are
    imported, add the duty on their import value to F (section 7), not to v.

 6.5 Gold sensitivity (EXAMPLE CALCULATION)
 Holding the lean v and m (denominator 0.6446) and moving gold plus or minus 10%:
  • 14K band: metal plus metal-linked labor = $288.11, so a 10% move = $28.81. Delta P = 28.81 /
    0.6446 = $44.70, which is 8.0% of $557.83.
  • 18K band: metal plus labor = $492.49, so a 10% move = $49.25. Delta P = 49.25 / 0.6446 =
    $76.40, which is 8.7% of $874.90.
 Rule of thumb from these numbers: for a solid gold band, every 10% move in spot changes
 the required price by roughly 8% to 9%, because metal plus metal-linked labor is about 80% to
 87% of F once the large DDP and international shipping lines are gone. That is why the 7% repricing
 band in 4.6 is sensible. If your real US labor quote is a fixed dollar amount, sensitivity falls slightly.
 Quick sensitivity formula: delta P = (delta metal cost x 1.2 if labor is tied to metal, or x 1.0 if labor is
 fixed) / (1 minus v minus m).

 6.6 What the worksheet teaches
  1. Metal is now the dominant line. With no duty and a 9.5% Etsy stack, gold plus labor is about
    80% to 87% of F, so the gold repricing band is the main margin control.
  2. The discount allowance has to sit inside v. If you plan to run 25% Cyber Specials, either the
    list price already carries it or you will lose money on gold pieces (see section 8.4).
  3. Labor tied to a percentage of metal inflates cost when gold rises. Negotiate US bench
    labor per piece in USD, not as a percentage of gold value.
  4. The placeholder labor line moves every result. Replace it with the US workshop quote
    before setting live prices.
  5. In Amuletta, set each product's cost template with metal grams and karat as variables pulled
    from a daily gold price, so per-order margin reflects the gold price on the purchase date.

 8. Discount architecture
 8.1 Etsy's discount tools (2026)
  • Promo codes: percentage-off codes have to be whole numbers from 5% to 75%. Fixed-amount
    and free standard shipping codes also exist. Codes can have a minimum order and, since June
    2026, a usage limit [S30: help.etsy.com, 2026; growingyourcraft, 2026-10-08].
  • Sales: percentage off or free standard shipping. Listing-specific sales cannot have a minimum
    order. Shop-wide sales with a minimum cannot exclude items [Etsy Help 115014260108, 2026].
  • Bundles: up to 3 listings, and they do not stack with other promotions [Etsy Help
    115014260108, 2026].
  • Targeted offers: abandoned cart (sent after an item has been in a cart for more than 2 hours,
    at most once every two days, up to 12 discounts per email, offer expires after 60 days), favorited
    items (same cadence and expiry), and post-purchase thank-you (coupon valid for 1 year).
    Shoppers who opted out of marketing emails do not receive them [Etsy Help 115014260108,
    2026]. Note that the cart trigger is now 2 hours. Older help text said 24 hours [S31:
    valueaddedresource.net, UNVERIFIED (pre-2025): , 2023-05-08].
  • Interested shopper offers (June 2026): sent automatically to shoppers Etsy judges interested
    based on view frequency and recency. Options are 5% to 70% in 5-point steps, a fixed amount, or
    free shipping, with an optional minimum. Each buyer receives at most one every 30 days, and
    offers expire 7 days after sending. Sellers do not choose recipients [S32: mikellane.com,
    2026-09-16; growingyourcraft, 2026-10-08].
  • Stacking: without an active free shipping promotion, a shopper cannot apply more than one
    discount to an item [Etsy Help 115014260108, 2026].
  • Fees on discounted orders: the 6.5% transaction fee applies to the displayed price, including
    the sale price [S33: litcommerce.com, 2026-10-20].

 8.2 Truthful discount rules (Etsy Seller Handbook, 12 Jan 2026)
 From "Understanding Truthful Advertising and Discount Practices" [S23: etsy.com, 2026-01-12]:
  • A sale has to be a temporary reduction from a genuine regular price that you actually sold at for
    a reasonable period.
  • If an item is discounted most of the time, the discounted price may legally be treated as its true
    price.

  • A strikethrough price has to be the usual price, offered for a substantial period and sold in
    reasonable quantities. No brief inflation before a discount.
  • "Up to X%" claims require a meaningful number of items to actually be at or near X%.
  • Do not manipulate price history, discounts or sale timing to trigger Etsy signals such as
    countdown timers, low-inventory flags or strikethrough displays.
  • Do not call gold-plated items "gold jewelry". This is a positioning advantage for solid gold sellers,

 8.3 Why permanent fake sales hurt
  1. Legal and policy risk (above). The "true price" becomes the sale price.
  2. Etsy may withhold the strikethrough or sale signals it treats as manipulated.
  3. On gold, a permanent 30% off means list price has to carry a 30% hidden uplift, and that uplift is
    also charged percentage fees. It is cost-inefficient.
  4. It trains repeat buyers to wait for codes.

    cheapest acquisition you will get.
  • Abandoned cart and favorited: 10% maximum on gold. With a 2-hour trigger, buyers can
    learn to abandon carts to get a code. Consider a fixed-amount offer instead.

  • Interested shopper offer: 10% with a minimum order. It reaches warm traffic at most once

  • Use fixed-dollar amounts on high tickets. $75 off a $1,195 ring (6.3%) reads well and caps

 8.6 Designing a discount budget that protects gold margin
  1. Set an annual discount allowance as % of gross sales (for example 5% to 8%) and put it into
    v in the pricing formula.
  2. Allocate it: Cyber Specials and Q4 about 50%, targeted offers about 30%, thank-you and repeat
    buyers about 20%.
  3. Discount the fixed part, never the gold part. Calculate the maximum discount per SKU as
    (P x (1 minus v_without_discount) minus F minus minimum acceptable profit) / P, and never go
    above it. Example A at $695 list, v_without_discount = 0.1554, minimum profit $50: (695 x
    0.8446 minus 359.58 minus 50) / 695 = 25.5% maximum discount.
  4. Before any event, export the SKU list with current F (from Amuletta at today's gold price) and
    calculate each SKU's maximum safe discount.
  5. After each event, compare Alura revenue against Amuletta per-order margin. Discount ROI =
    incremental contribution / discount cost.

 10. Cash flow: where gold shops go broke
  1. Made-to-order gold prepay. You collect the buyer's payment, but Etsy deposits come later
    and some may be held in reserve, while the US workshop needs metal paid now. Gold may also
    move between the order date and the purchase date. Mitigation: have the workshop buy or lock
    metal within 48 hours, keep a gold float equal to about 2 weeks of metal cost, and agree in
    writing which day's spot sets the metal price.
  2. Etsy payment reserves. Etsy can apply a fixed reserve (a percentage of sales held on a rolling
    basis) or a variable reserve (delay up to 180 days or deduct for up to 90 days) [S5: etsy.com,
    2026-07-31], and may apply a reserve to new sellers with no track record [S42: help.etsy.com,
    read 2026-10-22]. Triggers described by Alura include a rapid rise in order volume, shipments
    without tracking, shipping delays, more refund requests, and a first sale [S43: alura.io, undated;
    it cites 2022 data, so treat as UNVERIFIED (pre-2025) for the timing figures]. A Q4 spike in
    volume is itself a reserve trigger. Buy Etsy labels so tracking uploads automatically, and ship
    the same day the piece is finished.
  3. Deposit schedule: daily (needs a $25.00 balance), weekly, biweekly or monthly; weekly,
    biweekly and monthly pay on Mondays and deposits generally post within five business days [S5:
    etsy.com, 2026-07-31]. New sellers default to weekly on Mondays [S42: help.etsy.com, read
    2026-10-22]. Switch to daily once the shop is established.
  4. Faster payouts: Instant Transfers are available to this US shop: 1% fee (minimum $0.25,
    maximum $1.00), $25 minimum, $500 maximum per transfer, one per 24 hours, usually within
    about 30 minutes [S42: help.etsy.com, read 2026-10-22]. Use them to fund metal purchases in
    Q4 instead of carrying a larger float.
  5. Payment account visibility: since September 2026 current, pending and total balances are
    shown separately, with fees grouped by sale [growingyourcraft, 2026-10-08]. Reconcile weekly in
    Alura.
  6. No duty prepayment on domestic orders. The old risk of prepaying duty to a carrier before
    Etsy funds cleared is gone. If you import components, duty is paid at import on the workshop
    invoice value and belongs in the gold float plan.
  7. Returns and remakes on custom sizes: a resized or remade gold ring loses labor and loss
    allowance again. Budget for it.
  8. Purchase Protection: Etsy may refund buyers up to $250; the seller pays any refund amount
    above $250, and since 7 May 2026 eligible orders above $250 still get up to $250 of coverage
    [S44: etsy.com, effective 2026-07-09; S45: etsy.com, 2026-04-07]. No DDP condition applies to
    domestic orders. A $1,500 ring claim goes far beyond that cap, so tracked, insured, signature
    shipping is essential, and Shipsurance claims must be filed before cancelling the order.
  9. Currency: sales, fees, deposits, workshop and shipping are all in USD, so there is no Etsy
    currency conversion fee [S4: etsy.com, 2026-10-05]. The only FX exposure is the owner's own

 12. Price testing on Etsy
 Etsy has no native A/B price testing (no source found offering one). These are practical methods:
  1. Sequential testing with stable conditions. Change the price on one variation or listing for
    14 to 28 days, keep photos, title and ads unchanged, and compare conversion rate and
    contribution per visit (not revenue). Avoid holiday weeks.
  2. Karat-ladder test. Move only the 18K variation price and watch the 14K/18K mix. If the 18K
    share stays steady after an increase, the price was too low.
  3. Twin listings (different design angle and photography, same product) at different price points.
    These have to be genuinely different listings to avoid duplicate-listing problems.
  4. Measure on contribution per visit: (orders x average net contribution) / visits. A higher price
    with lower conversion can still win.
  5. Use Etsy Marketplace Insights price ranges (in testing as of 2026) to see where demand
    clusters [growingyourcraft, 2026-10-08].
  6. Never test by "raising then discounting". That is the manipulation pattern Etsy prohibits
    [Etsy Seller Handbook, 2026-01-12].
  7. Gold drift confound: record spot at the start and end of each test. If gold moved more than
    5%, discard or adjust the test.

 14. COMMON MISTAKES
  1. Keystone on a stale gold cost. One January 2026 peak to February dip moved gold by more
    than $1,100/oz in a week [investingnews, 2026-02-25].
  2. Copying a non-US fee stack into the formula. Regulatory Operating Fee, currency
    conversion and Turkish processing rates do not apply to this US shop; using them overprices
    every SKU.
  3. Adding DDP duty or "duties paid" copy to domestic listings. Domestic US shipments carry
    no buyer duty; that copy belongs only to cross-border shops (module 09).
  4. Ignoring that Etsy's percentage fees include shipping and processing applies to sales
    tax, and that fees scale with gold.
  5. Permanent "sales" and inflated strikethrough prices, which are prohibited [Etsy Seller
    Handbook, 2026-01-12].
  6. Joining 25% events on SKUs priced at the floor. Example A at $495 list minus 25% loses
    $46.02; Example B at $895 minus 25% only breaks even.
  7. Stacking targeted offers with sales without modelling it. Promo codes go up to 75% and
    interested shopper offers up to 70%.
  8. Labor as a percentage of gold, which lets your cost rise automatically with every rally.
  9. Not buying metal immediately after a made-to-order sale.
  10. Ignoring reserves in Q4, when volume spikes are a reserve trigger.
  11. Pricing LGD stones by carat value. Buyers know stone prices are collapsing [JCK,
    2026-08-10].
  12. Branded outer packaging, which voids Shipsurance coverage on the parcel [S16:
    shipsurance.com, read 2026-10-22].
