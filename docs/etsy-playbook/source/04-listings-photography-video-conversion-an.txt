 1. The single biggest change this week: Etsy's Listing Image
 Requirements (updated 22 Oct 2026)
 This was updated on the research date itself and changes how a jewelry shop must build its photo
 set.
  • "For most products, you must include at least one unedited photo of the actual item against a
    real background." [S1: etsy.com, last updated 2026-10-22]
  • "Do not use photo editing software or AI tools to alter this required photo." [same, 2026-10-22]
  • The policy does not say which slot the unedited photo must occupy. It requires "at least one."
    [same, 2026-10-22]
  • Images must accurately show the item's color, condition, scale and materials. Misleading edits
    that hide flaws or alter appearance are prohibited. [same, 2026-10-22]
  • Customized and made-to-order items: "use photos of real, finished items you've made in the
    past." [same, 2026-10-22]
  • AI and editing tools are allowed on the other images as long as the final image accurately
    represents the physical product. Allowed: adjusting lighting, exposure or color to match real life;
    cropping, resizing, straightening; cleaning up or replacing the background if the product is still
    accurately shown. Prohibited: changing actual color, texture or condition; removing or hiding
    defects; "Using AI to change the core design or features of a physical item." [same, 2026-10-22]
  • Consequences: if the item received is materially different from the image and the buyer opens a
    case, Etsy may refund the buyer and "you may be responsible for the refund amount." Etsy may
    remove misleading listings, and repeated violations can lead "up to and including shop removal."
    [same, 2026-10-22]
  • Etsy's seller email of the same day adds: all photos must "accurately represent what a buyer will
    receive"; Etsy will give guidance when sellers add photos; it is investing in tools to detect
    misleading images; and a new member reporting option lets people flag listings they believe use

  • Seller Policy: you must "Accurately represent your items," including "country of origin,
    provenance, attributes, components or materials," and give "An accurate representation of how
    the item was made, by whom, and where it is being shipped from." [S3: etsy.com, 2026-10-05]
  • Seller Policy: AI-created items and production partners must be disclosed in relevant listings. [S3:
    etsy.com, 2026-10-05]
  • Creativity Standards: "Sellers must disclose within their listing description if an item is created
    with the use of AI." Made-by-seller items require "original photo or video content of the final
    product." For personalized or custom items such as engraved goods, the first image must show a
    finished customized item, not a blank or placeholder text. [S4: etsy.com, last updated
    2025-06-10]
  • Personalization help page: for personalized commercial items such as engraved or
    monogrammed products, "the primary image must show a finished, personalized item similar to
    the final product." Blank product images or mockups with placeholder text cannot be the main
    image. [S5: help.etsy.com, undated, retrieved 2026-10-22]
 Practical reading for jewelry: if a CAD render or AI-generated design concept is used to sell a ring
 that has not been made yet, it fails both "real, finished items you've made in the past" and the
 Seller Policy ban on "artistic renderings." If AI helped design the piece itself, disclose it in the
 description. Third-party commentary (not Etsy policy) suggests disclosing AI lifestyle scenes even
 where not strictly required. [S6: listadum.com, 2026-05-11]

 4. What the first photo must do
  • It is the search thumbnail: "The first photo on your listing is the thumbnail shoppers will see."
    [S14: etsy.com, 2025-08]
  • Avoid text overlays, collages or image collections as the first thumbnail. [S7: etsy.com,
    2025-08-26]
  • Search considers the first photo as part of its overall view of a listing; the AI title tool also reads
    the first photo. [S15: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]; [S16: etsy.com, 2026-04-27]
  • For an engraved item, the first photo must show a finished personalized piece. [S5:
    help.etsy.com, retrieved 2026-10-22]
 Job of the hero for a fine gold ring: in a 200 px tile, identify the object (ring), the metal color, the
 defining feature (engraving, enamel, profile) and the quality tier in under one second. That means:
 single ring, filling about 60 percent of the frame, three-quarter angle showing both the top and the
 band profile, engraving legible or at least visible, clean neutral or warm surface, no props
 competing.


 5. PHOTO SHOT LIST FOR GOLD RINGS
 Etsy's general guidance: multiple angles, close-ups of details and textures, lifestyle or worn images,
 natural light without flash, simple neutral backgrounds. [S7: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]
 Etsy jewelry case study (Anueva Jewelry): shoot near a window around 1 to 2 pm rather than under
 flat artificial light; move the piece if a reflection appears in a stone; avoid direct sun (except opal);
 bounce light with white poster board; avoid busy woven backgrounds; plain board for sparkly or
 milled pieces, woodgrain for natural styles; macro lens on a tripod because "even your breathing
 can blur the image"; burst shooting if handheld; manual mode, at least f/4.5 for close-ups; around
 f/5.6 for on-hand shots. [S17: etsy.com, page dated 2026-09-29]
 Video guidance for jewelry from Etsy's merchandising team is to show items on a model for scale
 and movement, and for custom items to show the personalization process. That page is from 2024
 (see Unverified section).

 Lighting and color for gold (technique guidance; no 2025 to 2026 primary source found beyond
 Etsy's case study):
  • Large diffused source (window with diffusion or a softbox) plus white and black cards. Gold
    reflects its surroundings; the black card creates contrast lines that read as "polished metal."
  • Lock white balance (custom white balance off a gray card). Auto white balance turns yellow gold
    green or orange as the frame changes.
  • Do not oversaturate yellow gold in editing. That is precisely the "changing the product's actual
    color" Etsy prohibits. [S1: etsy.com, 2026-10-22]
  • Shoot all variants in one session with identical settings so metal colors are comparable across the
    set.


 6. Listing anatomy
 6.1 Title
 Etsy guidance as of April 2026 [S16: etsy.com, 2026-04-27]:
  • Name the item clearly, using the noun once.
  • Put the top three descriptors for the category first (usually color, material, size).
  • Fewer than 15 words.



Etsy Fine Jewelry Master Course | Module 4: Listings, Photography, Video, Conversion and Revi                        Page 70
  • Occasions or recipients only when essential.
  • Move subjective words ("perfect," "beautiful") to the description.
  • No repeated words; no price, shipping or sale wording.
  • An optional AI title tool suggests titles from the current title, first photo and description, English
    listings only, in the search visibility dashboard. Etsy recommends downloading a CSV of listings
    before changing titles and reverting if needed.
 Character limit: 140 characters (third party). [S18: outfy.com, 2026-07-22]
 Example (EON): "14K Solid Gold Engraved Signet Ring, Custom Initial Ring, Personalized Heirloom
 Ring" (12 words). Example (Artifact Studio): "18K Gold Enamel Stacking Ring, Green Enamel Band,
 Handmade Fine Jewelry".

 6.2 Tags and attributes
  • 13 tags, multi-word natural phrases, vary across what it is, how it's made, who it's for. If an
    attribute already covers a phrase, no need to repeat it as a tag. [S7: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]
  • Tags up to 20 characters each (third party). [S18: outfy.com, 2026-07-22]
  • Fill material, primary and secondary color, occasion, and any category-specific attributes
    unlocked by choosing a precise category. [S7: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]

 6.3 Description
 Etsy guidance: put essential info (sizes, dimensions, colors, ordering directions) at the top; keywords
 naturally in the first few sentences; do not copy the title or list keywords; include dimensions,
 materials, care and customization; tell the story; use bullets or short paragraphs. [S7: etsy.com,
 2025-08-26]
 Length: about 10,000 characters, with only the first 160 reliably shown in previews (third party).
 [S18: outfy.com, 2026-07-22]
 What a US buyer of fine gold needs to see (derived from Etsy's materials accuracy rule and US
 marking law context):
  • Karat and alloy, stated precisely. Seller Policy requires accurate materials and country of origin.
    [S3: etsy.com, 2026-10-05]
  • AI disclosure if AI was used to create the design. [S4: etsy.com, 2025-06-10]
  • Weight in grams (approximate, per size), band width, thickness, top dimensions.
  • Stone type, natural or lab, size, color and clarity grade if relevant.
  • Making time and the ship-by logic.
  • Sizing, resizing, returns on personalized items, warranty, care.

 6.5 Variations
  • Up to 2 variation properties per listing; third-party source states a maximum of 70 combinations
    and recommends 4 to 12 options. [S19: listifyai.net, 2026-03-27] (Etsy's own variations help page
    failed to load in this session.)
  • Processing time can be set per variation. [S20: help.etsy.com, undated, retrieved 2026-10-22]
  • Common structure for gold rings: Property 1 = Metal (14K Yellow, 14K White, 14K Rose, 18K
    Yellow...), Property 2 = Ring size. Price varies by Property 1 (karat and color), and, if weight
    genuinely rises with size, by size too.
  • Karat x color x size will overflow two properties. Solutions: combine karat and color into one
    property ("14K Yellow Gold"), put size in the second, and move engraving options into
    personalization fields.

 6.6 Personalization field
 Standard (all sellers) per Etsy help [S5: help.etsy.com, retrieved 2026-10-22]:
  • Field types: Text box, List of options, File upload.
  • Text box character limit: 1 to 1,024 characters.
  • Up to 5 custom option fields per listing.
  • List of options: up to 30 choices, 10 or fewer recommended.
  • Add-on prices from $0.20 to $500 equivalent, available only on optional text box fields.
  • File upload: one field per listing, up to 10 files, 100 MB per file, .jpg .png .svg .heic .pdf. Useful for
    handwriting or fingerprint engraving.
  • Each field has a "This field is required" checkbox.
  • Instructions: explain how and where the personalization appears, with an example.

  1. List of options, required: "Engraving position" (Face / Inside band / Both).
  2. Text box, required, limit sized to the physical space (for example 3): "Face initials. Type exactly

 Set the character limit to the physical maximum. If the buyer cannot type more than fits, you
 prevent the most common proof dispute.

 6.7 Processing time, estimated delivery, shipping
 From Etsy help [S20: help.etsy.com, retrieved 2026-10-22]:
  • Each physical listing gets a processing profile: processing time plus a made-to-order or
    ready-to-ship label (label not shown to buyers). Can differ by variation.
  • Made-to-order processing time includes the time to make the item.
  • Processing starts the day after purchase; ship-by is the end of the longest processing time in the
    order, in the shop's time zone, shipped by 11:59 pm.
  • Choose Monday to Friday or include weekends; US and Canada can exclude holidays. As a US
    shop, exclude the US holidays your partner workshop observes; the owner's Turkish public
    holidays do not affect the processing schedule.
  • Processing time also controls when a buyer can open a case and leave a review.
  • If no processing time is set, buyers see no ship-by date.
  • "Actual processing times" are calculated from the past 120 days with at least 5 shipped orders;
    buyers may see shorter ranges if you are faster than stated.
  • A ship-by date can be extended once, before it passes, by up to 21 days, after confirming with
    the buyer.
 From Etsy search guidance: set realistic processing times. [S7: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]
 Seller Policy for US-bound orders: must use a Delivery Duty Paid (DDP) method and must not use
 methods that make buyers pay extra fees after purchase. [S3: etsy.com, 2026-10-05] This applies to
 international shipments into the US; it does not apply to this US shop, whose orders ship
 domestically with no customs step.
 Etsy says buyers report expensive shipping as a top-10 barrier and that moving some shipping cost
 into item price while keeping the total the same can improve sales. [S7: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]
 Shipping price is a ranking factor for US sellers. [S15: etsy.com, 2025-08-26] The specific "under $6"
 rule applies to US domestic listings, so it applies to this shop: keep visible shipping under $6 or free
 (see Unverified section for its 2024 source).

  • Listings should have a return policy; missing it affects listing quality. [S15: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]
  • Clear shop policies help search placement; Etsy's shop policy template "can give an extra boost."
    [S14: etsy.com, 2025-08]
  • Honor what your policies say; cancellations after payment require a full refund; you have 48
    hours to resolve a problem once a buyer contacts you. [S3: etsy.com, 2026-10-05]
  • EU/UK buyers may have a 14-day withdrawal right which "may not apply to certain items like
    custom items." [same]
  • About section must accurately describe shop members involved in making and running the
    business. [same] For EON, name the US partner workshop (city, state) and its makers, and
    disclose it as a production partner.
  • Shop icon is a listing-quality and trust signal. [S15: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]
  • Limits (third party): shop title 55 characters, announcement 5,000 characters, up to 20 custom
    sections at 24 characters each. [S18: outfy.com, 2026-07-22]
 Shop page build for fine jewelry:
  • Banner: one real photograph, ring on hand, no text clutter. Use 1600 x 400.
  • Announcement: first two lines carry current making time, free tracked US shipping note, and
    proof-before-engraving promise.
  • Sections: by intent and product type ("Signet Rings," "Engraved Bands," "Stacking Rings,"
    "Enamel," "Ring Sizer," "Couples Sets").
  • Featured listings: the 4 best converters, not the newest.
  • About: workshop photos, makers' names, video if available, hallmarking, materials sourcing.
  • FAQ: karat and purity, hallmarks, sizing and resizing, proof workflow, US delivery times and
    insurance, warranty, care, gift options.

 6.9 Gift options
  • Gift messages: Settings > Options > Offer gift messages; message prints on the packing slip.
    [S22: help.etsy.com, retrieved 2026-10-22]
  • Gift wrapping: per-order fee you set, cannot be 0; optional wrap photo and note. [same]
  • Buyers can always mark an order as a gift; this cannot be turned off. The invoice then omits the

 7. Conversion rate
 7.1 What Etsy says it measures
 Etsy search looks at "how well a listing converts, how many people view it and then make a
 purchase," plus listing quality (multiple high-quality photos, return policy), relevancy, shop quality,
 average review rating, message response rate and case rate over the past three months, shipping
 price (US sellers), recency, and personalized ranking. [S15: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]
 Conversion is therefore both an outcome and an input to visibility: a listing that converts better is
 shown more.

 made-to-order band (1 to 2 percent), and the shop's own trailing 90-day numbers matter more than
 any blog figure.

 7.3 The funnel
 Impressions (search, ads, offsite) > click-through to listing (CTR) > listing visit > favorite or add to
 cart > checkout > purchase > review > repeat.
 Diagnose at each step in Shop Manager Stats: views, visits, favorites, orders, conversion rate, traffic
 sources and search terms per listing.

 7.5 How specific levers affect conversion (with evidence level)
  • Delivery speed: buyers see the estimated delivery range on each listing; Etsy's research says
    buyers are more likely to buy items that ship faster, "even if it's only by one day." [Seller
    Handbook, 2024-07-09, see Unverified]. The 2025 to 2026 processing-time page confirms Etsy
    uses actual processing times to show buyers faster ranges where earned. [S20: help.etsy.com,
    retrieved 2026-10-22]
  • Shipping price: top-10 barrier per Etsy buyer research; fold into price. [S7: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]
  • Reviews: average rating is a search input; recency-weighted (past 12 months carry most weight,
    each rating's weight halves yearly). [S24: help.etsy.com, retrieved 2026-10-22]
  • Photos: listing quality factor, first photo read by search. [S15: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]
  • Price: Etsy advises refining price "through experimentation and market research." [S7: etsy.com,
    2025-08-26]

 7.6 Abandoned cart and favorite offers
 Third-party description of Etsy's automatic offers (undated page): abandoned-cart offer emailed 24
 hours after a shopper leaves an item in cart over 24 hours; favorited-item offer 48 hours after
 favoriting; thank-you offer post-purchase; only to shoppers opted into marketing; set up under Shop
 Manager > Marketing > Sales and Discounts > "Automatically send offers to interested shoppers."
 The page also says sending unsolicited discounts when a shopper favorites or buys is against Etsy
 guidelines. [S25: alura.io, undated]
 For high-ticket rings, a 10 percent abandoned-cart offer on a $900 ring is $90; test whether it lifts
 profit, not just orders.

 7.7 A/B testing
 Etsy: no native listing A/B tool was found in 2025 to 2026 Etsy sources. Etsy's April 2026 title article
 suggests downloading a CSV before title changes and reverting if needed, which is sequential
 testing, not split testing. [S16: etsy.com, 2026-04-27]
 Alura A/B Tests [S26: alura.io, undated; S27: alura.io, 2026-06-30]:
  • Swaps the live listing between version A and version B "every 8 hours."
  • Testable: title, description, price, tags, images, video. One test per listing at a time; Growth plan
    allows up to 4 tests at once.
  • Measures orders and revenue per version, sales lift, active hours per version; you pick sales or
    revenue as the judging metric. Alura recommends revenue for price tests.
  • Auto-end "after a week or more depending on how much the listing sells," or stop manually
    ("Stop test and choose winner").
  • June 2026 rebuild added a Statistical Confidence card (High, Medium, Low), activity timeline,
    CSV/PDF export.
 Caveats for a low-volume, high-ticket shop: with a few orders a month, a week-long test will almost
 always read "Low" confidence. Rules for EON and Artifact Studio:
  • Test only on the top 3 to 5 listings by visits.
  • Test one variable, highest-leverage first: hero photo, then price, then title.

 8. Reviews
 8.1 Mechanics (Etsy help)
  • 100-day review window from delivery date (tracked) or estimated delivery date. [S24:
    help.etsy.com, retrieved 2026-10-22]
  • Score is a recency-weighted average; last 12 months weigh most; each rating's weight halves
    each year. [same]
  • Buyer photos and videos in reviews are visible to anyone viewing reviews. [same]
  • Reviews can be downloaded as JSON from Settings > Options > Download data. [same]
  • Processing time also controls when a buyer can leave a review. [S20: help.etsy.com, retrieved
    2026-10-22]

 8.2 Rules on earning reviews
  • Seller Policy: reviews and responses may not "Undermine the integrity of the Reviews system" or
    "Include shilling or otherwise falsely inflate a shop's review score." [S3: etsy.com, 2026-10-05]
  • Third-party summary: asking once is acceptable; offering discounts, freebies, upgrades or
    refunds tied to leaving a review, a specific rating, or removing a bad review breaks the rules; the
    US FTC Consumer Reviews rule (effective late 2024) bans fake and incentivized reviews. [S28:
    listingview.io, 2026-07-27]
 Ethical review system for fine jewelry:
  1. Delight first: proof, on-time ship, beautiful unboxing, care card.
  2. One message only, timed a few days after tracked delivery: thank them, ask if the fit is right
    (catches resize issues before they become 3-star reviews), and mention that a review with a
    photo helps a small workshop. No discount, no conditional anything.
  3. A printed card in the box that says the same thing. No QR to a coupon.

 8.3 Responding and negative reviews (Etsy help)
 [S29: help.etsy.com, retrieved 2026-10-22]
  • Try private resolution first via the order's message icon; quick help may lead the buyer to edit.
  • Buyers can revise a review for up to 100 days after estimated delivery, but only if you have not
    responded. Once you respond publicly, the buyer can no longer edit the review, even if you
    delete the response. This is the single most important tactical rule: resolve privately before any
    public reply.
  • You can respond once per review; you can delete but not edit; buyers cannot reply.
  • Keep it short, professional, specific, product-focused; no tracking numbers, links or private info.
  • Etsy removes reviews only for policy violations (private info, harassment, spam, solely about
    carrier or Etsy, threats or extortion, shilling). Negative but compliant reviews stay; Etsy does not
    judge accuracy of quality complaints, and will not remove "not received" claims even with
    tracking.

 the ring arrived later than you hoped. We've since shortened our making time and now ship every

 8.4 Star Seller
 Monthly evaluation over 3 months: 95 percent of initial messages answered within 24 hours; 95
 percent of orders shipped on time with tracking (tracking required on all USD orders); 4.8 average
 rating; at least 5 orders and $300 in sales in the period. [S30: help.etsy.com, retrieved 2026-10-22]


 9. Personalized and engraved jewelry operations
 9.1 Proofing workflow
  1. Order lands. Personalization fields auto-captured.
  2. Within 24 hours (also protects Star Seller response rate), message the buyer a proof: a rendered
    or photographed mockup of the engraving text, font, position and size. A proof sent by message
    is not a listing image, so the image policy does not restrict it.
  3. Ask for written approval in Etsy Messages ("Reply YES to approve").
  4. Start making only after approval; if no reply within 48 hours, send one reminder; if still none,
    proceed exactly as typed and note this in the message history.
  5. Photograph the finished engraving before shipping and keep it on file. It resolves "wrong
    engraving" disputes and, with permission, feeds future listing photos.
 Processing time must include the proof loop. If the buyer is slow, Etsy allows a one-time extension of
 ship-by by up to 21 days with buyer confirmation. [S20: help.etsy.com, retrieved 2026-10-22]

 9.2 Engraving guide (listing photo 11)
  • Show 3 to 5 fonts at actual size on a real ring.
  • Show maximum characters per position (face, inside).
  • List allowed symbols.
  • Show how ampersands, dots and hearts render.
  • Note: longer text means smaller letters.

 9.3 Sizing support
  • Size chart image with US size, inner diameter mm, circumference mm.
  • Ring sizer as its own low-price listing; credit its price against the ring (a transparent coupon, not
    review-linked).
  • Wide bands fit tighter: say so and recommend going up a quarter or half size above a stated
    width.
  • Resizing policy: state free or paid, within how many days, and that the buyer pays return
    shipping or not. Seller Policy requires you to honor what you state. [S3: etsy.com, 2026-10-05]
  • Some designs (fully engraved eternity bands, enamel all round) cannot be resized; say so in the
    description and in the size chart.

 No 2025 to 2026 primary source found. Practice guidance: rigid box with insert, outer shipping box

 weight, stones, maker, date), warranty card, handwritten or printed note. Photograph exactly this

 10. Buyer psychology: high-ticket gold from an unknown seller
 The buyer is asking four questions. Answer each with something visible on the listing.
  1. "Is it really solid gold?" Hallmark close-up, karat and purity in the first 160 characters, weight in
    grams, materials certificate shown in the packaging photo. US context: if a maker stamps a
    quality mark, US practice is that it must also carry a registered trademark nearby, and karat gold
    may be under karat by no more than 3 parts per thousand (7 with solder). [MJSA compliance
    guide, undated, see Unverified]. Accurate materials and origin are an Etsy Seller Policy
    requirement. [S3: etsy.com, 2026-10-05]
  2. "Will it look like the photo?" Real unedited photos (now mandatory), on-hand scale, buyer photo
    reviews, color-true editing. [S1: etsy.com, 2026-10-22]
  3. "Will it arrive, on time, without surprise fees?" Estimated delivery range, tracking, "ships from
    [US state]" and free shipping statement. Etsy's DDP rule for US orders covers international
    shipments only and does not apply to this domestic shop. [S3: etsy.com, 2026-10-05]
  4. "What if something goes wrong?" Clear returns, resize and warranty policy, fast message replies
    (Star Seller badge), professional responses to the few negative reviews.
 Trust signals stack, ranked by effort-to-impact for EON and Artifact Studio:
  • Star Seller badge (operational, free).
  • Photo reviews from real buyers.
  • Hallmark macro photo and materials infographic.
  • About section with named makers and workshop photos (accuracy required by Seller Policy).
  • Making-process image or video.
  • Warranty card and certificate in the box, shown in photos.
  • Consistent brand: shop icon, banner, cohesive photo style (Etsy names shop icon and consistent
    branding as trust builders). [S15: etsy.com, 2025-08-26]

 12. TACTICS
  1. Build a "real photo" bank this month. Since 22 Oct 2026, every listing needs one unedited real
    photo. Batch shoot each design in every metal color once, unedited plus edited, and refresh
    listings.
  2. Shoot heroes for crop safety: subject in the central 60 percent, then check the thumbnail tool in
    all three shapes.
  3. Put a hallmark macro in slot 3 or 4 on every gold listing. It is the cheapest trust signal you have.
  4. Size the text field limits to the metal. A face that fits 3 letters gets a 3-character limit.
  5. Promise a proof in the first 160 characters or the personalization section. It removes the biggest
    risk of buying engraved gold online.
  6. Respond to every first message inside 24 hours to protect Star Seller (95 percent threshold).
  7. Never reply publicly to a bad review until private resolution is exhausted; replying locks the
    review.
  8. Set processing time to what you actually achieve, then beat it; Etsy shows faster ranges once 5
    or more orders over 120 days prove you are faster.
  9. Fold shipping into price and state "free tracked US shipping" for US buyers.
  10. Sell a ring sizer listing and credit its cost toward the ring.
  11. Use Alura A/B only on high-traffic listings, hero photo first, revenue as the metric for price tests,
    run long enough for Medium confidence.

  12. Reuse buyer review photos (with permission) as listing images in slot 16; they are real photos
    of the actual item.
  13. Use Etsy's AI title tool for suggestions, but export a CSV first and keep brand-critical words
    ("solid gold," "14K").
  14. Create a packaging shot that matches what ships, exactly.
  15. One review request message per order, timed after delivery and framed as a fit check.

 13. COMMON MISTAKES
  • Using CAD renders or AI concept images to sell rings not yet made (violates "real, finished items"
    and the Seller Policy ban on renderings).
  • AI-recoloring one ring to show yellow, white and rose (changes "actual color").
  • No unedited photo in the set after 22 Oct 2026.
  • Hero with text overlay, collage, or a blank band instead of an engraved one.
  • Portrait-oriented hero that crops off the ring in landscape or square thumbnails.
  • Auto white balance producing greenish or orange gold across a listing.
  • Saying "gold" or "solid gold" without the karat, or omitting weight.
  • Hiding country of origin; it must be accurate.
  • Listing a ships-from location other than the partner's actual US address.
  • Processing time that ignores the proof loop, causing late shipments and lost Star Seller status.
  • Character limit left at 1,024 on a face engraving, inviting impossible requests.
  • Variation price jumps that appear only after a size is chosen, without explanation.
  • Responding publicly to a negative review before trying to fix it privately.
  • Offering a discount or freebie for a review.
  • Copying the title into the first lines of the description.
  • Running A/B price tests judged on order count instead of revenue.
  • Trusting blog conversion benchmarks over the shop's own 90-day data.
