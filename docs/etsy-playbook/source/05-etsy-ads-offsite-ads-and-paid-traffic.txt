 2. Etsy Ads mechanics in 2026
 2.1 How the auction works
  • Ads appear only in designated ad spaces in Etsy search results, and also on Category pages and
    Market pages, on web and app [S15: help.etsy.com, live page read 2026-10-22; S16:
    help.etsy.com, live page read 2026-10-22].
  • Listings compete in an auction. Two main factors: listing quality (likelihood of a click) and bid
    [S17: help.etsy.com, live page read 2026-10-22].
  • Quality score reflects listing and shop history, plus strong titles and tags. A listing with a low
    quality score may need a higher bid to compete [same source].
  • The bid is the most you would pay for one click on that listing. Bids rise or fall between auctions,
    informed by likelihood of sales (search query, time of day, device) and relevance (title and tag
    match, and whether the listing meets the shopper's filters such as ship-to location and price
    range). Listings that fail the filters are not shown [same source].
  • Ads use a different algorithm from organic Etsy search [same source].
  • You may not see your own ads when you search, because likelihood of sale affects placement.
    Etsy says to check ad stats rather than searching for yourself [same source].
  • Ads only reach buyers in countries you ship to [S16: help.etsy.com, live page read 2026-10-22].
    This is the only geographic control. If you ship worldwide but your real market is the US, your ads
    also run abroad.

 2.2 Cost per click
  • Etsy publishes no CPC range. CPC "can vary by listing and by placement" and is set automatically
    [S14: help.etsy.com, live page read 2026-10-22].
  • THIRD-PARTY ranges (estimates, not Etsy data):

 2.3 Budget rules
  • Minimum daily budget: $1 [S16: help.etsy.com, live page read 2026-10-22].
  • Every seller starts with a maximum daily budget of $25. Etsy recalculates the maximum weekly
    from the last 7 days of spend. It can rise if you consistently use your full budget. Days with no
    spend do not lower it [same source].
  • A drop in maximum can be tied to an amount due on your Etsy bill. Paying it will likely restore the
    limit [same source].
  • Restarting a campaign keeps your previous maximum limit. To raise the cap before a peak
    period, spend your full budget early [S21: alura.io, THIRD-PARTY, Alura summary of Etsy help
    content, , undated, read 2026-10-22].
  • THIRD-PARTY claims a $100 per day upper limit [S19: support.profittree.io, 2025-12-26]. Etsy's
    own page does not state a hard ceiling. Treat $100 as unconfirmed.
  • Waiting period: a new shop must wait 15 days after opening before starting a campaign [S16:
    help.etsy.com, live page read 2026-10-22].

 2.4 How Etsy spends the budget
  • You pay per click, never per view [S14: help.etsy.com, live page read 2026-10-22].
  • Clicks are totaled per day and added to your Payment account the next day. "You'll never spend
    over your daily budget." Once the budget is used, ads stop for the day [S16: help.etsy.com, live
    page read 2026-10-22].
  • Etsy says machine learning now improves "seller budget pacing" [S22: fool.com, ETSY Q2 2026
    call, CFO Charles Baker, , 2026-08-12].
  • Important reading of the earnings commentary: Etsy reports that Etsy Ads is the primary driver of
    take rate growth [S10: sec.gov, 2026-08-05]. That means ads are a growing share of what sellers
    pay Etsy. It does not prove ads are profitable for any given seller. Your own break-even math
    decides.

  3. Listing strategies, only at $25+ per day [S5: etsy.com, 2026-06-01]:
     • Greater visibility: more reach. Etsy suggests it for bestsellers, new launches, key shopping
       moments.
     • Lower click cost: cheaper clicks. Etsy suggests it for thin-margin or limited-inventory items.
     • Efficient spending: balanced, the default.

 2.6 Which listings get advertised by default
  • All current listings are included when a campaign starts. New listings created after the start can
    be added or left out [S16: help.etsy.com, live page read 2026-10-22].
  • Etsy's own beginner guidance is to promote all active listings first so the algorithm learns, and
    not to adjust for 30 days [S24: etsy.com, 2026-06-01].

 2.9 How ads interact with organic ranking
  • Etsy states ads run on a separate algorithm from organic search [S17: help.etsy.com, live page
    read 2026-10-22].
  • Etsy does not publish any statement, in 2025 or 2026 sources found, that ad clicks or ad sales lift
    organic rank. The common seller belief that "ads boost SEO" is not confirmed by Etsy. What is
    confirmed: listing and shop history feed the ad quality score [same source], and good titles and
    tags help both. So the safe statement is: better listings help ads; whether ads help organic rank is
    not documented.
  • Etsy's Shop Stats separate traffic sources so you can watch whether organic visits grow while ads
    run. Etsy Ads clicks from desktop show as Advertising; clicks from the Etsy app are reported
    under "Etsy app & other Etsy pages", and the Etsy Ads figure in "How shoppers found you"
    excludes app ad visits [S25: help.etsy.com, live page read 2026-10-22]. This means your Stats
    page undercounts ad traffic. Use the Ads dashboard for ad numbers.


 3. Offsite Ads
 3.1 Core rules (ETSY, live page read 2026-10-22: [S28: help.etsy.com])
  • Channels: Google, Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest, Bing, Etsy Publishing Partner sites, Google
    Display Network (over two million sites, including YouTube and Gmail).
  • TikTok is not on Etsy's Offsite Ads channel list. Etsy does say it moved its own paid social spend
    toward platforms like TikTok [S10: sec.gov, 2026-08-05], but that is Etsy's marketing, not a
    stated Offsite Ads channel for your fee.
  • Fee: 15% if the shop made under $10,000 USD in the past 12 months; 12% if $10,000 or more.
  • Cap: never more than $100 USD per order.
  • Fee base: Etsy's page uses two phrasings ("the amount you made from your sale before the
    advertising fee" and "order total"). THIRD-PARTY sources state it applies to item price plus
    shipping plus gift wrap [S26: craftybase.com, read 2026-10-22]. Assume shipping is included.
  • Currency: calculated in USD; non-USD shops converted at the sale's exchange rate.
  • Rate review: Etsy reviews sales monthly to set your rate.
  • Attribution: 30 days after the ad click. If the last click before the order was an Etsy Ad (onsite),
    only the Etsy Ads cost applies, not the Offsite fee. Multiple separate orders within the window are
    each eligible for the fee.
  • Charged only on sales, not clicks.
  • Enrollment: everyone is auto-enrolled. Above $10,000 in 365 days, participation is mandatory for
    the life of the shop. Below, you can opt out: Shop Manager > Settings > Offsite Ads > Opt out.
    Re-enroll with "Turn on Offsite Ads".
  • Opting out can take up to three business days to take effect, and clicks before that can still be
    attributed within 30 days [S26: craftybase.com, THIRD-PARTY, , read 2026-10-22].
  • Your current fee rate shows on the Shop Dashboard. The Offsite Ads dashboard shows clicked ad,
    click date, channel or website, purchased listing, and order number and date [S25:
    help.etsy.com, live page read 2026-10-22]. Since June 2026 it also shows revenue, fees, visits,

  • Partner Checkout on Google: US sellers only, ship from continental US, free shipping, no variants,
    no personalization. This US shop meets the location and free-shipping conditions, but
    personalized or variant-based made-to-order rings still do not qualify [S28: help.etsy.com, live

 3.2 Is Offsite Ads worth it for high-ticket gold?
 Arguments FOR keeping it on (while under $10K and still allowed to choose):
  • The $100 cap makes the effective rate fall as price rises. At 15%, the cap is hit at $666.67; at
    12%, at $833.33. A $1,500 ring pays at most $100, an effective 6.7% (EXAMPLE arithmetic from
    the Etsy cap and rates).
  • Etsy, not you, pays for the clicks. Etsy's CEO said Etsy "improved our PLA performance really
    meaningfully through better segmentation and bidding strategies", and the CFO noted Amazon
    pulled out of the PLA (product listing ad) auction [S22: fool.com, 2026-08-12]. Google Shopping is
    the channel where high-ticket jewelry searches happen.
  • THIRD-PARTY guidance says Google Shopping suits high-ticket items such as "high-end jewelry"
    [S29: insightagent.app, 2026].
 Arguments AGAINST:
  • The 30-day click window means some fees land on buyers who would have bought anyway (for
    example, someone who already knew your shop, searched Google, clicked an Etsy Shopping ad).
  • At $150 to $600, the full 12% or 15% applies on top of roughly 9.5% plus $0.45 per order in base
    Etsy fees for a US seller (see Section 4 and FORMULAS). On thin-margin items this can wipe out
    profit.
  • Once you pass $10,000 in 365 days, you lose the choice forever.
 Decision rule (tactical): keep Offsite Ads ON if your contribution margin after all other fees stays
 positive after deducting 15% (or $100, whichever is lower) on every listing. If any listing goes
 negative, either reprice it or opt out. Check monthly in the Offsite Ads dashboard how many orders
 and how much fee it produced, and whether those were new buyers.

 3.3 How to calculate Offsite Ads impact
  • Offsite fee per order = min(rate x order total, $100).
  • Offsite effective rate = fee / order total.
  • Monthly Offsite cost = sum of fees in the Offsite Ads dashboard.
  • Incremental test (practical): Etsy offers no holdout test. If you are under $10K, you can compare
    a 60-day period ON vs a 60-day period OFF, same season (rarely clean). Simpler: look at the
    Offsite dashboard's channel and listing breakdown. If most Offsite orders are first-time buyers on
    high-price listings, the program is likely bringing new demand.

 Base fee load before ads: 6.5% (transaction) + 3% (processing) = 9.5% of the item price, plus $0.25
 per order, plus $0.20 listing renewal per sale, plus 3% of any sales tax the buyer pays (processing is
 charged on tax; transaction fee is not). No Regulatory Operating Fee, no currency conversion. Ads
 are on top. Sales tax is collected and remitted by Etsy as marketplace facilitator and passes
 through, so the examples below use the no-tax case; with an illustrative 8% tax, base fees rise by
 about 0.24% of price (for example $38.45 to $39.41 on a $400 ring) [arithmetic from the sources
 above; 8% is an assumption, not a sourced rate].


 5. FORMULAS
 All formulas are standard arithmetic. All numeric examples are EXAMPLE values.
  1. Etsy fee rate (US shop, no Offsite, no sales tax) F = 0.065 + 0.03 = 0.095, plus fixed $0.45 per
    order ($0.25 processing + $0.20 listing renewal).
  2. Contribution before ads (C) = Price - (Price x F) - fixed fees - COGS. COGS for made-to-order gold
    = gold + labor/casting + stones + plating/finishing + packaging + US domestic shipping label
    you pay (free shipping) + signature confirmation + shipping insurance.
  3. Contribution margin m = C / Price.
  4. Break-even ROAS = 1 / m = Price / C. (Matches the THIRD-PARTY form "Break-even ROAS = 1 /
    profit margin" [S29: insightagent.app, 2026]. Alura's Insights calculates break-even ROAS "after
    Etsy's fees, using your own margin" [S11: alura.io, 2026-08-10].)
  5. Target ROAS (keep a profit share k of contribution after ads) = 1 / (m x (1 - k)). Example: keep
    half the contribution, k = 0.5, target ROAS = 2 / m.
  6. Max CPA (break-even cost per ad order) = C. Target CPA = C x (1 - k).


Etsy Fine Jewelry Master Course | Module 5: Etsy Ads, Offsite Ads and Paid Traffic                                      Page 90
  7. CPA = CPC / CVR (CVR = orders / clicks).
  8. Max affordable CPC = Target CPA x CVR.
  9. Clicks needed per order = 1 / CVR.
  10. Offsite fee = min(r x order total, $100), r = 0.15 or 0.12. Cap price point = 100 / r.
  11. Attribution haircut: because Etsy credits any order within 30 days, use Adjusted ROAS =
    Reported ROAS x i, where i is your estimate of the true incremental share (unknown; pick a
    conservative value such as 0.6 to 0.8 and refine by comparing periods with ads on and off). This
    is a planning assumption, not an Etsy metric.
  12. Ad spend as % of revenue = total ad spend / total shop revenue (all channels).
  13. Blended CPA = total ad spend (Etsy Ads + Offsite fees + external ads) / total orders.

  • Offsite at 15% would be $180 (or $144 at 12%) but is capped at $100 (8.3% effective).
    Contribution after Offsite = $275.73 (23.0%).
 Lesson: high gold content lowers margin percentage, which raises break-even ROAS, even though
 dollar profit per order is higher. Offsite Ads becomes relatively cheaper as price rises because of the
 cap.

 7. The Etsy Ads operating playbook
 7.1 Before you spend anything (readiness gate)
  • Listing quality first. Etsy's auction rewards click likelihood; titles, tags, photos, and history all feed
    the quality score [S17: help.etsy.com, live page read 2026-10-22]. Ads amplify listings; they do
    not fix weak ones.
  • Ship-to settings: ads only show where you ship [S16: help.etsy.com]. If US is the focus and
    international shipping, insurance, and returns are painful for fine jewelry, narrow shipping profiles
    to the US so ad spend stays on domestic buyers.
  • Know each listing's break-even ROAS (Section 5) and enter margins in Alura's margin settings
    (one overall margin or per section) [S23: alura.io, read 2026-10-22].
  • Production time and processing time must be realistic: a made-to-order ring with long processing
    time can lower conversion, which raises CPA.

 7.2 Launch phase (days 0 to 30)
  • Etsy's own guidance: start around $3 to $5 per day, advertise all active listings, and make no
    changes for 30 days [S24: etsy.com, 2026-06-01].
  • THIRD-PARTY variants: $1 to $3 per day for testing [S19: support.profittree.io, 2025-12-26; S18:
    listingview.io, 2026-06-06]; $3 to $5 per day on 3 to 5 best listings [S29: insightagent.app, 2026].



Etsy Fine Jewelry Master Course | Module 5: Etsy Ads, Offsite Ads and Paid Traffic                      Page 92
  • For a $150 to $1,500 gold shop, a $1 to $3 budget usually buys only a few clicks a day and may
    need months to produce statistically useful order data. A practical compromise
    (recommendation, not a sourced number): start at $5 to $10 per day for 30 days, which yields
    enough clicks per listing to see CTR and search terms, then decide.
  • Etsy strategy note: below $25 per day you cannot set listing strategies; everything runs on
    Efficient spending [S16: help.etsy.com]. That is fine for launch.

 7.3 "Advertise all then prune" vs "selective"
  • Advertise all then prune (Etsy's default and Etsy Handbook advice): gives the algorithm data on
    all 40 listings, finds unexpected winners, but thin budget gets spread across 40 listings and early
    spend is uneven [S24: etsy.com, 2026-06-01].
  • Selective (THIRD-PARTY advice: "Advertising your best existing listings is recommended over
    spreading budget across the whole shop" [S18: listingview.io, 2026-06-06]): concentrates spend,
    faster learning per listing, but you may never discover sleepers.
  • Recommendation for 40 listings: hybrid. Turn on all listings that clear a quality bar (strong first
    photo, complete tags, price where break-even ROAS is below about 4), leave off any listing whose
    break-even ROAS is very high (thin margin) or which has weak photos. Use ad groups to label
    them: "Hero", "Test", "Seasonal", "New". Ad groups exist for exactly this purpose: Etsy suggests
    groups for best-sellers or new listings, and sellers use them to separate seasonal products and
    tests [S13: valueaddedresource.net, 2026-09-01].

 7.4 Testing structure
  • Unit of test: a listing (you cannot test keywords directly; you can only exclude search terms and
    change titles/tags/photos).
  • One change per listing at a time: either photo, title, price, or strategy. Wait at least 30 days per
    Etsy for strategy changes [S5: etsy.com, 2026-06-01].
  • Use ad groups as test cells. Example: Group "Photo test A" vs Group "Photo test B" with
    comparable listings. Remember totals show one group at a time [S16: help.etsy.com].
  • Minimum evidence before judging a listing:
     • Alura rules require at least 10 clicks before judging a listing [S23: alura.io, read 2026-10-22] (a
       floor, too low for $400+ items).
     • THIRD-PARTY rule: judge only after ad spend reaches about twice the product price [S19:
       support.profittree.io, 2025-12-26]. For gold jewelry, a better variant is: judge after spend
       reaches your Max CPA (Section 5), because one order at break-even CPA is the minimum
       signal.
     • THIRD-PARTY rules of thumb: turn off if $30 spent with zero orders, or ROAS under 2x after 30
       days [S18: listingview.io, 2026-06-06]. For a $400 ring with a $163 Max CPA, $30 is too early
       to judge.

 7.5 When to pause a listing
 Pause (or switch to Lower click cost if on $25+) when ANY of these is true after the evidence
 threshold:
  1. Spend has reached the listing's Max CPA with zero ad orders and the listing has no organic sales
    either.
  2. 30-day reported ROAS is below break-even ROAS, and the 60-day ROAS also is.




Etsy Fine Jewelry Master Course | Module 5: Etsy Ads, Offsite Ads and Paid Traffic                    Page 93
  3. CTR is well below the shop's average for 30 days (the ad is losing auctions and teaching Etsy it is
    not clickable). Fix the first photo before deciding.
  4. Search terms are mostly irrelevant (for example "gold plated", "cheap", "vermeil", "sterling")
    and you cannot exclude them.
 Do NOT pause when:
  • The listing has few ad orders but many favorites and "orders from ads" on OTHER listings in the
    shop originate from it (look at which listings get the clicks vs which listings sell; with the any-item
    attribution, a hero piece can drive sales of a cheaper piece).
  • It is within 3 to 6 weeks of a peak season for that item.

 7.6 Using ads data to find keywords
  • Open each listing's Search terms (last 30 days) [S14: help.etsy.com].
  • Etsy's Handbook phase three: review popular listings and keywords, and add high-performing
    keywords to other relevant listings [S24: etsy.com, 2026-06-01].
  • Process:
     1. Export or note all terms with orders. These are proven buyer language. Put them into titles
       and tags of the selling listing and siblings.
     2. Terms with many clicks and zero orders: if irrelevant, exclude (if available in your dashboard,
       or via Alura Optimize keywords); if relevant, the listing failed to convert, so check price,
       photos, and processing time.
     3. Terms with views but low CTR: the listing appears for that query but the thumbnail does not
       win. Test a new first photo.
     4. Cross-check volumes and competition in eRank Keyword Tool and Keyword Lists [S40:
       help.erank.com, read 2026-10-22] and in Etsy Marketplace Insights (15 free searches per
       week) [S3: etsy.com, 2025-09-15]. Since June 2026, Marketplace Insights is testing 30-day,
       90-day, and 12-month ranges, price ranges, and search conversion rates [S27: help.etsy.com,
       June 2026]; since September 2026 it is testing personalized search term recommendations
       [same, Sept 2026].
  • Search term data is hidden for low-traffic listings [S14: help.etsy.com], which is another reason
    not to spread a tiny budget over 40 listings.

 7.7 Scaling
  • Raise budget only when shop-level adjusted ROAS stays above target ROAS for 30 days.
    THIRD-PARTY rule: scale by 20% to 30% when ROAS stays above 3 to 4 [S29: insightagent.app,
    2026]. For gold, replace "3 to 4" with your target ROAS.
  • Frequent budget changes reset learning [S18: listingview.io, 2026-06-06]. Change budget in
    steps, not daily.
  • Once you can sustain $25 per day profitably, unlock listing strategies: hero rings on Greater
    visibility during peak; thin-margin pieces on Lower click cost [S5: etsy.com, 2026-06-01].
  • To grow the max daily budget ahead of Q4, spend your full budget consistently from early
    autumn; the cap recalculates weekly [S16: help.etsy.com].

 8. WEEKLY ADS ROUTINE (about 45 minutes, same day each
 week)
  1. Pull numbers (Etsy Ads dashboard, last 7 and last 30 days): views, clicks, CTR, orders, revenue,
    spend, ROAS. Alura Overview shows the same plus profit vs previous period [S23: alura.io].
  2. Shop-level check: adjusted ROAS (reported x your haircut) vs target ROAS. Ad spend as % of
    total revenue vs your stage budget (Section 10).
  3. Listing triage (sort by spend):
     • Spend above Max CPA with zero orders: pause or switch strategy.
     • ROAS above target with healthy volume: candidate for Greater visibility or more budget.

    the $100 cap.
  6. Organic health: Shop Stats traffic sources. If 80%+ of traffic comes from Etsy Ads, THIRD-PARTY
    guidance says you are too dependent on paid traffic [S29: insightagent.app, 2026].

    2026-08-03]; Meta/Pinterest ad managers for spend and outbound clicks.

 11.6 Legal disclosure for influencer posts (FTC)
 FTC Endorsement Guides were revised in 2023 and the FTC's FAQ was last modified 2025-07-15
 [S44: ftc.gov, modified 2025-07-15]. Key rules:
  • Free product sent with the expectation of promotion requires disclosure, even if small or
    returned.
  • Disclose clearly and conspicuously, at or near the start, not only in comments, not behind "more".
  • Video: disclosure in the video itself, visual and audio when the endorsement is spoken.
    Livestreams: repeat periodically.
  • Acceptable: "Ad:", "Paid ad", "#ad" at the beginning, "Sponsored by [brand]", "Thanks [brand] for
    the free product".
  • Likely insufficient alone: "Gifted", "#ambassador", "#client", "#sp"-style run-together tags,
    "thank you".
  • Affiliate or commission links: disclose near the link ("I get commissions for purchases made
    through links in this post"); "affiliate link" alone is not adequate.
  • Platform disclosure tools do not guarantee compliance; brand and endorser are both responsible.
    Disclose in the language of the post.
  • Your duty as the brand: put the disclosure requirement in writing in every seeding agreement
    and monitor posts.


 12. TACTICS (fine gold jewelry specific)
  1. Lead with the first photo. CTR is the auction input you control most. Test a clean on-hand shot vs
    a white-background shot for hero rings.
  2. Put the karat and metal in the title start ("14K Solid Gold Signet Ring"). Search terms like "gold
    filled", "plated", "vermeil" waste clicks on a solid-gold listing; exclude them if your dashboard
    allows.




Etsy Fine Jewelry Master Course | Module 5: Etsy Ads, Offsite Ads and Paid Traffic                                   Page 99
  3. Split catalog into ad groups by price band ($150 to $300, $300 to $700, $700+). Break-even
    ROAS differs per band; judge each band against its own break-even.
  4. Advertise entry pieces ($150 to $300) for discovery. The 30-day any-item attribution means an
    entry-piece click can lead to a higher-ticket order; check orders from ads by item.
  5. Keep thin-margin, gold-heavy pieces off ads or on Lower click cost; their break-even ROAS may
    be above 4.
  6. Narrow ship-to countries if you only want US traffic for ads [S16: help.etsy.com].
  7. Build the max budget cap before Q4: spend the full budget consistently from October [same].
  8. Keep ads on in January for engagement season; competition from gift sellers drops after
    December.
  9. While under $10K, keep Offsite Ads on only if every listing stays profitable after a 15% fee; the
    $100 cap favors your $700+ pieces.
  10. Use Share & Save links on every owned channel (Instagram bio, email, WhatsApp follow-ups);
    4% back on a $400 order is $16.
  11. Use promo codes with redemption caps for creators (June 2026 control).
  12. Etsy's "interested shopper offers" automatically send offers to shoppers who engaged; Etsy
    says they reach "2x more potential buyers on average" than other targeted offers alone (Etsy
    internal analysis, 2026-03-18 to 2026-04-17) [S27: help.etsy.com, June 2026]. Pair with ads: ads
    bring the visit, offers close it. Factor the discount into contribution.
  13. Watch for the "extra ad budget when you hit your sales goal" test; if offered, set the goal on
    ad-attributed sales only if Etsy allows, since organic sales could unlock spend you did not need
    [S13: valueaddedresource.net, 2026-09-01].
  14. Re-run break-even ROAS when gold price moves; your COGS changes, so does every threshold.

 13. COMMON MISTAKES
  1. Using generic "ROAS 3x is good" rules. A gold ring with 31% contribution margin needs ROAS 3.2
    just to break even, and 6.4 to keep half its contribution (EXAMPLE, Section 5).
  2. Treating reported ROAS as incremental. Etsy credits any order within 30 days of a click [S14:
    help.etsy.com].
  3. Judging after $30 or 10 clicks on a $400 item. Not enough data at high price points.
  4. Changing budget, strategy, and listings every few days. Etsy says give it 30 days [S24: etsy.com,
    2026-06-01].
  5. Spreading $3 per day across 40 listings, then getting no search term data because traffic per
    listing is too low [S14: help.etsy.com].
  6. Searching for your own ads to "check" them. Etsy says you may not see them [S17:
    help.etsy.com].
