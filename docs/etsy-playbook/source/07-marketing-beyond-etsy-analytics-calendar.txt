 1. Etsy's own marketing tools (2025 to 2026)
 1.1 Sales, promo codes and targeted offers
 Etsy's current help article lists these promotion types [S1: help.etsy.com, accessed 2026-10-22]:
  • Promo codes: percentage off from 5% to 75% in whole numbers, fixed amount off, or free
    standard shipping. Codes can carry a redemption limit (which cannot be changed after creation)
    and can be limited to one use per buyer.
  • Sales: percentage off or free shipping for a set audience and period. A sale with an order
    minimum is shop-wide and cannot exclude items; a sale without a minimum can target specific
    listings. If several sales apply, only the highest discount is used.
  • Order minimum discounts and discounted bundles (up to 3 listings; a listing can be in only
    one bundle; bundles do not stack with other promotions).
  • Targeted offers, emailed by Etsy to opted-in shoppers:
     • Abandoned cart: triggers when an item has sat in a cart more than 2 hours; sent at most
       once every 2 days; expires 60 days after creation.
     • Favorited item: same frequency and 60-day expiry.
     • Interested shopper: based on Etsy's read of interest (view frequency and recency); at most
       once every 30 days; expires after 7 days; single use.
     • Thank you: sent after a completed order to drive a repeat purchase; expires 1 year after
       sending.
  • Stacking: a buyer can redeem one targeted offer at a time, and a sale plus targeted offer
    resolves to the higher discount [same source].
 2026 changes: "Interested shopper offers" sent automatically to engaged shoppers, and promo
 codes limitable by number of uses, both dated June 2026 by a third-party tracker [S2:
 growingyourcraft.com, updated 2026-10-08].
 Tactical read for gold jewelry. Gold prices make deep discounting dangerous (see section 7).
 The targeted offers are the most margin-efficient discounts because they only reach people who

 1.2 Share & Save and Creator Collective
  • Share & Save: any seller can join, no application. You get 4% back on your Etsy bill for eligible
    orders that come through your link within 30 days; new members earn 6.5% for the first two
    weeks. Links are generated in Shop Manager and shared on blogs, email and social; tracking is in
    the Share & Save dashboard [S3: etsy.com, article body dated 2026-08-03].
  • Creator Collective: application based, at least 500 followers on one supported channel, 10%
    commission on eligible orders from any Etsy shop. You can qualify for both but the same sharing
    activity cannot earn from both programs [same source].
 Tactic: every link you put in Instagram bio, Pinterest pins that point to Etsy, email to past buyers
 who opted in, and influencer seeding briefs should be a Share & Save link. It also protects you from
 Offsite Ads fees on that traffic only if the attribution resolves to Share & Save rather than an Offsite
 Ad click; Etsy does not publish a precedence rule on the pages fetched, so treat this as UNVERIFIED
 and test by watching your bill.

 1.3 Offsite Ads and Etsy Ads (marketing cost you must plan around)
  • Offsite Ads: shops under $10,000 in a rolling 365 days pay 15% and can opt out; shops at or
    above $10,000 pay 12% and stay enrolled for life; fee capped at $100 per order; 30-day
    attribution after an Offsite Ad click; if the last click was an on-site Etsy Ad, only the CPC applies
    [S4: enrichlabs.ai, 2026; vendor blog].
  • For a solid gold ring, the $100 cap is the number that matters: on a $1,000 ring you pay the full
    $100 maximum, so Offsite Ads costs roughly 10% at that price point (simple arithmetic on the
    stated cap).
  • Etsy Ads: $1 minimum daily budget; new advertisers start with a $25 max daily budget
    recalculated weekly; listing strategies require $25+ [same source]. Per-listing ad strategies at
    $25+ daily budget dated June 2026 and ad groups dated September 2026 [S2:
    growingyourcraft.com, 2026-10-08]. Ad groups are confirmed by Etsy's own Fall 2026 update [S5:
    etsy.com, 2026-09-28].

 2.2 Instagram and TikTok for fine jewelry
 Confirmed context: Etsy's social team features shops that post in-process videos, behind the scenes
 and product groupings [S14: etsy.com, 2026]; Etsy's fall 2026 report shows hand-made process
 items surging (hand painted +280%) [S10: etsy.com, 2026-10-14]. Platform algorithm specifics for
 2025 to 2026 were not researched (budget gap).
 Formats that consistently suit solid gold made-to-order work (craft logic, not platform data):
  • Hand try-on: one hand, natural daylight, slow rotate, ring catching light. Show scale with a
    known object.
  • Making-of: wax or CAD to casting to polishing to stone setting. 15 to 30 seconds.
  • Engraving reveal: blank band, engraving, wipe, macro of the letters. The personalization
    moment is the emotional hook.
  • Gold pour / casting: high visual drama; caption with the karat and hallmark so it reads as solid
    gold, not plated.
  • Hallmark proof: macro of the 14k or 18k stamp. Answers the vermeil vs solid gold question
    directly.
  • Unboxing from the buyer's view: packaging, insert card, ring box opening.
  • Story posts: "This ring carries the coordinates of where they met." Meaning-led, matching EON's
    heirloom position.

 2.4 UGC and micro-influencer seeding, with FTC compliance
  • FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255, revised 2023) require clear and conspicuous
    disclosure of any material connection, including free or discounted product, affiliate commission
    and early access. Disclosures hidden behind "more" or in a pile of hashtags are treated as
    misses; "#sp" is discouraged; brands are expected to train and monitor creators [S24:
    blog.promise.legal, 2026-04-16].
  • FTC Consumer Reviews Rule (16 CFR Part 465, effective 2024-10-21) prohibits fake or
    AI-generated reviews, buying reviews, incentives conditioned on sentiment, undisclosed insider
    reviews, and review suppression such as routing only happy buyers to public reviews [same
    source].
  • Maximum civil penalty reported at $53,088 per violation in 2025, and a December 2025 batch of
    FTC warning letters on incentive-for-5-star and review gating [S25: beancount.io, 2026-08-16;
    secondary source, verify on ftc.gov].
 Seeding playbook for gold jewelry:
  1. Shortlist 20 micro creators (roughly 5k to 50k followers) whose audience is US women 28 to 45
    who post meaningful-gift or bridal content.
  2. Offer one personalized piece in exchange for an honest post, not a positive one. Written brief
    requires "#ad" or "Gifted by EON" at the start of the caption and spoken or on-screen disclosure
    in video.
  3. Give each creator a Share & Save link (you earn credit) or, if they are Creator Collective
    members, let them use their own link; they cannot double-dip.
  4. Track with a spreadsheet: shipped date, posted date, disclosure check, link clicks from Share &
    Save dashboard.
  5. Never ask seeded creators to leave Etsy reviews. A gifted item review is an incentivized review
    and the Reviews Rule bars sentiment-conditioned incentives.
  6. Gold value note: a 14k ring is a high-value gift. Use a smaller entry piece (initial pendant, thin
    stacking band) for seeding.

 3.2 What you can and cannot do with buyer data and packaging
 From Etsy's current Seller Policy (last updated 2026-10-05) [S26: etsy.com]:
  • Buyer information "may only be used for Etsy-related communications or for Etsy-facilitated
    transactions."
  • You "may not use this information for unsolicited commercial messages," and without consent
    you "may not add any Etsy member to your email or physical mailing list."
  • Messages may not be used for "unsolicited advertising or promotions," or to contact someone
    who asked not to be contacted.
  • You must "not facilitate off-platform transactions," and listings may not encourage or facilitate an
    off-platform transaction.
  • The policy page contains no explicit rule on packaging inserts.
 Practical, lawful repeat-buyer system:
  1. Use Etsy's Thank you targeted offer as the default repeat incentive. It is Etsy-native and
    fully compliant.
  2. Insert card: care instructions, hallmark explanation, the story of the piece, a QR to a care or
    ring-size page on your website, and an opt-in invitation ("Join the EON letter for anniversary
    reminders"). The buyer chooses to sign up; you do not add them. Avoid printing "order direct and
    skip Etsy" or a discount for buying off Etsy, because that reads as facilitating an off-platform
    transaction. Because the policy is silent on inserts, the conservative line is: brand and opt-in yes,
    off-Etsy discount no.
  3. Do not export Etsy buyer emails into Klaviyo or Shopify unless the buyer explicitly opted
    in through your own form. Printify's 2025 guide says consent is required before exporting buyer
    emails from Etsy's order download [S27: printify.com, 2025-01-27].
  4. Etsy messages only for order-related communication (order received, engraving proof,
    shipped, delivered, care). Alura follow-ups are scoped to these triggers and skip cancelled,
    refunded, active-conversation and negative-review orders; a keyword blocklist (refund, return,
    damaged, broken, missing) holds messages [S28: alura.io, accessed 2026-10-22]. Since
    2026-07-31 Alura sends follow-ups automatically through its Chrome extension against
    shop-level rules [S29: alura.io, 2026-07-31].

  6. Anniversary hook: personalized rings come with dates. If a buyer opts in on your own site, an
    anniversary reminder email 3 weeks before the engraved date is the highest-intent repeat trigger

 4.1 Pattern by Etsy
  • Still operating; 6,938 live stores (Q4 2026 to date), down 14% year over year from 8,112 in Q3
    2025; peak was 17,599 in Q2 2021; in the last 90 days 90 merchants moved in and 96 moved out
    [S30: storeleads.app, updated 2026-10-16].
  • $15 per month after a 30-day trial; no major features since launch, per a 2025 review [S31:
    growingyourcraft.com, 2025-12-13].
  • Verdict: no shutdown announced, but a shrinking, stagnant product. EON already has its own site;
    do not use Pattern. For Artifact Studio, use Pattern only as a cheap brand page if a full site is not
    justified.

 4.2 Own website plus Google Merchant Center
  • Google Merchant Center 2025 spec update: new offer-level attributes carrier_shipping,
    shipping_handling_business_days and shipping_transit_business_days (effective
    2025-04-08) to drive delivery estimates in Shopping ads and free listings; member prices banned
    from price fields from 2025-07-01 (use loyalty_program); US sales tax no longer required from
    2025-07-01; promotion policies expanding from January 2026 [S32: support.google.com,
    2025-04-08].
  • For made-to-order gold, set handling business days honestly (production time), and transit days
    for the US domestic carrier (about 3 to 5 business days). Wrong handling time is the most
    common cause of late deliveries that damage a new site's reviews.
  • Generative AI disclosure attributes (structured_title, structured_description) exist since
    2024 (UNVERIFIED pre-2025, 2024-04-13 via ppc.land). If AI writes EON feed titles, declare it.

 4.3 Avoiding channel conflict
  1. Price parity or Etsy slightly higher. Etsy fees plus 12 to 15% Offsite Ads mean Etsy can carry
    a higher price, but a visible gap invites buyers to leave Etsy and can look like steering. Keep
    listed prices equal and use site-only value (free ring sizer, extended engraving characters) rather
    than site-only discounts.
  2. Different roles. Etsy: discovery, gifting, first purchase, reviews. Website: brand storytelling,
    bridal and high-ticket custom, Google Shopping, owned email list.
  3. Different hero SKUs. Keep a few website-exclusive designs (heirloom signets, bridal) so
    Google traffic and Etsy traffic are not bidding against each other on identical products.
  4. Never link from Etsy messages to the website to complete a sale.

 6. MARKETING CALENDAR 2026-2027
 6.1 How to compute your made-to-order cutoff
 Formula: Last order date = target delivery date minus (production days at the US
 workshop + US domestic transit days + 2-day safety margin). Fill in your own measured
 production and transit times; the course baseline is 5 to 7 business days making plus 3 to 5 business
 days US transit (module 09). Equivalently, last order date = carrier send-by date minus production
 days minus safety margin.
 Context that changes the math in 2026:
  • US de minimis ended 2025-08-29, and from 2026-07-09 non-US Etsy sellers must use Delivered
    Duty Paid shipping for US orders to qualify for Etsy Purchase Protection [S36: etsy.com,
    2026-06-09]. None of this applies to this US shop: orders ship domestically, so there is no
    customs step, no tariff pricing and no duties copy in listings. (Context only: Etsy's pilot tariff
    estimator in the listing form, June 2026 [S2: growingyourcraft.com, 2026-10-08].)
  • USPS 2026 recommended send-by dates (for mail already in the US system) for delivery by Dec
    25: Ground Advantage Dec 17, Priority Mail Dec 18, Priority Mail Express Dec 19 [S37: usps.com,
    2026]. These apply directly, because the US workshop hands parcels to USPS domestically:
    subtract production days and the safety margin from them. For UPS or FedEx, use that carrier's
    holiday bulletin.
  • No Etsy-published 2026 Christmas shipping cutoff was found. Etsy's guidance is to set firm
    last-order dates, adjust processing times for custom items, and publish cutoffs in the Shop
    Announcement, policies and item descriptions [S38: etsy.com, timestamp 2026-10-18].

    mass market, higher end more resilient [S50: gold.org; S51: gold.org, 2026]. Globally H1 2026

 8. Competitive research method (ethical)
  1. Find top shops: search Etsy for 15 core phrases ("solid gold signet ring", "14k initial ring",
    "personalized gold name ring", "gold birthstone ring for mom"). Record the shops on page one,
    the Etsy's Picks badges and Star Seller badges.
  2. Estimate sales: EverBee per-listing monthly estimates (self-reported 80% accuracy, directional
    only) and eRank competitor tracking for shop-level stats [S35: revenuegeeks.com, 2026-08-17].
    Cross-check with public shop sales counts and review velocity.
  3. Reverse-engineer, do not copy: study title structure, price ladder, custom options, photo
    sequence, video use, processing time, shipping price, review themes (what buyers praise and
    complain about). Never copy designs, photos or text; Etsy lists lack of originality and IP issues as
    reasons shops are not featured [S14: etsy.com, 2026].
  4. Gap matrix: for each competitor, note where they are weak (no hallmark proof, long
    processing, generic story) and make that your listing's first photo or first line.
  5. Quarterly refresh: repeat with Etsy's Marketplace Insights terms when that test reaches your
    shop.

 12. COMMON MISTAKES
  1. Exporting Etsy buyer emails to a mailing list without explicit opt-in (Etsy Seller Policy).
  2. Using Etsy messages for promotions or new-collection announcements.
  3. Inserts offering a discount to buy off Etsy (facilitating off-platform transactions).
  4. Review incentives tied to stars, or asking only happy buyers for reviews (FTC Consumer Reviews
    Rule).
  5. Gifting influencers without written disclosure requirements.
  6. Raising prices before a sale to inflate the discount.
  7. Deep shop-wide Black Friday discounts on solid gold when gold is near record highs.
  8. Shipping without valid tracking or missing the ship-by date and losing Purchase Protection.
  9. Missing your own Christmas cutoff by using USPS send-by dates without first subtracting
    production days.
  10. AI-generated lifestyle images on Pinterest that get "AI modified" labels and look like
    dropshipping.
  11. Building on Pattern or "Shop updates" without confirming they are still supported.
  12. Treating EverBee revenue estimates as fact.
  13. Listing Valentine's or Mother's Day collections too late for press and Pinterest lead times.
  14. Calling gold-plated or vermeil "gold" in any copy (inviting cases and eroding the solid gold
    position).
  15. Identical products at different prices on Etsy and the site.
