 2.4 UPS for jewelry (direct)
  • UPS lists jewelry and gold or other precious metals as restricted, shipped "on a contractual
    basis only", and "articles of high or unusual value" only under contract [S19: ups.com, read
    2026-10-22]. The page places these in its import and export lists; it does not state a separate
    domestic rule.
  • UPS Terms (effective April 17, 2026): no package with actual value over $50,000 accepted
    ($70,000 under Enhanced Maximum Declared Value); packages of $1,000 or more need a
    high-value shipment summary when tendered to a driver or Customer Center; raw gold or
    platinum over 50% by weight (bullion, scrap) is an article of unusual value [S20: assets.ups.com,
    effective 2026-04-17].
  • A $500 domestic jewelry declared value cap for standard UPS accounts is reported by a third
    party [S21: shipaid.com, ShipAid (secondary), 2026-06-06] but could not be confirmed in the UPS
    terms text retrieved. Treat as unconfirmed; ask UPS in writing before using UPS for rings.

 2.7 Signature confirmation: what is and is not a rule
  • Etsy: no current Etsy policy requires signature confirmation above a dollar amount. The Purchase
    Protection policy (effective July 9, 2026), the Purchase Protection help article and the case
    resolution article do not mention signature confirmation [S3: etsy.com, effective 2026-07-09];
    [S25: help.etsy.com, read 2026-10-22]; [S26: help.etsy.com, read 2026-10-22]. The older Seller
    Protection rule requiring signature on orders of $250+ is UNVERIFIED (pre-2025), and a 2022
    report says Etsy refunded a signed-for $580 order anyway [S27: valueaddedresource.net,
    2022-12-30], UNVERIFIED (pre-2025).
  • FedEx: declared value of $500+ under Declared Value Advantage means automatic Direct
    Signature Required [FedEx declared value page, above].
  • Shipsurance: if a signature service is required, the claim is excluded unless that service was
    used [Shipsurance terms, above].
  • Recommendation (house rule, not a platform rule): signature confirmation on every order
    over $250, Adult Signature Required on $1,000+. Reason: Etsy covers only $250 per case, so the
    seller carries the rest and needs the strongest delivery proof plus insurance. Indirect signatures
    let a neighbor or concierge sign; direct or adult signature is recommended for high-value
    shipments [ClickPost, 2026-10-22].

 3. If you ever ship internationally or import
 Domestic US orders involve no customs, duties or tariffs. These points apply only if the business
 changes its setup.
  • Importing finished pieces or components from Türkiye (B2B): HTS 7113.19.50 (gold
    jewelry, other; 7113.19.50.25 for rings) carries 5.5% general duty [S28: hts.usitc.gov, read
    2026-10-22], and Türkiye is in the 12.5% Section 301 forced-labor tier from July 24, 2026 [S29:
    taxnews.ey.com, EY, 2026-07-24]. Combined 18%, charged on the import (workshop invoice)
    value, not the Etsy retail price; dutiable value details are a customs broker question.
  • No de minimis: duty-free treatment for imports of $800 or less was suspended for all countries
    from 12:01 a.m. on August 29, 2025 [S30: content.govdelivery.com, CBP CSMS #66065494,
    2025-08-28]. Every import pays duty.
  • Country of origin stays honest: if finished rings are made in a Türkiye workshop and shipped
    from the US, that workshop is the production partner and the listings keep Türkiye as origin;
    production partners must be disclosed and ships-from must match reality [S10: etsy.com,
    2026-10-05]; [S13: help.etsy.com, read 2026-10-22].
  • Türkiye export side: micro-export (ETGB), KDV export exemption and Jewelry Exporters'
    Association approval apply only when goods are exported from Türkiye [S31:
    sanayigazetesi.com.tr, Sanayi Gazetesi, 2026-05-11]; [S32: goldium.com.tr, Goldium (secondary),
    2026-08-10]. Confirm with a Turkish accountant before the first shipment.
  • Selling to buyers outside the US: UPS Etsy labels are domestic only [UPS labels help page,
    above]; check Etsy's international shipments page for current rules [S33: help.etsy.com, read
    2026-10-22]. Declare the real value with a specific description, and never mark a sale as a gift;
    that is a false declaration.

  • Production partner disclosure: listings made by the US workshop are "designed by a seller," not

 5.2 Time zones
  • Istanbul is 7 to 8 hours ahead of New York, depending on US daylight saving time. Example at 7
    hours: 15:00 to 18:00 in Istanbul is 08:00 to 11:00 in New York. If the workshop is in Central,
    Mountain or Pacific time, add 1 to 3 more hours.
  • House rule: the daily handoff window is the overlap of the owner's afternoon and the US morning.
    The owner sends the day's board and any approved proofs before US opening; the workshop and
    fulfillment send status and photo sets before US close, which the owner reviews first thing the
    next Istanbul morning (still overnight in the US).
  • On-time shipping is judged by the end of the ship-by day in the shop's time zone [Shipink,
    2026-08-25]. For this shop that is the US time zone in Etsy settings, not Istanbul time. Mark
    shipped (or scan the Etsy label) before that cutoff.

 5.3 Who answers messages
  • Help with Order: reply within 48 hours, always [Etsy policy, 2026-07-09]. Primary: owner.
    Backup: fulfillment colleague, with a written handover whenever the owner is traveling or on
    Turkish public holidays (Bayram), and the owner covering US holidays when the US side is closed.
  • All first messages within 24 hours for Star Seller (95% over a rolling three months, including
    weekends and holidays); auto-replies count [S35: craftybase.com, Craftybase (secondary),

 5.4 Step by step
 Day 0, within 2 hours of the order (or next Istanbul morning if it arrives during the
 Istanbul night)
  1. Owner: fraud screen (see Section 9). Check billing name vs shipping name, address type, order
    value, buyer account age and history, any message asking to change the address.
  2. Owner: confirm the address is the one on the Etsy order. Etsy's policy requires shipping to the
    address provided on Etsy and warns that alternate addresses sent via Messages may be fraud;
    you may cancel, refund and ask the buyer to reorder [Etsy policy, 2026-07-09].
  3. Owner: send the Order Confirmation message (Template 1). Confirm ring size, karat, color and
    engraving text exactly as typed.
  4. Owner: log the order in the shared production board: order number, ship-by date (in the shop's
    US time zone), size, karat, metal color, engraving, proof needed (Y/N), value band for signature
    and insurance (Section 2.7), assigned production day.
 Day 0 to 1: Proofing (custom and engraved only)
  5. Owner: send a digital proof (Template 2) in Etsy Messages: engraving rendered in the chosen
    font at scale, ring width, stone layout. Ask for a written "Approved" in Etsy Messages.
  6. Owner: screenshot the approval with date and paste the approved text into the production board
    by copy-paste, never retyped. The workshop engraves only from the board text marked
    "Approved."
  7. The clock risk: Etsy counts from your stated processing time, and informal agreements in
    Messages to change ship dates do not count for Purchase Protection [Etsy policy, 2026-07-09]. If
    the buyer is slow to approve, you still own the ship-by date. Build proof time inside the 5 to 7
    days, or change processing only through Etsy's own tools, never by side agreement.
  8. If no approval in 24 hours, send one reminder. If no reply in 48 hours, proceed with the text
    exactly as ordered and say so in the reminder.
 Day 1 to 5: Production batches (workshop)
  9. Batch scheduling against about 10 rings per day: assign each order to a production day by
    ship-by date, earliest first. Keep 2 slots per day (20%) unbooked for remakes and resizes. When
    the 5-day forward board shows more than 40 booked rings, the owner lengthens processing time
    on listings before dates are missed (house rule).
  10. Casting, finishing, setting, engraving, stamping, polishing.
  11. Workshop posts end-of-day status on the board before US close.
 Day 5 to 6: QA, photo-before-ship, pack
  12. Workshop runs the QA Checklist (Section 6). Two-person sign-off for orders over $1,000 (house
    rule; the second person can be the fulfillment colleague).
  13. Workshop or fulfillment sends the photo and video set to the owner in the shared order folder:
    ring on a scale showing weight, karat stamp macro, engraving macro next to the approved proof,
    ring on a mandrel showing size, ring in the box, plain outer box sealed with label visible.
  14. Owner reviews the photo set and replies "OK to ship" on the board. House rule: review within
    the next Istanbul morning so the US side has it before its own morning; if the ship-by date is
    today and no reply has arrived by US midday, fulfillment ships on a passed QA checklist and the
    owner reviews afterwards. Never miss a ship-by date waiting for an approval.

 6. QA CHECKLIST
 Run by the workshop on every piece. Record result and initials in the order file; the photo items are
 what the owner reviews remotely.
 Metal and marks
  • [ ] Karat matches the order (14K or 18K as purchased).
  • [ ] Karat stamp present, legible, correctly placed (585 for 14K [Goldium, 2026-08-10]; 750 for
    18K).
  • [ ] Maker's mark present.
  • [ ] Metal color matches order (yellow, white, rose).
  • [ ] Weight recorded on calibrated scale; within your stated tolerance of the listing weight.
 Size and fit
  • [ ] Size measured on a calibrated mandrel; matches order (US size).
  • [ ] Comfort fit or flat interior as listed.
  • [ ] Width and thickness measured with calipers; match listing.
 Engraving and personalization
  • [ ] Engraving text matches the approved proof character by character (copy-paste from the board
    entry marked "Approved," which was copied from Etsy Messages; never retype).
  • [ ] Capitals, punctuation, dates, symbols, and font as approved.
  • [ ] Engraving depth even and readable.
 Stones (if any)
  • [ ] Stone type, size, count match listing.

 8. Etsy cases, Purchase Protection and chargebacks
 8.1 Purchase Protection for Sellers (version effective July 9, 2026)
 Eligibility requirements, all must be met [S3: etsy.com, Etsy policy, , effective 2026-07-09, updated
 2026-06-09]:
  • A. Meet minimum Customer Service Standards and reply to Help with Order within 48 hours.
  • B. Accurate "ships from" location and valid tracking (or Etsy label). For this shop, ships-from must
    be the US partner or fulfillment address.
  • C. Etsy Payments.
  • D. Ship to the Etsy order address; be alert to alternate addresses via Messages.
  • E. Package carefully.
  • F. Processing times set, ship within them, Estimated Delivery Date shown; informal date changes
    in Messages do not count.
  • H. All Shop Policies completed, especially returns, exchanges and custom orders.
  • I. Good standing.
  • J. DDP for US-bound international orders. Not relevant to this shop's domestic shipments [S3:
    etsy.com, effective 2026-07-09].
 Coverage:




Etsy Fine Jewelry Master Course | Module 6: Shipping, Fulfillment, Customer Service and Risk          Page 116
  • Etsy may refund buyers up to $250 USD including shipping and Etsy-collected taxes; the seller is
    responsible above $250 [same source].
  • Since May 7, 2026, eligible orders above $250 get up to $250 coverage (example: Etsy covers
    $250 of a $400 refund) [Etsy Seller Handbook, 2026-04-07]. Before that date, only orders of $250
    or less were covered [same source]. One help-center version still reads as if orders over $250 are
    excluded [S38: help.etsy.com, read 2026-10-22], while the en-us version says coverage applies
    "even if the order total exceeds $250" [S25: help.etsy.com, read 2026-10-22]. The policy and
    Handbook govern.
  • Damaged items: only the first damage case per calendar year [Etsy policy, 2026-07-09].
  • Not-as-described orders are excluded [same source].
  • Etsy "strongly recommends purchasing insurance," and if you have carrier or third-party
    coverage, claim there first [same source].
  • Signature confirmation is not required by the current policy at any dollar value (Section 2.7).
  • High-value orders are always reviewed by a person; Etsy is nearly doubling human reviewers
    [Etsy Seller Handbook, 2026-04-07].
  • Cases do not hurt shop ranking or Star Seller eligibility when the order qualifies [S1: etsy.com,

 Customer Service Standards (secondary summary): message response rate, on-time shipping and
 tracking, average review rating, case rate; under 300 orders no more than 3 cases refunded from
 your account, at 300 or more orders case rate under 1% [S40: my-shop.co, mySHOP, 2026-09-05].
 Basic messaging standard cited as 80% of first messages within 48 hours [Listybox, 2026-01-12].
 Implication for a gold ring shop: on a $1,200 ring that goes missing, Etsy covers at most $250.
 The remaining $950 is yours unless Shipsurance, carrier coverage or specialty insurance pays.
 Insurance is not optional in this category.

 8.2 Case timelines
  • Buyer must use Help with Order first; a case can open if the seller does not respond within 48
    hours or the issue is not resolved in 48 hours [Etsy Cases Policy, 2026-06-09].
  • Sellers must respond within 2 calendar days when Etsy requests information [same source].
  • Buyers can open a case within 30 days after the estimated delivery date (changed from 100
    days) [Etsy, 2026-05-07]; [S41: novadata.io, Nova, 2026-04-10]. For non-delivery the buyer waits
    7+ days after the maximum estimated delivery date [S26: help.etsy.com, read 2026-10-22].
  • Late = arriving 7 or more days after the estimated delivery date; tracking scans checked with at
    least a two-day buffer [Etsy Seller Handbook, 2026-04-07].
  • Outcomes for never-received or 7+ days late are generally not reviewable; damaged or
    not-as-described outcomes can be disputed, with a decision in 1 to 3 business days [mySHOP,
    2026-09-05].
  • Only one dispute route: a chargeback blocks or closes an Etsy case [Etsy Cases Policy,
    2026-06-09].
  • File any Shipsurance claim before cancelling the order [Etsy insurance help page, read
    2026-10-22].

 8.3 Item not received vs not as described



Etsy Fine Jewelry Master Course | Module 6: Shipping, Fulfillment, Customer Service and Risk     Page 117
  • Not received or very late: full refund to the buyer [Etsy Cases Policy, 2026-06-09]. Your defense is
    tracking showing delivery, ideally with signature, at the Etsy order address, within the estimated
    window.
  • Not as described: significantly different color, size, model, material, undisclosed damage, false
    authenticity claims, wrong quantity, shipping from a different location than advertised [same
    source]. For gold, "material" means karat. A buyer who tests a ring and finds it is not the stated
    karat has a strong case. Your defense is the karat stamp photo, assay or certificate, weight
    photo, and the listing text. "Shipping from a different location than advertised" is why the
    ships-from setting must match the US address actually used.
  • Custom items: the Cases Policy does not address custom physical items specifically [same
    source]. Your proof approval in Messages is the key evidence when a buyer later disputes
    engraving.

 8.4 Chargebacks
  • A chargeback is a dispute with the buyer's bank or card company; Etsy supplies information but
    does not decide; Etsy cannot refund once a chargeback is filed; types include fraud, non-delivery,
    not as described, and return [Etsy Help, Chargebacks, 2026].
  • Etsy typically has 30 to 45 days to respond and may contact the seller for information on Etsy
    Payments transactions [same source].
  • Answer Etsy's chargeback information request the same day with the full evidence pack (Section
    8.5). The owner handles this; the shared order folder means the evidence is reachable from
    Türkiye.

 8.5 Evidence to keep for every order
  • Order receipt and the address as shown on Etsy.
  • All Etsy Messages (never move conversations off Etsy).
  • Proof approval screenshot with date.
  • QA record and photo set: weight on scale, karat stamp, engraving, size on mandrel, packed box,
    sealed box with label.
  • Owner "OK to ship" record.
  • Etsy label, Shipsurance or other insurance confirmation, signature service purchased.
  • Tracking history including delivery scan and signature image (download it; carriers purge).
  • For returns and exchanges: inbound tracking and an unboxing video on arrival at the US address.
 ClickPost recommends photographing item, box and label before sealing and keeping the drop-off
 receipt for claims [ClickPost, 2026-10-22].

 8.6 Reserves
  • Primary source: Etsy can apply a fixed reserve (a percentage of sales held on a rolling basis) or a
    variable reserve (delay up to 180 days or deduct for up to 90 days); holds can last until the
    shorter of 180 days after estimated delivery or the end of an investigation [S42: etsy.com, last
    updated 2026-07-31]. Etsy may apply a reserve to new sellers with no track record [S43:
    help.etsy.com, read 2026-10-22], and may place reserves after unusual activity or elevated risk
    [S10: etsy.com, 2026-10-05].
  • Secondary: triggers include a rapid increase in order volume, shipments lacking tracking,
    shipping delays, more refund requests, and a first sale; funds release when tracking is added and
    Etsy verifies transit, or when the default holding period passes; Star Seller status may help [S44:

 9. Fraud patterns in high-ticket jewelry
 Sourced points:
  • Alternate addresses sent through Messages are Etsy's named fraud warning; cancel, refund and
    ask for a reorder [Etsy policy, 2026-07-09].
  • Fraud is a named chargeback type (cardholder says they did not authorize) [Etsy Help,
    Chargebacks, 2026].
  • Indirect signatures let a neighbor or concierge sign; direct or adult signature is recommended for
    high-value shipments [ClickPost, 2026-10-22].
  • Shipsurance claims for lost parcels need a carrier tracer and a recipient-signed statement
    [Shipsurance terms, read 2026-10-22]; a buyer unwilling to sign one is a warning sign.
 Shop rules (operational practice, not sourced statistics):
  • Never ship to an address that differs from the Etsy order.
  • Red flags that justify a hold and a polite verification message: new account with first order above
    $1,000; billing and shipping in different states with a freight forwarder or reshipper address;
    requests to rush to a different address; requests to change address after dispatch (refuse; let the
    carrier's own redirect process and the buyer handle it, and note that redirects can void your
    evidence); requests to communicate or pay off Etsy; multiple high-value orders in a short window
    with different names to one address.
  • If in doubt, cancel and refund before production. A cancelled sale costs a fee; a fraudulent $1,500
    ring costs the ring.
  • Use signature confirmation on high-value orders per the Section 2.7 recommendation, and
    always when your insurer requires it.
  • Never lower the insured value or mark a parcel as a gift at a buyer's request.
  • The workshop and fulfillment never act on buyer instructions that did not come through the
    owner and Etsy Messages.


 10. CASE PREVENTION
  1. Use an Etsy label with valid tracking on every order. It marks the order shipped and
    satisfies the Purchase Protection tracking requirement automatically [S4: etsy.com, 2026-10-13].
  2. Answer Help with Order within 48 hours, every time, and all first messages within 24
    hours for Star Seller (95% over a rolling three months, including weekends and holidays)
    [Craftybase, 2026-04]. Auto-replies count [same source]; set one for nights in both time zones
    and for Turkish and US public holidays, and name a backup responder (Section 5.3).
  3. Dispatch inside processing time with valid tracking. Star Seller requires 95%+ on-time
    shipping with tracking, 4.8+ average rating and at least 5 orders in the window; arrival date does
    not matter, dispatch date does [Craftybase, 2026-04]. Ship-by is judged in the shop's US time
    zone [Shipink, 2026-08-25].
  4. Set an honest Estimated Delivery Date. The 30-day case window starts from it [Nova,
    2026-04-10].




Etsy Fine Jewelry Master Course | Module 6: Shipping, Fulfillment, Customer Service and Risk     Page 119
  5. Insure every order to full value: Shipsurance on the Etsy label up to $5,000, Registered Mail
    or specialty insurance above that (ClickPost names Parcel Pro and Jewelers Mutual [ClickPost,
    2026-10-22]). Etsy pays at most $250.
  6. Ship in plain outer packaging, or the Shipsurance claim can be excluded [Shipsurance terms,
    read 2026-10-22].
  7. Complete every Shop Policy field, especially returns, exchanges and custom orders [Etsy
    policy, 2026-07-09].
  8. Write listings that match the product exactly: karat, weight range, width, stone specs,
    made-to-order timing, size exchange terms, accurate ships-from and production partner
    disclosure.
  9. Proof every engraving in writing in Etsy Messages.
  10. Proactive messages at every stage (Templates 1 to 6). Most "where is my order" cases
    start with silence.
  11. Fix money issues in Messages fast (small defect, missing package) before a case or
    chargeback, while respecting the insurer's rule to file before cancelling.
  12. Offer a sizer before production for buyers unsure of size; sizing is the top reason for returns
    in rings.


 11. Warranty, care, resizing, lifetime service as trust levers
 No 2025-2026 source with Etsy-specific data on warranty programs was gathered. The following is
 strategy, clearly labeled as such:
  • One free size exchange on custom and engraved rings is already your policy. Put it in the
    listing's first lines, in Template 1 and in Template 6. It answers the biggest objection to buying a
    ring online.
  • Lifetime cleaning and polish (buyer pays tracked shipping to the US workshop, you pay return
    shipping) creates a reason to contact you again and an opportunity to cross-sell.
  • Manufacturing warranty (e.g., 12 months on casting defects and stone settings) signals
    confidence. Etsy notes sellers remain responsible for warranty or return obligations under local
    law [Etsy policy, 2026-07-09], so write it precisely, and agree with the production partner in
    writing who pays for warranty remakes.
  • Care card in every box: remove before chlorine, store separately, polish cloth.
  • Anniversary message at 11 months: offer a complimentary polish, a matching band, or
    engraving on a new piece.
  • Returns logistics: returns and exchanges go to the US partner or fulfillment address, and Etsy
    USPS return labels are available within the 50 states [S4: etsy.com, 2026-10-13]. Fulfillment films
    the unboxing and sends it to the owner the same day.


 12. COMMON MISTAKES
  1. Using branded boxes or tape on the outside. Shipsurance excludes packaging that alludes
    to the contents [Shipsurance terms, read 2026-10-22].
  2. Using padded envelopes for jewelry over $500 [FedEx, read 2026-10-22].
  3. Using UPS for gold rings without a written UPS answer. UPS lists jewelry and precious
    metals as restricted, contract only [UPS prohibited items page, read 2026-10-22].




Etsy Fine Jewelry Master Course | Module 6: Shipping, Fulfillment, Customer Service and Risk       Page 120
  4. Assuming FedEx will insure the ring. FedEx caps jewelry declared value at $1,000 per
    package and does not provide insurance [FedEx, read 2026-10-22].
  5. Assuming Etsy covers a $1,500 ring. Etsy covers up to $250 [Etsy, 2026-05-07].
  6. Relying on the $100 included coverage. Add Shipsurance to full value [Etsy insurance help
    page, read 2026-10-22].
  7. Cancelling or refunding before filing the Shipsurance claim [same source].
  8. Skipping signature on a required-signature shipment, which voids the Shipsurance claim
    [Shipsurance terms, read 2026-10-22].
  9. Wrong carrier selected in Etsy's tracking dropdown when a label is bought outside Etsy, so
    the buyer sees no tracking [Shipink, 2026-08-25].
  10. Marking shipped by Istanbul time. On-time is judged by end of the ship-by day in the
    shop's time zone [Shipink, 2026-08-25].
  11. Agreeing to a new ship date in Messages and thinking it protects you. It does not [Etsy
    policy, 2026-07-09].
  12. Shipping to a new address the buyer typed in Messages [Etsy policy, 2026-07-09].
  13. Missing the 48-hour Help with Order reply on a weekend, a Bayram or a US holiday. Set
    the auto-reply and name a backup.
  14. Holding a shipment for the owner's photo approval past the ship-by date. Use the
    fallback rule in Section 5.4.
  15. Inaccurate ships-from or missing production partner disclosure [S10: etsy.com,
    2026-10-05]; "shipping from a different location than advertised" is also a not-as-described
    ground [Etsy Cases Policy, 2026-06-09].
  16. Sharing the owner's Etsy password with the workshop or colleague instead of using shop
    member roles (recommendation, Section 5.1).
  17. Letting unused labels expire unrefunded. USPS 10 days, UPS 14 days, FedEx 10 days [S4:
    etsy.com, 2026-10-13].
