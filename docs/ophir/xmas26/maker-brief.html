Ophir Gold USA · Christmas 2026

Twenty Christmas rings:
design brief and request for quotation

Prepared 2026-10-16 · 20 designs · each in 10K, 14K and 18K · yellow, white and rose · US 4–12 in half sizes

What we need from you, per design

  1. Confirm the design can be cast and finished solid, as drawn and described, at the stated dimensions. Tell us where you would change a dimension and why.
  2. The finished weight in 14K at US 7. Our weights are geometric estimates and will be wrong by some margin; yours replace ours.
  3. Your price for one finished piece in 14K yellow at US 7, labour included, and your rule for 10K, 18K and for other sizes.
  4. Where a design carries a stone, the stone cost separately (stones named per page), and whether you supply or we do.
  5. Lead time for a first sample of each design, and for a run of ten.

How to read the pages

Each page is one design: the render, the construction in plain words, the story the listing tells the buyer, and the specification. The renders are design visualisations, not photographs of samples; where the picture and the text differ, the text governs. One render (R06) is marked as not yet matching its description.

All twenty are solid gold with no hollow sections, no plating on the white gold, and no engraving or personalisation. The two signets with cut motifs (R04, R05) carry the motif only; no lettering.
