## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (EN)

EON relaunched in mid-July 2026 and proved demand in three weeks: 10 orders, $3,370.78 realized revenue, 1.2-1.3% conversion (2-3x the Jade Gold benchmark of 0.5-0.7%), same-day shipping, 5.0 rating. The engine works. The economics behind it are almost exactly break-even, and three forces are pulling it negative.

**The real number.** Reconciling Amuletta's own ledger (supplier invoices + Etsy fees + ads + labels + other costs) against realized revenue, the shop's all-time net is **+$64.12** — a 1.9% margin, not the $1,757 "net profit" Etsy's dashboard shows for August (that figure excludes cost of goods entirely). COGS runs 68% of revenue; Etsy platform costs another 23%.

**Three forces.**
1. **Returns.** 5 of 10 orders are in return/refund (4 open requests worth $1,657.56 + 1 completed refund). Of *delivered* orders that is 56%, roughly four times the fine-jewelry norm. If the open requests complete, the shop goes from +$64 to roughly **-$1,590**. None of the four return labels has been used yet — these are still recoverable as exchanges.
2. **Discount architecture.** Base prices are set at engine price ÷ 0.75, so the permanent 25% sale lands exactly on the engineered price and *does* preserve the ~24-25% target margin. That is the good news and the problem: it means the "sale" is structural, which contradicts the About page's "no permanent fake sales" promise, and it leaves no headroom — the 30% buyer-offer ceiling (open on 18 listings) and July's 30%/50% coupons are provably loss-making (-$153 on one order). Gold spot is now $4,375.77/oz, 8.5% above the engine's $4,033 base, and the "priced on the day you order" rule is not being applied.
3. **Ads without attribution.** $272.62 spent all-time, zero attributed orders, budget aimed at the unproven Satin/18K series (9 listings, 6 lifetime views) instead of the 8 listings that actually sell — a direct violation of the rule set in the Jade Gold audit.

**SEO.** Organic search is 10.7% of all-time visits (16.5% in August, improving) against a 30-50% healthy target. The planned keyword system never reached production: across 40 live titles, "Personalized" appears 0 times, "Heirloom" 0 times, "mens" 0 times — while men's and personalized-engraving queries are the proven demand in this category.

**Priority in the first 14 days:** convert the 4 open returns into exchanges, end the blanket sale on 28 August without extending it, repoint ads to the proven 8 at a $25 stop-loss, publish nothing new until the 18K wave in September, and fix the double-entry COGS bug in Amuletta so the panel stops lying in both directions.

## 4. Fiyat ve Marj Analizi

**Mimarinin gerçeği.** Canlı taban fiyat = motor fiyatı ÷ 0.75. Kanıt: 14K milgrain $420 × 0.75 = $315 = motorun 2mm fiyatı birebir. Dolayısıyla:

| İndirim | Efektif fiyat | Sonuç |
|---|---|---|
| %25 (mevcut) | motor fiyatı | Hedef marj %24-25 **korunuyor** (ampirik Ağustos ort. ~%30) |
| %30 (buyer offer tavanı) | motor × 0.93 | İnce: ampirik 2mm %9.0, 5mm %17.0 |
| %50 (Temmuz kuponu) | motor × 0.67 ≈ landed × 1.03 | **Garantili zarar** (ampirik −$153) |
| Başabaş sınırı | ~%27.9 | Bunun ötesi zarar |

Yani "indirim marjı yiyor" değil, **"indirim mimarisi marjı gizliyor"**: sürekli %25, fiilen liste fiyatıdır. Bedeli marj değil, marka vaadi ve manevra alanı: gerçek bir kampanya yapacak yer kalmıyor ve %30 buyer offer açıkken (18 listing) ince marj katmanına kapı açık.

**Spot riski.** Motor $4,033/oz (16 Tem). Bugün $4,375.77/oz (aylık +%7.9, YTD +%31.2; 12 Ağu $4,410). Gram doğrulaması: fatura 10K medyan ~$55/g (küçük gramajda $60-66'ya çıkıyor), 14K $77.00/g — teorik hesapla uyumlu. Güncel spotta beklenen yeni seviye 10K ~$59-63/g, 14K ~$83-88/g. Karma marj ~%29 → ~%26. Zayıf hücrelerde (2mm + %30 offer) başabaş spot ~$4,700 — bugünkü fiyata tehlikeli yakın.

**İşçilik gerçeği.** Motor $30 varsaydı; faturalar $37.98-$116.54, ortalama **$63.28**. Kademeli tablo önerisi: flat/dome $45-55, milgrain $60-65, dokulu (hammered) ~$100, custom/matte ~$120, küçük gramajda minimum işçilik tabanı.

**Öneriler (marj korumalı):**
1. 28 Ağustos'ta sale'i **uzatma**. 1 Eylül - 23 Kasım tam fiyat: hem marka vaadini onarır hem Cyber indirimini gerçek kılar.
2. Taban fiyatları motor fiyatına çek (şişirilmiş taban + sürekli indirim yerine dürüst tek fiyat) — bu, About'taki iddiayla tek tutarlı yol.
3. Buyer offers tavanını %30 → %15; 2mm giriş hücrelerinde kapalı.
4. Kupon tavanı %15, sadece marjı kanıtlı satırlarda (≥3mm, 14K+, milgrain/hammered). %50 kalıcı yasak.
5. Custom SOP: teklif motordan geçmeden verilmez; landed × 1.30 altına teklif yok. Oly Jewelry $220 kaydının hangi siparişe ait olduğu netleşmeli.
6. Motoru güncelle: işçilik kademeli tablo + spot $4,375 tabanı; spot ±%5 hareketinde reprice (Phase 3'teki "gold repricing alert" canlıya alınmalı).
