## EN

**Diagnosis.** In the last 30 days the shop produced 1,451 visits, 12 orders and $7,917 at a 0.8% conversion rate. Only 15% of visits (215) came from Etsy search; the rest came from Etsy app pages, Etsy's own marketing, direct traffic and 113 visits from Etsy Ads. Over 3 months, half of 8,514 visits went to 10 listings; 62 listings had fewer than 5 visits and 23 had none. Reach is low because most of the catalog is invisible to search.

**Four concrete causes.** First, 73 of 185 listings (39%) carry auto-generated word-salad tags such as "engraved solid women", "thin wide edge and", "men personalized set", "minimalist christma". They match no buyer query; Etsy's tag matching is phrase based. Second, 44 listings have no attributes at all (Gold purity, Band color, Recipient, Occasion empty); attributes feed Etsy's filters, so those listings never appear in filtered searches. Third, 67 listings have no video, and the data is brutal: the 118 listings with video took 7,764 visits and 38 orders, the 67 without took 750 visits and 2 orders. Fourth, titles are metal-first ("14K Yellow Gold Beveled Wedding Band") while 55% of real search terms contain "mens" and 4% contain "women". The buyer is shopping for a man's ring, types 10k twice as often as 14k and six times as often as 18k, and prefers white gold. The shop meanwhile is overweight rose gold: 49 listings, 684 visits, 2 orders in 3 months.

**The September series was born dead.** The 24 "6mm or 7mm, 10K 14K 18K" listings launched in September 2026 (Celtic, Victorian, Honeycomb, Diagonal, Split Finish, Stone Finish, Watch Link) took 15 visits and zero orders in 3 months. All have empty attributes, 21 have no video, and they are priced $960 to $1,580 while orders come from under $500 (28 listings, 21 orders) and $500 to $900 (44 listings, 14 orders). The 86 listings priced $900 to $1,300 produced 3 orders in total.

**Keyword decisions.** Measurable Etsy volume sits in head terms: "mens wedding band" 4,021 searches a month (eRank), "gold ring" 7,499, "14k gold ring" 2,122, "10k gold ring" 963, "engraved ring" 1,945, "anniversary gift for him" 3,477, "solid gold ring" 1,713, "rose gold ring" 1,636. Attribute long-tails ("milgrain band", "beveled wedding band", "6mm gold band") measure under 20 on Etsy; they stay in tags but do not carry titles. The shop's strongest opportunity is the fidget ring: 4,868 searches, competition 5,477, KD 3; the existing fidget listing is already the second most visited product and one 14K listing is serving that whole demand. "anxiety ring" adds 1,433 searches at KD 26. Second opportunity: "gold signet ring" (1,511, KD 53) and "chunky gold ring" (1,107, KD 48); the shop has neither, which is a collection decision.

**What was built.** For all 185 listings a new title, 13 new tags, the attributes to fill, the section fix (53 moves) and the video gap sit in `data/optimization-matrix.csv` in priority order. Rules: wedding band titles start with "Mens", karat and metal inside the first 40 characters, of the 13 tags 3 are head terms, 4 style, 2 width, 2 gift intent. `CLAUDE.md` tells Claude Code how to apply the matrix through the Etsy Open API in batches of 20 with a resumable state file: dry run, write, verify.

**Measurement.** Same Etsy Stats screen at 30 and 60 days: Etsy Search visits from 215 to 450, zero-visit listings from 23 to under 15, conversion from 0.8% to 1.2%.
