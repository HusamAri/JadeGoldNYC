## Locked product geometry

- One solid-gold, stone-free, closed wedding band.
- Broad satin-finished center plane with a quiet linear grain.
- One continuous, extremely fine milgrain line on each side of the satin center.
- One crisp mirror-polished bevel outside each milgrain line.
- Bevels are planar and faceted, not domed, rolled or rope-shaped.
- Polished comfort-fit interior with a clean, uninterrupted inner wall.
- Symmetrical edge order on both sides: polished bevel, fine milgrain, satin center, fine milgrain, polished bevel.
- No groove through the center, no Greek key, no hammered texture, no diagonal ribs, no stones and no decorative engraving.
- The satin center remains the visually dominant surface. Milgrain stays narrow and regular. Polished bevels remain clearly visible without overtaking the center.

## Proportion lock

For the canonical 5 mm photography sample:

- Satin center: approximately 66 to 70 percent of total width.
- Both milgrain lines combined: approximately 4 to 6 percent of total width.
- Both polished bevels combined: approximately 24 to 30 percent of total width.

The same relative construction is preserved from 4 mm through 12 mm. The design is never simplified into a plain beveled band at narrow widths.

## Alloy and karat rendering lock

Geometry, finish placement and proportions never change between listings. Only alloy appearance changes:

- 10K Yellow Gold: pale straw yellow.
- 14K Yellow Gold: balanced natural honey yellow.
- 18K Yellow Gold: deeper and more saturated yellow.
- 10K White Gold: cool silver white, never chrome.
- 14K White Gold: neutral soft white, never chrome.
- 18K White Gold: subtly warm white, never yellow.
- 10K Rose Gold: muted pale blush, never red.
- 14K Rose Gold: balanced blush copper, never red.
- 18K Rose Gold: richer warm rose, never red.

## Image acceptance gate

Every product photograph must pass all checks:

1. One ring only and a complete circular band.
2. Satin center, twin milgrain lines and twin polished bevels are all visible where the camera angle permits.
3. Milgrain is continuous, evenly spaced and confined to two fine edge lines.
4. Bevels are planar, crisp and mirror polished.
5. Center remains satin and does not become mirror polished, hammered or brushed across the width.
6. Interior is smooth, polished and comfort fit.
7. Alloy tone matches the listing karat and metal.
8. Contact and cast shadows are physically plausible.
9. No invented hallmark, text, logo, watermark, packaging or extra jewelry.
10. Product geometry agrees with the supplied references before style or atmosphere is considered.
11. Lighting is restrained and physically coherent, with directional key light, soft ambient fill, accurate contact shadows and reflections that belong to the scene.
12. Satin gold, polished bevels, stone, glass and skin retain natural micro-detail. Plastic smoothing, artificial HDR contrast, oversaturation, excessive sharpening, glowing edges and synthetic vibrancy are rejected.
13. Metal reflections follow the photographed environment without painted gradients, clipped highlights or impossible shadow directions.
14. The set reads as inventive professional home-studio photography made with real window daylight and restrained glass-shaped light beams, never as a generic luxury-jewelry template.
15. The Etsy hero remains clean and immediately legible at thumbnail size. Supporting frames each serve a distinct function: surface detail, profile, construction, scale on hand and editorial atmosphere.
16. Avoid marble cliches, artificial cinematic color grading, fake sparkle, theatrical haze and decorative props that compete with the ring.

Any image that fails one check is rejected and regenerated with a single targeted correction.
