# EON SEO — Size & Width Edge

Full size and width range is a real SEO and conversion edge on Etsy,
especially large sizes. Most solid-gold shops stop early on both.

## What matters

- **Large sizes (US 11–13)** and **wider bands (6–8 mm)** are thin competition,
  high intent. People who need them search specifically and buy.
- **“Made in your size and width”** is a trust claim Google and shoppers both
  reward, if it is true per band.

## What not to do

- Do **not** build dozens of thin pages like `/size-13-meridian` or every
  size × width combo. That looks like doorway spam and usually fails.
- Do **not** claim “all widths on every band” if a collection is slim-only or
  fixed-width. Overclaiming hurts SEO trust and returns.

## Brand collection ↔ catalog profile (SEO priority)

| Collection (photo / brand) | Catalog profile | SKU tip | SEO push |
|----------------------------|-----------------|---------|----------|
| **Meridian** | Dome | `*01` | Hardest: US 4–13 + full width range (true on current matrix) |
| **Keystone** | Beveled | `*03` | Wide-band intent (6–8 mm+ language) |
| Obelisk | Flat | `*02` | Neutral for now |
| Testament | Milgrain | `*04` | Neutral for now |
| **Cornice** | Knife Edge (or future slim line) | `*05` | Keep **honest**. Do not claim slim-only 2–3 mm until inventory matches. Current Knife still offers 2–12 mm. |

Etsy titles stay search-led (`solid 14k yellow gold wedding band…`), not
collection-name-first. Meridian / Keystone / Cornice are brand language for
site, photo, and internal priority — not doorway URL slugs.

## Best SEO shape

1. **Etsy first** — titles, tags, attributes, and first lines of description
   that state US 4–13 (matrix goes to 16) and available widths per listing.
2. **On-site, in structure not slogans** — product metas + Measure + ring-size
   guide: “Cut to US 4–13. Widths from 2 mm to 8 mm where the profile allows.”
3. **One or two intent guides, not fifty pages**
   - Large ring sizes / men’s larger sizes → Meridian (Dome) listings
   - Wide solid gold wedding bands → Keystone (Beveled) + Meridian
4. **FAQ / HowTo (AEO)** — “Do you make size 13?” “Can I order an 8 mm band?”
   Short factual answers (added under `SIZE QUESTIONS` on pilot listings).
5. **Schema** — keep product URLs as now; size/width as details or FAQ, not
   fake variants for every SKU unless each is a real separate offer.
