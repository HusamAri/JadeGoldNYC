## Geometry authority

The two WhatsApp photographs supplied on 2026-08-15 are the only geometry authority.

The ring must keep all of these features:

1. One continuous Greek key center band.
2. Crisp right-angle turns with a coherent repeat around the ring.
3. Two fine milgrain borders directly framing the center band.
4. One braided rope rail on each outer side.
5. Polished edge rails and a polished comfort-fit interior.
6. Fine microtexture in recessed center fields.

No gemstones, lettering, symbols, engraving or extra jewelry may be added.

## Atmosphere references

The three square reference photographs define photographic language only. Their ring geometries, colors and exact props must not be copied.

- Reference 3: controlled warm light, restrained composition and clear material separation.
- Reference 4: translucent glass, physical refraction and natural directional shadows.
- Reference 5: quiet architectural framing, bright conversion-oriented exposure and fine surface detail.

## Aegean Glass art direction

Aegean Glass translates the Greek root through proportion, right-angle rhythm, limestone and refracted daylight. It avoids literal temple props and souvenir styling.

- 10K scenes: glacial smoke glass, cool chalk limestone and pale daylight.
- 14K scenes: cobalt glass, pale limestone and clean Mediterranean sunlight.
- 18K scenes: smoked amethyst glass, deep limestone warmth and amber late-afternoon light.

Glass geometry must be new and abstract. It may suggest a single meander turn, but it must not copy the turquoise arch in reference 4.

## Metal tone matrix

| Metal | 10K | 14K | 18K |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Yellow Gold | Pale straw gold | Balanced honey gold | Deep rich gold |
| White Gold | Cool silver white | Neutral soft white | Warm champagne white |
| Rose Gold | Muted blush | Balanced blush copper | Rich rose gold |

White gold must never look like chrome. Rose gold must never look red. Yellow gold must never look orange or brassy.

## Quality gates

Every generated photograph must pass these checks:

- Geometry: all six locked product features are visible where the camera angle permits.
- Consistency: one ring only, one coherent family and one exact metal and karat tone.
- Realism: physically plausible gold, glass, refraction, contact and scale.
- Natural shadow: the ring is connected to its surface by a believable contact shadow.
- Greek roots: the scene communicates right-angle rhythm, limestone and Aegean light without costume or cliché.
- Etsy readiness: square crop, safe thumbnail composition, no text on photographs and at least 2000 by 2000 pixels in final delivery.
