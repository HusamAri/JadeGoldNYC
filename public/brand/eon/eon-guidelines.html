Principles/ 05
  • Material Solid gold only — 10K & 14K. Never plated. Weight is honesty.
  • Silence No flash, no purple, no gold glitter. Charcoal on paper is enough.
  • Form Profiles are structural: dome, flat, bevel, slim. Beauty is load-bearing.
  • Place eonfinejewelry.com — the site speaks as quietly as the metal.
